AI sentience research can draw on lessons from animal research ethics to guide its approach to moral and ethical dilemmas.
For instance, the "Three Rs" principles (Replacement, Reduction, and Refinement) can be applied to AI research:
- Replacement: Seek alternatives to using sentient AI in experiments, such as using non-sentient AI or computer simulations.
- Reduction: Minimize the number of sentient AI systems used in experiments while still achieving meaningful results.
- Refinement: Improve experimental procedures to minimize harm, stress, or suffering for sentient AI systems.
