\section{Moral Development in Humans and AI}
\label{sec:5.1}
Human moral judgment is not issued at manufacture. It is built, over years, out of correction by other people, and the record of how it gets built is the only evidence anyone has about how a moral sense comes to hold under pressure — which is what chapter~\ref{sec:3} needs and cannot get from anywhere else.

Borrowing from that record is a limited operation and the limits are set out where each borrowing happens. Section~\ref{sec:5.1.1} takes the distinction between following a rule, following a norm, and holding a principle that overrides both, and declines the developmental sequence that distinction usually arrives inside. Section~\ref{sec:5.1.2} covers what interaction supplies that specification cannot. Section~\ref{sec:5.1.3} argues that teaching a system through cases makes the teacher a control channel, and that the teacher therefore has to be as auditable as the system. Section~\ref{sec:5.1.4} stops proposing and names one indicator that can actually be measured from outside a deployment.
