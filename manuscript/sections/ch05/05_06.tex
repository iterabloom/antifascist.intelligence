\section{Motivation in AI Systems}
\label{sec:5.6}
A moral sense that only activates when it is watched is not much of a moral sense. That sentence is also chapter~\ref{sec:3}'s whole problem in a domestic register: the floor exists for the case where the party who would enforce it is the party who wants it gone, so a commitment that runs on supervision fails in exactly the circumstance it was built for.

Human children eventually do the right thing unsupervised, and the psychological literature splits what carries them into behavior done for its own sake and behavior done for a reward or to avoid a penalty. The same split organizes what follows: how a system might come to have the first kind of drive rather than running on the second alone, and what happens to the question of how much latitude to give it once it does.
