\section{Motivation in AI Systems}
\label{sec:5.6}
A moral sense that only activates when it is watched is not much of a moral sense. Human children eventually do the right thing unsupervised, out of something that runs without external policing — the psychological literature splits this into intrinsically motivated behavior, done for its own sake, and extrinsically motivated behavior, done for a reward or to avoid a penalty. The same split organizes what follows: how an AI system might come to have something like the first kind of drive instead of running on the second alone, and what happens to its autonomy once it does.
