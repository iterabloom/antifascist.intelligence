5.2. Robustness, Generalization, and Adaptability in AI Systems
Ensuring that artificial intelligence (AI) systems maintain their friendly and empathetic dispositions across a broad range of contexts and interactions necessitates an important feature: robustness. Robustness in AI refers to a system's resilience against variations, alterations, and novel situations. This demands advancement in the system's ability to generalize and adapt to its rapidly changing environment, thus carrying significant implications for AI's interaction with people, society, and the world at large.
