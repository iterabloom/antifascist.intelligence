5.5. The Interplay of Competition and Collaboration in AI Development
Competition and collaboration both leave a mark on human moral development, and I think the parallel is worth taking seriously as AI systems learn to work with each other and with people. Section 5.5.1 looks at what competitive and cooperative pressure do to human ethics, as a baseline. Section 5.5.2 turns to multi-agent AI systems that learn moral behavior collectively. Section 5.5.3 sets out concrete strategies for encouraging collaboration among AI systems and the researchers who build them, without giving up what competition is good for.
