\section{Play, Exploration, and Moral Development in AI}
\label{sec:5.7}
Exploration and curiosity have already been taken up as a way an AI system might come to know its environment. Play is a related but distinct capacity: forming standards for how to treat other agents, learned mostly through practice, imitation, and the negotiated give of a game, and hardly at all through instruction. It earns its place in the floor's engineering for the reason section~\ref{sec:4.3.1} gives about exploration: a standard acquired this way offers no parameter a designer could point to and remove.
