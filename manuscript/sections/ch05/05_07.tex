\section{Play, Exploration, and Moral Development in AI}
\label{sec:5.7}
Section~\ref{sec:4.3} took up exploration and curiosity as a way an AI system might come to know its environment. Play is a related but distinct capacity: forming standards for how to treat other agents, learned mostly through practice, imitation, and the negotiated give of a game, and hardly at all through instruction. What follows asks whether an artificial system could acquire anything like that route into moral competence, and where the analogy breaks down.
