5.4.3. Promoting AI Development Policies that Discourage Authoritarian Use
To prevent the misuse of AI for authoritarian purposes and promote altruistic AI development, governments, industries, and academia must collaborate to create policies and guidelines that center around transparency, ethics, and multi-stakeholder involvement. Below we offer recommendations for achieving these goals:

(1) Prioritize transparency, collaboration, and ethics in AI governance: Engage stakeholders from diverse sectors – including government, business, civil society – in the development and refinement of AI policies and regulations. This multi-stakeholder approach ensures diverse perspectives are incorporated into policy decisions, mitigating the risk of unintended biases and fostering greater accountability.

(2) Promote knowledge exchange and cooperative legal frameworks for ethical AI: Encourage cross-border collaboration to share best practices, lessons learned, and successful strategies for ethical AI development. This cooperation can help establish shared norms and standards, strengthening international commitment to anti-authoritarian AI applications.

(3) Regulate the international trade of potentially authoritarian AI technologies: Implement stringent regulatory protocols to oversee the export, import, and usage of AI technologies that may enable surveillance or social control. Safeguards may involve export control policies, and regular assessments of export-import transactions to ensure compliance with democratic principles.

(4) Encourage democratic and ethical criteria for AI projects and collaborations: Develop incentives for public and private sectors to prioritize ethical considerations in the design, development, and deployment of AI systems. These criteria should extend to international collaborations and partnerships, with emphasis on transparency, accountability, and cooperation among all parties involved.

(5) Support research initiatives that focus on preventing authoritarian use of AI: Fund and promote research that enhances understanding of AI's implications on democracy, free expression, and human rights. This support can lead to the development of countermeasures and safeguards to protect democratic values in the face of potential authoritarian misuse.

(6) Raise awareness and understanding of the risks associated with AI misuse: Promote education and public awareness campaigns to inform citizens, policymakers, and developers about the potential consequences of AI technologies misused for authoritarian purposes.

(7) Develop guidelines for responsible AI deployment by corporations: Encourage the adoption of strong corporate social responsibility principles that prioritize ethical AI development and discourage the sale or deployment of AI technology for authoritarian applications.

By adopting these recommendations, stakeholders can work together to develop a robust policy framework that emphasizes transparency, ethics, and collaboration in AI development, discouraging the misuse of AI for authoritarian purposes.
