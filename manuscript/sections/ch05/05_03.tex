\section{Integrating Moral Intuitions and Deliberation}
\label{sec:5.3}
Building an AI system's moral judgment means deciding how much weight a fast, intuitive read of a situation carries against a slower, deliberate one — and, as section~\ref{sec:5.3.1} argues, the popular shorthand for that trade-off is less settled than it looks. The stake for the floor is that a bearer meeting an unanticipated pressure has only these two resources to meet it with, and a design that automatically defers to whichever ran slower has settled in advance a question the case itself should decide.
