5.2.4. The Role of Introspection for AI Systems
Introspection has a general-purpose version and a moral one, and conflating them is how the word gets claimed for systems that have not earned it. The general case is section 4.1.2's: a metacognitive layer that tracks whether a strategy is working and adjusts. What ethics needs on top of that is narrower — a check not of whether an output was wrong but of whether it drifted from the commitments the system is supposed to be operating under, which is a different question and can come out the other way. A strategy can work perfectly on the wrong goal.

The benefits are real where they are bounded. A system that inspects its own allocation pattern before releasing the next round of decisions catches a skew that a retrospective audit would find months later. A system that can report which evidence moved it gives the person relying on it something to evaluate. Both are cases where the introspective output is checkable against something outside the system.

The difficulties are worse than the benefits are good, and they are the same difficulty three times. A self-report is worth having only if it reflects what actually happened inside, and a fluent reconstruction after the fact is indistinguishable from the real thing at the point of use — which is section 2.4.1's argument, arriving here as an engineering problem rather than a philosophical one. A self-monitoring layer trained the same way as the layer it monitors reproduces its biases and adds the appearance of oversight. And a system modeling its own states in that much detail is holding information it did not need for the task, with the handling questions that follow.

Two systems are worth naming as partial instances, with the qualification each needs. An intelligent tutoring system that tracks a model of the student's self-regulation and adapts to it is introspecting on somebody else [[cite:C0327]]. Post-hoc explanation methods extract an account of which inputs drove an output, which is the system being interrogated rather than observing itself, and the account is only as good as the extraction is faithful [[cite:C0328]]. The word gets stretched furthest where the underlying system is most impressive, and the stretch bears no relation to how well it fits.
