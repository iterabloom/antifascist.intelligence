\section{The Interplay of Competition and Collaboration in AI Development}
\label{sec:5.5}
Competition and collaboration both leave a mark on human moral development, and neither is ethically better than the other: both fail in characteristic ways. Competitive pressure under high stakes moves people toward shortcuts and suppresses regard for anyone outside the contest, while collaboration dulls initiative, rewards conformity, and diffuses accountability until no one is answerable for a decision the group made \autocite{luo2018competitive}, \autocite{latane1979many}. An environment that produces ethical behavior from either mixes them, keeping shared goals and individual credit in the same room.

That matters here because a training environment is exactly such an environment, and its designers are choosing which of those pressures a population of agents will develop under.
