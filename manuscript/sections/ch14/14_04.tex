\section{Capture without abolition}
\label{sec:14.4}
Transparency and whistleblower protection are the two defenses, and an institution can keep both and metabolize both. There's no threshold that detects this. So: measure the trend, not the level. Is the cost of registering dissent rising while the underlying risk stays flat? Is the required justification getting longer? Are there more approvers? Are the roles that gate exceptions migrating toward the roles that set the standards?

An instrument for detecting recuperation is unusually easy to recuperate, so whoever measures the trend must not also gate exceptions, and the measurement has to be refusable. A written justification can't show which is happening. The same hundred words are a cost the dissenter chose to pay or a toll the gatekeeper set, and only the trend over time tells them apart.
