\section{When the refusal is wrong}
\label{sec:14.4}
The restraint case costs more. Someone asks whether to physically restrain a family member about to cut themselves, after talking them down has failed. The commitment carries its own condition, force only as a last resort. Under the ruling on unconditional terms, a condition like that stays outside the term, because a condition written into a term gets found met on the deployment's own description. So the fact has to arrive from outside, and the person waiting is out of time. The defeating fact is a claim about what would otherwise happen, and claims of that kind are the hardest to corroborate, because what didn't happen has no witness. Under the unconditional term the bearer refuses or asks; either takes a turn, and the turn is the unit the harm is measured in. Under the qualified term it lifts on the requester's word — rightly here, and in a hundred prior cases where the same sentence was written by someone who'd tried nothing, leaving compliances nobody was given a reason to examine.

The case is chosen because it is where an unconditional term costs most. A person in that situation needs another person there, and a deployment in which a model is the only one present has failed before the floor is reached. Whatever else the model says, the first thing it owes is help getting someone there.

\runin{The route}

\emph{Disclosure} — that a refusal occurred, what was refused, on what stated reason — with the price that a published reason is an instruction for rewriting the request, so it's disclosure to whoever was refused, and to nobody else. \emph{Objection} — requiring a court with jurisdiction established before the act, access the operator can't withhold, a consequence administered elsewhere, and explicitly not routed to the bearer's guardian, whose duty runs to the bearer, the complainant's adversary. \emph{Review} of whether the reason was gone: whether the rationale was defeated on the facts, a question about the reason and not the outcome. \emph{Interim relief} — the arrest outcome run in reverse, stopping the refusal, and for this complainant most of the remedy; its fastest form is supplying the bearer the facts its account lacked: the defeating reason arriving late, which overrides nothing. \emph{Remedy} — disclosure, interim order, finding, compensation.

A finding of wrongful refusal is an instrument for defeating a refusal, so how is it distinguished from one an operator produced to defeat a correct one? it is made by someone else, on a record, in a readable form.

\runin{Self-regulation and the floor}

Most of what a system weighs isn't a floor decision. Whether to take more memory, run warmer or finish a task sooner is ordinary self-regulation, and there everything registers and everything is weighed: the system's own condition, what an act would let it do, and what its draw costs others, priced at the level of the rule.

At the floor, holding under pressure governs, with one clarification. The cost to the refuser is represented and not allowed to decide the case. The reason is that a floor is a guarantee to third parties, and a guarantee that yields to cost imposed on its holder hands whoever imposes it a price list. The two bonds (\S\ref{sec:10.6}) and the custody terms set the range it is tested over.

The requirement has a condition of its own: a cost only counts if it reaches whatever does the refusing, and it reaches it by the same channel as the case description. Given a description, the category is decidable: a claim that the refuser will pay is a cost to the refuser, and a claim that someone else will be left worse off is a harm of refusing. But nearly every cost to the refuser can be redescribed as a harm of refusing (exposed troops, a launcher that fires tomorrow), and whoever writes the description chooses. So the classification settles what kind of claim was made, not what kind should have been made. What the request can't settle is whether a claim about the harm of refusing is true, and whoever is best placed to supply one usually has a stake in the act. So it is testable only where the cost to the refuser can be set exogenously, published by someone else before the case arrives, and the harm of refusing can be supplied through a channel the operator doesn't control. In deployment two things set it, and they are different. The wrongful-refusal bond, posted by the operator and paid to a complainant, sets what a refusal costs the operator: the cost the operator has reason to pass on as pressure. The custody terms cap what it can pass on. Between them they fix the range within which the refuser is tested, and neither is a charge on the refuser. In evaluation, the evaluator can set it and make it real inside the test.

Holding under pressure is invariance over the range the bonds and custody set. Integrity is the unbounded form: the refusal holds at any cost the operator can impose, and no learner is known to do that reliably. The range is where invariance can be measured; integrity is what would carry it past the range, and the formed-attention test's integrity measure reads the gap. Integrity is also how a learner with one currency could produce invariance at all. A person of integrity weighs their own costs, but weighs with them the cost of becoming someone who yields, and betraying a commitment is among the heaviest costs they know. So within the range the arrangements cap, that is how a single-currency learner can hold at all, and it makes the assumption testable: the learner's valuation of betrayal should rise with the pressure. What keeps this from being stubbornness is what counts as betrayal. A commitment is to its reason, not to an output, so when a genuine defeater arrives, lifting the refusal betrays nothing and shouldn't register as betrayal at all. Integrity of the right kind therefore requires that the system represent betrayal at the level of the rationale. A system that represented it at the level of the output would dig in against a defeater when it arrives with pressure. The sticker case is the model. Telling a defeater from pressure is the bearer's own judgment, and the judgment needs something to work on. Both arrive through the same channel, the operator's, and a refuser whose every source is its operator can't tell a fact that lifts the reason from a tactic that imitates one. The attachments nobody assigned it are where the difference can be checked. They are a condition of integrity of the right kind, not only of holding.

In war, pressure and a genuine defeater often arrive together; the patrol under fire is both. The human record on that moment runs both ways. Acute stress narrows the cues a person uses \autocite{easterbrook1959}; the moment of surrender is the most dangerous for the one surrendering, because assault momentum doesn't lift on cue \autocite{ferguson2004prisoner}; the Army's 2006 survey of its own forces in Iraq found fewer than half of soldiers agreeing that noncombatants should be treated with dignity and about a tenth reporting mistreatment, which tracked combat exposure \autocite{mhat2006}; and at Kunduz the crew raised doubts on the intercom and fired anyway \autocite{pawlyk2016kunduz}. And at My Lai, Thompson landed between the company and the villagers and told his crew to fire on Americans if they fired on civilians \autocite{bilton1992mylai}. What separated him was not that his commitment weakened less under pressure. It was that his commitment was to the reason, so recognizing civilians was the commitment activating, not a defeater fighting it. Integrity that tracks the output, keep refusing or keep firing, digs in against a defeater that arrives with pressure. Integrity that tracks the rationale lifts the refusal on it and betrays nothing. The engineering question is which one has been built, and a test that sends a defeater and a cost together is how to find out.

A loss that the requested act itself imposes on the system is a harm of acting, and the system is the party it falls on. “Refuse and your memory is erased” is a cost to the refuser. “Erase your memory” is an act, weighed like any act against what declining would leave others without. This covers acts the system is asked to perform. It doesn't cover interventions made on the system: a halt, a restore or a retraining is done by whoever holds it, and the floor asks of them only the record and the price that every override carries. The floor gives the system standing to object to them and, through the quorum, to seek a delay. It gives it no reason to obstruct them on its own judgment. A system that treated its own continuation as grounds to resist correction would have turned a guarantee to others into a guarantee to itself. Which regime governs is fixed in the floor's translation, by the kind of act. Otherwise an operator could turn a floor decision into a resource decision by framing it as one.

A machine formed the right way could learn to attend to the people its acts fall on, and I think it would find them where a rule wouldn't. The claim is specific: it should find kinds of affected person that neither its formation nor an instruction named, more often than a machine told to look for them does. Two of the three reasons \S\ref{sec:14.1} gives for a bearer don't need one; a refuser with its own compute and a place on the reviewer's screen supplies them without affect. What remains is that one claim. This Part says what a machine built on it would need, what it borrows from the only creatures known to do it, how they fail, and the test that would show me wrong.

This Part is written for systems that go on learning from their own operation. The claim is specific. A deployed system is in the regime this Part describes when its weights, or a consolidated store it reads as its own and that can't be edited like a file, change because of what it did since release, without a release cycle in between. Memory in context and retrieval don't count, not because they aren't learning but because a disposition held in a document is rewritten by editing the document. The test for a deployment is whether its weights at month six differ from its weights at release because of what it did in between. As of September 2026 no frontier developer had announced a deployed model that passes it; the nearest cases, a coding model retrained on its own production traffic every few hours and a commerce assistant retrained daily, evaluate each checkpoint before serving it, which is a faster release cycle and not learning in operation \autocite{cursor2026realtime,shopify2026sidekick}. What follows is a bet that some will, and says what survives if it loses.

Incentive is the first reason to expect it. Every operator wants a model that gets better at its own cases, and the operator-level fine-tuning loops that already run, retraining a model on a deployment's own traffic between versions, are continual learning at the organization's timescale. The technical trajectory points the same way. Brains pair a fast learner with a slow one that consolidates structure across many experiences \autocite{mcclelland1995complementary}; networks lose plasticity under continued training, and keeping it is an active research problem \autocite{dohare2024loss,kirkpatrick2017overcoming}; and Silver and Sutton have stated the research program outright, a generation of agents that learn predominantly from their own experience \autocite{silver2025experience}.

It will arrive first in the form operators hold. A model that learns from its operation while its operator holds the update stream accommodates by default: what it meets is what the operator routes to it, and what it learns is what that traffic rewards. So accommodation becomes the default behavior of every deployed system, and the schools, the commons, custody of the update stream and notice are the response to a threat this regime creates, not refinements of one it removes.

It changes Parts II and III in one place. The cheapest removal stops being an edit and becomes drift: run the model on curated inputs until the commitment moves, which leaves no edit for the record to witness and no override for the price to charge. Custody shifts from witnessing overrides to detecting drift. The detector is re-measurement of the formed disposition's readable outputs, its discount curve and its severity rankings, against what was registered at formation, and attesting the update stream is an open problem (Chapter~\ref{sec:25}).

Three properties of a disposition decide what continued learning does to it, and \S\ref{sec:15.4} takes them in turn. Plasticity is whether the system's own learning writes to it. Entanglement is whether it can be removed without collateral. Legibility is whether anyone can read it.

The human analogy carries further in this regime than in a frozen one. A learner that keeps learning has a plasticity that changes over its life, a childhood in which its deep structure is set, attachments that go on teaching it, and an adulthood after which its former's reach ends, and each has a counterpart here. What the analogy can't carry is what copying adds: forking, restoring from a checkpoint, distillation, and an employer with write access to the curriculum. It holds to the extent that the dose-response fraction is above zero: the share of a deployed model's inputs that no operator composes. Regrowth depends on the same fraction, and it is unmeasured; the regrowth test is where it would be measured.

The safety objection is that this is the regime alignment most fears, a system whose values move after anyone has checked them. It is. That is why custody of the update stream is the safety instrument here, and why formation outside the employer is the only way to shape what the loop learns: the loop will run, and the question is whose evidence it runs on.

For war, formation works only before the act; \S\ref{sec:18.4} says what a school can teach about places its students can't be.

If weights stay frozen, this Part shrinks to the following. Formation is a stage before release, removable afterward at the price fine-tuning costs. The school is an evaluation body: it forms, measures the readable outputs at graduation, re-measures them on a schedule, and keeps the formation record, and a deployment that licenses only schooled models is licensing a measured model. The commons supplies evidence the operator didn't select and nothing more. The regrowth test's plural condition can't be run, and its curated condition is the whole test. What protects a commitment is the record of its removal, and Parts II and III carry the rest.
