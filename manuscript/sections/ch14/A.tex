\chapter{Further Tests}
\label{ch:appendix}
\standing{The first part of this appendix holds for any mechanism. The second
assumes the route, and assumes a welfare regime nobody has built.}
These are the research agenda's remaining entries: open questions with a
definite test, where a result would refine the proposal rather than change
what it recommends. The form is chapter~\ref{sec:11}'s — the unresolved
issue, the test, what would count against the proposal, and the access the
test requires — and the division is by what each entry presupposes. The
first part compares constructions that can be built now. The second
presupposes a bearer and a welfare regime that do not exist, so nothing in
it can be run until something else has been.
