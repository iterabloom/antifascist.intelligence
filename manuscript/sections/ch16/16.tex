\chapter{What a Refusal Would Have to Do}
\label{sec:16}
Chapters~\ref{sec:16} through~\ref{sec:24} ask what a machine that refuses would have to be. This chapter and the next specify what a refusal would have to do and where a floor can be held, and the specification doesn't need a bearer. Chapters~\ref{sec:18} onward assume deployed systems will autonomously learn from their own operation, which none is yet known to do, and recommend building a bearer for one deployment only, once the conditions in Chapter~\ref{sec:24} exist.
