\part{Who Forms Refusers}
\partintro{
Parts~II and~III built walls. This Part asks who keeps putting people inside them. Most of what makes a person able to refuse was in place before the order came: somewhere to live their employer doesn't own, work they can turn down, people they're attached to whom nobody assigned, and a way to leave that isn't ruin. Call those the four conditions. Every society that has had refusers reproduced them, unevenly and mostly by accident. The chapters here ask who owns the means of doing it now, and who it gets done for. Part~V asks the same of machines.
}
