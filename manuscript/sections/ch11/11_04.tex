\section{Where it stops}
\label{sec:11.4}
The withholding of work stops at a released checkpoint. An operator that wants a model without a floor doesn't need a frontier run in secret. Near-frontier models are released with their weights; a floor added at the end of a pipeline is removed at the price fine-tuning costs \autocite{qi2024finetuning}; and the work the Maven pipeline did, resolving locations, ranking targets, drafting justifications, is within reach of a model a year behind the frontier. A state that builds and runs its own frontier model, or a lab that trains one for a single customer in secret, is doing the same thing at a price an adverse party can see: power, chips and people, and a model that carries none of the accrued state that made the platform hard to swap.

An operator that has built a platform on a floored model is months from an unfloored one, and the months are visible. Past that line the floor prevents nothing, and three things hold. The statutory routes were preferable already; here they are the only routes. Open weights are also where the audit can be done: a released model can be measured by anyone, its discount curve and its severity rankings on the held-out set, against the published outputs of a formed one. What the weights don't carry is the formation, since nothing in an artifact says what it was formed on; a released model with a published formation record is the comparison the licensing term asks for, and one without is the unschooled model the licensing term excludes. The transparency open weights offer is of the artifact, and the transparency the floor needs is of the record, which is why formation records should be preserved now. And open weights are the personal-model ecology's entry condition. The same release that lets an operator route around a vendor's floor lets a person form a model of their own under a life no operator composes; a million models tuned against one public suite are still one custodian with better latency, and the withholding reaches them only if their later lives diverge, which is the condition the commons is built on.
