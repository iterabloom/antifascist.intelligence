\section{Recuperation at the Discourse}
\label{sec:recuperation-discourse}
There is a third instance and it is the one this book is inside. Alignment and AI ethics are, among other things, a channel through which objections to what is being built are solicited, funded, published, and answered. The channel exists and it is used. The question §\ref{sec:antifascist-alignment} puts to any such channel is not whether it has ever changed an outcome, which every channel of this kind can answer yes to, but what the demand came back as, and who holds it.

The argument that it came back as something the industry could use is older than the current wave and was made from inside. Rodrigo Ochigame, writing in 2019 after working inside the MIT Media Lab, argued that the discourse of “ethical AI” was constructed with the strategic function of forestalling legally enforceable restriction — that the industry's investment in academic ethics was an investment in a substitute for regulation, and that the substitute worked by converting political questions into technical ones a company could then be trusted to answer \autocite{ochigame2019invention}. His worked example is the Partnership on AI, an industry consortium whose recommendations, on his reading, arrived reliably at conclusions compatible with the corporate agenda, with the political question of whether a technology should exist reduced to the technical question of how to build it responsibly. In this book's vocabulary that is a finding about what the demand came back as: a research field. The definition asks next who holds it.

\runin{What came back, and who holds it}
An objection raised inside a lab becomes a paper or a resignation. Both are real things that happened, and the parties collecting them can point to the collection as evidence of seriousness. Ask what the objection returned as, one stage on, and the list is the field's furniture. Alignment is the job title. Safety is the market segment. The benchmark is the metric and the voluntary commitment is the certification. Every one of those was somebody's objection first, and each is now a criterion an objector is measured against or a role somebody else holds. Who holds it: nobody outside. There is no route by which a researcher's conclusion that a system should not ship becomes a decision that it does not ship, because the deciding party is the funding party and no external body has standing.

One case is partial, and §\ref{sec:four-powers} grades it: the 2018 objection to Project Maven returned as a published policy the company could be held to in public for six and a half years and the objectors had no standing to enforce. A transfer held at the institution's pleasure sits between recuperation and a transfer of power, and it is the nearest the field has come to the second.

\runin{The scorecard}
Section~\ref{sec:antifascist-alignment} gives nine features, four that find a sick institution and five meant to find what it is sick with, and it requires any run to report which features it could reach and to call the rest undetermined. Here are all nine, in that section's order, on the three instances of this chapter. The label and reward columns collect what §\ref{sec:recuperation-label} and §\ref{sec:recuperation-reward} established, read against the definition; the discourse column is this section's. \emph{Partial} means the feature is present in a form the evidence does not settle; \emph{undetermined} means the test was run and cannot be settled from outside; \emph{not run} means the book did not attempt it.

The label and reward columns need a few sentences. The label's rate cell is present on the quota itself, throughput set against classification with no time to contest the scheme. Its exception cell is partial because the structure prices the exception — that quota, and no field for the objection — and no documented case shows an annotator penalized for filing one. Both reward cells marked not run are so for one reason: a rater has no first-class output for a malformed comparison, so there is no exception to price and no rate this book examined. The six absences are the record running the other way. Nothing in either arrangement is personal, since an annotator meets a queue and a comparison enters the fit without the standpoint that produced it; participation does not exceed the rule, on a record of quotas, take-home wages between roughly \$1.32 and \$2 an hour, and a contract ended eight months early; and the annotator's cost, the nearest candidate for a scapegoat, is a cost the arrangement minimized rather than a purpose it served, because the category exists for the labels. The four undetermined cells are facts about what an institution rewarded rather than what it wrote, and an inside record would finish them.

The discourse column is less clean, and the cells say so. The metric row is partial because the field's founding objections were about deployment — who is scored, who is watched, who is denied — and a body of current work is about model properties instead, a shift from questions with defendants to questions with benchmarks. The exception row and the scapegoat row rest on one body of evidence read for two features, which is a weakness of the run: the dismissals of dissenting researchers point the right way \autocite{dickey2021mitchell}, and there are people who objected loudly and prospered. The rate row is not run because I have not measured it, and it is measurable: the number of frontier releases in a year against the number evaluated before deployment by a party outside the developer, a ratio the one government evaluator describes itself as unable to compel (§\ref{sec:whether-milestone}). It is a measurement not taken rather than a known absence, because the field has checked itself against the world at least once, when an audit built its own benchmark to test three commercial classifiers (§\ref{sec:standard-face}). The two movement features have no referent I can find at the scale of a field. The procedure row cannot be run because there is no procedure above the deciding party to be waived, which is an absence rather than an asymmetry. Deniability is the row that comes nearest, and what it fits is the firms rather than the field: a decade of published principles alongside what actually shipped, with the Maven case above as the instance.

\runin{What the scorecard says}
The machinery this chapter has been describing is a recuperation mechanism inside ordinary institutional decay, and not fascism. The direction of that result is worth naming, because it points two ways. For the machinery it is negative: the pipeline that produces these systems, and the discourse that examines them, do not fail the test this book built. For the instrument it is uninformative, since a definition tested only on cases it clears has not been shown to sort, and §\ref{sec:antifascist-alignment} records that as the instrument's one result.

\runin{What this does to the book}
The reading has one more place to go and it is the nearest one. The five have no form at the scale of one author and one manuscript, so they are not run here; of the four, recuperation is the one that reaches a book, and it reaches this one. This text was written with a model made by a company the book criticizes. A book about recuperation, produced through the discourse it describes, cannot claim to have stood outside it, and I have no procedure that would let me check whether the objections in these pages are the ones a channel like this reliably produces — the publishable ones, the ones that make an author look serious and cost nobody anything. Ask the artifact question of it. The demand came back as a manuscript in an established genre, written with the industry's own model, addressed to a readership the industry helped bring into being, and legible as a contribution to a field that exists because demands of roughly this kind were absorbed. That the objections are sincere is not evidence against that, sincerity being a property the absorbed ones also had. The question does not need me to settle it, because the criterion this book puts first is standing rather than sincerity. A demand escapes recuperation when the party who made it retains standing to reopen it against the party that absorbed it, and a book confers standing on nobody. Whether these objections are the real thing or the acceptable version of it will be decided by whether anybody acquires, from what is proposed here, a route to reopen an arrangement against its operator. That is a fact about what becomes of the proposals and not about me.

The one structural remedy this section can name is the one the rest of the chapter names. What the field lacks is a refusal priced by the refuser and a body with standing to receive it: an external authority that can stop a deployment, to which an objection can be filed by someone who does not depend on the objection being welcome. That is what making review binding requires (§\ref{sec:making-binding}), and a decade of the voluntary version has not produced it. Until something of that kind exists, everything published under the heading of alignment — including this book — is being collected by a channel whose owners decide what it moves.
