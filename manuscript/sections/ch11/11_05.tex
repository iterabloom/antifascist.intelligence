\section{Before the statute: the auto-proceed class}
\label{sec:11.5}
Until the statute passes, the pipeline runs. This section and the three after it describe what a floor can do meanwhile. Each measure I propose in the nearby sections lapses when the statute takes effect.

The removal terms say a determination made at a rate no adjudicator could sustain didn't occur. Chapter~\ref{sec:7} carries the ratio's sorting rule over to removal, though not the ratio itself, and here the sorting has a direction. The floor makes acting on a person require more than declining to act, and in an abduction and forcible transfer pipeline the act is adverse: a charging document, a detention, a location, a removal. So a case may auto-proceed only toward liberty, such as release on recognizance, termination of proceedings, return of documents, or a grant or renewal of status. No adverse act is ever in an auto-proceed class.

Before operating, the deployment pre-registers the liberty classes, the thresholds that assign cases to them, the evidence the sorting may rely on, and the expected share of each. For adverse determinations it pre-registers something else: for each kind of determination, the minimum time below which the determination is taken not to have occurred. The floor court sets that minimum, not the agency.\note{
A quota of 700 cases a year is about three a working day. Whether three a day leaves a judge enough time to decide an asylum claim is then settled before any case is heard, by a court, and written down where anyone can check it. Otherwise the agency settles it afterward, by meeting the number. A validation guide that turns a tattoo into points is a determination made in no time at all, and Table A.2's individual-determination entry says it didn't occur.
}

Then the determinations are sampled. Reviewers appointed by the floor court, and until it exists by the court that has the case, as a special master or monitor, none of whom produced the record, re-adjudicate a sample of adverse determinations, weighted toward those made below the minimum, against evidence the agency didn't compose, and the error rate is published. When a kind of determination's sampled error rate exceeds its published bound, that kind is suspended until the bound is met, and everyone it was applied to is notified.\note{
The liberty classes are sampled too, so that their bounds are honest. An error there releases someone who should have been held for a hearing, and the hearing can still be held. Unlike Maven's, this pipeline's errors can be found, because the people they fell on are alive, can be located and can say what happened. Sampling finds the errors the people couldn't report. Counsel at government expense, and a stay that holds the removal while it is contested, are what let them report the rest.
}
