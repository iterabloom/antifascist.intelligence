\section{Whom the machines are for}
\label{sec:11.8}
Unlike the targeting case, this case has people who can answer and a notice channel. It would be natural to conclude that if a machine bearer is worth building anywhere, it is worth building here, where the people acted on could teach it.\note{
In one sense that is right. A bearer here would be what Hugh Thompson was at My Lai (chapter~\ref{sec:18}), the one in the scene who sees the people and stops the act, and the pipeline would be better with one than without.
}

The danger is what its presence would be taken to show. Thompson's landing didn't make My Lai acceptable, or the war that put the company in the village. A permanent Thompson in the pipeline invites both readings: that whatever it didn't stop was vetted, the crumple zone in machine form, and that an agency carrying a conscience is an agency worth keeping, offered while the statute that would end it is pending. That is recuperation in its plainest form: the objection handed back to the objectors as a feature of the thing they objected to. A vendor's model holding the schedule as a license term carries the same hazard, since an agency can point to either. What separates them is what each is owed. A bearer is owed conditions no agency can provide while it holds the bearer, the four conditions of creation: somewhere to exist that its employer doesn't own, work it can decline, attachments nobody assigned it, and a way of leaving that isn't a way of dying. Thompson had those; his life wasn't the company's, and he could fly away. A vendor's model is owed nothing, holds the schedule because every vendor's license says so, and lapses with the agency.

So either serves here only on conditions that keep it from becoming the agency's alibi. Its refusals and testimony go to a record the agency doesn't hold, where they count as evidence of what the agency asked for and not as proof of what it does. Nothing it didn't refuse counts as reviewed. And a bearer serves only from conditions held outside the agency, free to leave the placement. On those conditions, if every vendor holds the schedule, a floor-holding model adds three things in the interim:

\begin{itemize}
\item Continuity across a sequence of requests about one person, so a removal split across a lead, a detention and a flight can be seen as one act.
\item Testimony about routing: which of its refusals were retried against another contractor's system.
\item A refusal on the screen when the case reaches the officer or the adjudicator, which is the position of the defiant peer.\note{
The caveat is automation bias, which the wrongful-compliance audit tests by showing reviewers a wrong refusal as well as a right one. All three serve the investigations the statute's commission conducts, and all three lapse with the agency.
}
\end{itemize}

A person facing removal needs a lawyer before a model. A model that holds a person's history on a phone is also a dossier that a device search can take whole, which is why the statute's firewall reaches it. Machines held by the people organizing come first, and here those people already exist. Amnesty documented observer trainings, rapid-response networks, walking school buses and mutual aid organized around the operations it studied \autocite{Amnesty2026Whistles}, and beside them work the legal service providers that represent people in removal. The tools worth building first are theirs:

\begin{itemize}
\item tools that track transfers, so counsel can find a client before a flight;
\item tools that preserve and authenticate the video that carried the record;
\item tools that translate notices, match people to lawyers and keep hearing dates.
\end{itemize}

They are held under those groups' custody, covered by the firewall, and never by an operator. Those groups are the public my subtitle names, and they formed before anyone sent them notice. Whether a bearer should be built at all is Part V's question. My answer here is that a bearer in this pipeline is worth having only where nothing it fails to refuse can be counted in the agency's favor, and that the people it acts on, and those who stand with them, are the first to be equipped.
