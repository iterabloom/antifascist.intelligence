\section{Where the acts move}
\label{sec:11.3}
Table A.3, in Appendix~\ref{sec:A}, runs the nine positions on this pipeline, position by position, and a tenth that only ending the agency opens: where its workforce goes.

The positions that failed at Minab, the case description and review, are the ones this pipeline has documented most fully. The documents show both failing by design rather than by accident: a risk tool with its release option removed, a points table that made a tattoo into membership, and a review that need not occur unless the person knows to ask for it. The abduction and forcible transfer pipeline adds a failure Minab did not show: a halt ordered in time and contemptuously ignored.
