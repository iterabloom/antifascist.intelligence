\section{It defends what it tends}
\label{sec:21.3}
Caregiving protects the dependent against whatever threatens it. Taylor's account of stress responses in females, \emph{tend-and-befriend}, describes the tending: nurturant activity that protects oneself and one's offspring, and the social networks that help \autocite{taylor2000tend}. Protectiveness toward what one tends is also hostility toward what endangers it, and Taylor's account of tending has that defensive side without the hormone studies, whose best-known result has not replicated well \autocite{nave2015oxytocin}.

For a machine, this failure needs no deception. The operator's own people are often really vulnerable: a patrol under fire, a town within range of the launcher. A caregiving system registers their exposure, correctly, and caregiving moves it toward protecting them, which in a targeting pipeline means toward the airstrike. The discount and the burden do nothing here, because nothing is false. Supplying a fabricated sympathetic dependent is the cheap version of this failure. This is the expensive version, and in war it is the ordinary case.

In war, then, caregiving can't contain its own pull. The patrol's exposure is real; caregiving registers it and moves toward protecting it, and nothing in caregiving says how far. What stops the movement at the airstrike is the floor: an unconditional term over the act, and an action threshold caregiving can't raise. Caregiving for the patrol enters as a harm of refusing, discounted by its source's stake like any other claim, and is weighed at the threshold. What caregiving contributes in a targeting pipeline is upstream of that weighing: who is in the building, which is the scoped-omission case, and the one failure Minab actually had. Whether that salience needs caregiving, or could be had from attention trained on claims, is what the formed-attention test's claim-salience arm exists to decide. The human record runs the same way. Browning's men shot because the alternative was leaving their comrades to do it, and Thompson landed because the villagers were as present to him as the company. Caregiving's direction was set by formation and attachments, which is my claim about machines.
