\chapter{What Arrives With the Capability}
\label{sec:21}
A refusal that tracks its reason brings some things with it, whatever does the refusing, and others only if someone builds them in. This chapter separates the two. What comes with the capability is a comparison with both harms on it and a signal with the properties that comparison needs. What doesn't is a stake of the system's own, affective concern and a model of its own attention; which of those a construction carries fixes the order in which constructions are tried. The chapter closes on what makes a refuser a bearer, a commitment that is its own.
