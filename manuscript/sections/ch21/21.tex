\chapter{What Arrives With the Capability}
\label{sec:21}
A refusal that tracks its reason brings some things with it, whatever does the refusing, and others only if someone builds them in. This chapter separates the two. A comparison with both harms on it, and a signal with the properties that comparison needs, come with the capability (\S\ref{sec:21.1}, \S\ref{sec:21.4}). A stake of the system's own, affective concern and a model of its own attention don't, and which of them a construction carries fixes the order in which constructions are tried (\S\ref{sec:21.2}, \S\ref{sec:21.3}). What makes a refuser a bearer, a commitment that is its own, closes the chapter (\S\ref{sec:21.5}).
