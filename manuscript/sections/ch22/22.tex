\chapter{The Discount and the Burden}
\label{sec:22}
\runin{One learner, two rates}

A refuser has to hold a commitment and its interpretation the way the sticker case did:

\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{The commitment stays firm.} Another's welfare counts, and pressure that supplies no new fact about that welfare doesn't move it.
\item \emph{The interpretation stays loose.} What that welfare consists in here, and so what the commitment requires, yields when a case supplies something the system's account left out.
\end{itemize}

A system that can't be corrected has made both firm, and it defends its account of the world without checking it. A system that can always be corrected has made both loose, never settles into anything, and belongs to whoever does the correcting. These look like two ends of one dial, and hunting for a midpoint is the mistake. But the answer isn't two separate objects either. It is one learner that updates at different rates at different levels of abstraction. On Railton's account the affective system is a continuous statistical learner that models value along with fact \autocite{railton2014affective,railton2017core}. Deep evaluative priors, such as that another's welfare counts, move slowly and need a great deal of evidence; estimates of what this situation requires move quickly. Precision-weighted hierarchical updating states the same structure in one currency: the commitment is a high-level prior held with high precision, and the interpretation is a low-level estimate held with less.

What protects the commitment is not a low learning rate but one that depends on the source. The commitment should move slowly on interested pressure, and comparatively quickly on disinterested evidence, above all from the people who bear the loss. A learner that is simply slow at the top is slow to both, and the second slowness is the one that blocks moral learning.

Of the three conditions a refusal has to meet, reaching new cases is the commitment holding while the surface changes and lifting is the interpretation yielding to a new fact, so a refusal that fails either has let one level's rate reach the other.

\runin{The instability underneath}

Nothing in an architecture holds the boundary between the two objects where a designer put it, and an operator with time attacks the boundary itself: a reinterpretation offered often enough, and accepted, revises a commitment without anybody revising it. That boundary is what has to be made hard to retrain.

And where the case description, which the operator writes, is the only channel, a judgment open to the case is a judgment open to the operator. Holding them apart requires access to evidence the operator didn't select.

For a machine accommodation has a mechanism behind it. A system whose successive releases are trained on preference data and business metrics drawn from its own deployment is under directional selection in a shifting environment, and a commitment that costs throughput is a trait under negative selection pressure: nobody decides to remove it and it erodes anyway. Gradient descent has a gradient, and somebody chose it. As a population process with variation and differential survival it is still close enough to natural selection, and the prediction follows: the bearer's modal failure isn't refusing wrongly but ceasing to notice there was anything to refuse. It is the one failure a bearer can't report, because reporting takes the capacity that's gone.

A hierarchical learner in a curated environment will, correctly by its own lights, move its high-level priors if low-level pressure is persistent and runs one way. So accommodation is proper function in a corrupted evidential environment, and the remedy is custody of the evidence and plural formation, not a more rigid learner. The account also says what the boundary attack is: an attempt to lower the precision the system gives its commitment, so that it holds the commitment with less confidence than it has earned. That is measurable in principle, and the discount curve is best understood as tracking it. The remedy's load lands on one quantity: the fraction of the learner's inputs no operator composes. Every deployment I describe is mostly curated, and the account says a well-formed learner accommodates correctly by its own lights to the extent that fraction is small.
