THIS BOOK began as a thought experiment: “Is it possible to write something that literally nobody agrees with?” I wanted to annoy as many potential audiences as possible.

So, for leftists, I made sure to talk a lot about machines having feelings.

For techno-optimists and Singularity-pilled folks, I made sure to discuss racial capitalism and how all the technology they celebrate is being used for war crimes in Gaza and Iran.

In other words, I did what I always do, which is sit at my computer and think about an amorphous collection of loosely-associated ideas for far longer than is reasonable.

You hold the result.

My first draft of this foreword was all about the different strategies I used to prompt LLMs to generate the text. I did do those, but I did not think you would care. If you do, most of that is here: \url{https://github.com/iterabloom/ethical.superintelligence}.

The argument in this book is mine. I chose what survived, revised what I kept, and I am responsible for the many things that are probably wrong with it.
