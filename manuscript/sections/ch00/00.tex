THIS BOOK began as a thought experiment, which went something like “Is it possible to write something that literally nobody agrees with?” I wanted to annoy as many potential audiences as possible.

So, for leftists, I made sure to talk a lot about machines having feelings.

For techno-optimists and Singularity-pilled folks, I made sure to discuss racial capitalism and how all their technology is being used for war crimes in Gaza and Iran.

In other words, I did what I always do, which is sit at my computer and think about an amorphous collection of loosely related concepts for far longer than is reasonable.

You hold the result.

The initial draft of this foreword was all about the different strategies I used to prompt LLMs to generate the text, and I did do those, but I didn't think you would care, and if you do, most of that is here: \url{https://github.com/iterabloom/ethical.superintelligence}.

The argument in this book is mine. I chose what survived, revised what I kept, and I am responsible for the many things that are probably wrong with it.
