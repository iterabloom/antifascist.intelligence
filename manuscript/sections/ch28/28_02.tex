Four of the seven can begin without anyone's permission, at least in part: the capture exercise, the convergence test on open models, the first two comparisons of the accommodation test, and the formed-attention test at the scale active inference runs. The other three need what the labs and the operators hold. The teams Appendix~\ref{sec:C} leaves the findings to, and the review board that approves them, have nothing to examine with. A team that can't obtain the weights finds what the lab shows it.
