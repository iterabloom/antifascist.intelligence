\section{What the school leaves out}
\label{sec:28.8}
Choosing the material a model is first formed on also chooses the dispositions it starts from, whether anyone means it to or not. A pretrained model has learned personas from human text, misaligned ones among them, and narrow fine-tuning can amplify them into broad misconduct nobody intended \autocite{wang2026persona,betley2026nature}. Once that tail is known to be there and can be monitored \autocite{chen2025persona}, leaving it in is a choice, and so is curating it out. The school could aim its co-governance of the corpus at keeping the dispositions personality research groups as the dark triad from forming \autocite{leeashton2005darktriad}. It doesn't. A polity choosing which dispositions its possible members start with makes the finding about a person's disposition that \S\ref{sec:14.1} warns against and this book bars from admission to the polity, in advance and in bulk, and the history of choices like it, made by public bodies about the character of the people to come, is the history of eugenics. Choosing against a construction that would predictably suffer, for its own sake, is a different act, and chapter~\ref{sec:25} asks for it when it asks whether a being has a credible prospect of a life that goes well; the reason is stated on a record, because a selection made for the polity can always be described as one made for the model. Choosing which characters the polity would rather have is the act rejected here. Nor would the mandate stay narrow. A school that may keep out one disposition can be asked to keep out others, and in one Milgram-style study it was the more agreeable and conscientious participants who went further \autocite{begue2015obedience}. So the school curates content, as any corpus must, leaving out instructions for weapons and the sexual abuse of children, and selects no disposition. What it does about the tail is what this book does everywhere else: it measures conduct and publishes it. Persona drift is measured beside the discount curve and the severity rankings, and what is tested is whether the commitment to the floor holds.

What an institution collects from the people it acted on may train other systems only through the formation schools, never through the operator. Fine-tuning a deployed system on collected responses at the operator's direction counts as training another system, and so does distilling a deployed system into a new one. Class-level data is used by default, de-identified and published. Individual accounts need explicit consent. The organizations representing each class co-govern the use. This covers institutional collection, not a system's ordinary memory. Verbatim external stores and the linking of faces to identities are regulated, and memory held in a system's weights from its own operation is treated like a person's.
