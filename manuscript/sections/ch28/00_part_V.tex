\part{Who Forms Refusers}
\partintro{
Part~IV asked whether a machine could be in the room. This Part asks who keeps putting people in it. Every society that has had refusers formed them, unevenly and mostly by accident (chapter~\ref{ch:reproduced}), and the chapters here ask who owns the means of doing it now, and who it gets done for. The human case comes first: who owns the computation, and who counts as the public that would hold it. The machine design comes beside it: the public schools a model would be formed in, and the commons of refusers it would answer to.
}
