\section{What representation is for}
\label{sec:28.1}
It does two jobs. It protects: it lets a party contest what is done to it. And it includes: it lets a party take part in the public inquiry into problems it shares with others \autocite{dewey1927public}. The two come apart, and often have. Children, noncitizen residents and people under guardianship have the first without the second. The order matters for the same reason: protection first, participation later, the franchise last.
