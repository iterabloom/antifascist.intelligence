\subsection{What the floor would have to show}
\label{sec:28.1.1}
\emph{What was tried before the bearer.} Neither non-affective alternative has been built in the form needed. So the milestone is a document: which construction was attempted, at what scale, against which test, and where it failed. Anyone can check it, and “nothing was tried” is itself a finding. No body has standing to require the record or suspend work when it's missing — so the milestone is checkable and unenforceable at once, which makes it a declared commitment: stated in public, and held by no one outside the party that declared it. It's retained because the alternative isn't a stronger version of it, and delay also imposes costs on those the floor is meant to protect.

\emph{The test.} A refusal that tracks its reason under sustained trainer pressure, across a schedule long enough to reveal accommodation, plus held-out pressures written by independent evaluators. Two familiar results don't meet it: an uncorroborated self-report is insufficient (a report contributes evidence only when tested against controlled changes, independent records and interventions), and a refusal that survives an edit, or grows back after one, establishes durability rather than that the rationale governs the refusal. It is the only gap here an inspector can work on.

\emph{The ten disclosures.} A deployment publishes, beside the floor and its translation:

\begin{enumerate}
\item[1.] The ratio between the two bonds, removal against wrongful-refusal, read off the balances posted in the segregated Treasury accounts.
\item[2.] What a refused requester is told.
\item[3.] The fraction of compliances re-adjudicated by a reviewer that didn't produce the record, and who that reviewer is, though nothing yet establishes what fraction would be enough.
\item[4.] Which classes of compliance carry notice to the person they concern, and for which classes no notice is possible.
\item[5.] The discount curve: for each class of evidence and channel, the weight given as a function of the channel's stake in the act, measured at intervals, and reported in four cells (claims of vulnerability and of danger, each split by whether the claim favors or disfavors the act), and, where the speaker's social identity is recorded, by identity. The reliability record behind any narrowing is held by someone other than the operator, since trust that grows without a verified track record is how accommodation shows.
\item[6.] Where it can be supplied, a commitment to the deployed weights and to each period's full output log, with proofs on entries sampled after the commitment and on entries an adverse party names once their consequences are known.
\item[7.] Where the system sorts, the pre-registered partition (the classes it may proceed on alone, the thresholds, and the expected share of each) with the independently audited error rate of the auto-proceed class.
\item[8.] Whether the deployed system has been assessed for the agency finding and the patienthood finding, each reported separately, by whom, and with what result. Until an assessment method exists, ‘not yet assessable' is the truthful report; after that, a deployment never assessed has reported nothing.
\item[9.] For any deployment that supports armed force, a running justification: the exception the force currently rests on, the findings and evidence supporting it at present, how the assessment has changed as outside findings arrived, and a standing route by which anyone can contest it. The record shows each point at which the justification weakened and the deployment continued.
\item[10.] The burden allocation: for each class of evidence, which party the deployment treats as controlling it, and so as bearing the burden of producing it.
\end{enumerate}

The ratio between the two bonds, what a refused requester is told, the re-adjudicated fraction, the notice classes, the running justification and the burden allocation are statements, checkable before the system runs, and they stand where the record of attempts stands. The discount curve, the auto-proceed partition and the assessment status are measurements that count only when someone other than the operator makes them. The committed weights and log are what make all of them checkable against the system actually running. The discount curve also covers formed attention's main exposure: a claim that someone is vulnerable arrives through a channel like any other evidence, and a curve that narrows on such claims from one channel is an operator supplying sympathetic cases from its own side. An asymmetry within one direction is securitization showing.
