\part{Finding Out}
\partintro{
I have spent five Parts saying what the room needs, and I could be wrong about any of it. This Part says how you'd find out: who holds what finding out takes, and which results would show that a policy doesn't put in the room what it promises, or that a machine in it is only another stamp. It is not the end of the argument, and a reader who takes the Part after it for a supplement has the order backward. The last row of Table 29.1, no system ever meets a finding, is what Part~VII rests on. Everything there is owed only to a system found to answer for what it does, the agency finding, or found able to be harmed, the patienthood finding. The first rests on the test of chapter~\ref{sec:24}, the second first on the record of how the system was built (\S\ref{sec:30.3}). The tests come first because what could defeat the construction should be in front of you before you are asked into the lives it would support. What follows them is the argument continued under findings nobody has yet made, and the walls of Parts~II and~III stand whether or not anyone ever makes them.
}
