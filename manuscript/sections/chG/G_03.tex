\section{Taiwan}
\label{sec:G.3}
Taiwan is the hardest current application of the emergency licence, and it runs on the extended term: it needs standing to defend and corroborated defense. Its title is claimed by a government that has never administered it; the Republic of China has held no United Nations seat since 1971; the Council is vetoed and the Court unavailable. A test of title would leave the defender waiting on a corroborated licence that can't arrive, and would let the attacker's claim, discounted everywhere else in this term, decide the case. The administration rule (\S\ref{sec:5.2}) avoids both. The government in Taipei has administered Taiwan, Penghu, Kinmen and Matsu since 1949, and no disinterested body has found that hold an occupation, so an attack on them opens the emergency licence. Resolution 2758 seated a delegation and names no territory \autocite{unga1971res2758}. A claim that force against a population the claimant has never governed is domestic is the claimant's description, and is discounted as one, so the term engages for both governments and gives neither an emergency licence to take the territory. The domestic clause reaches the defender and its helpers alike: the Republic of China's constitution gives its legislature the decision on war \autocite{roc1947constitution}, and the Taiwan Relations Act leaves any American response to constitutional processes \autocite{tra1979}, so a deployment's published reading has to say whether each authorization exists. A blockade or a quarantine is another matter: it is a continuing wrong and not an attack under way, and it waits on a corroborated licence that for Taiwan won't come. Whether an attack on Taiwan continues is also a fact the defender controls the evidence about, and no disinterested body will say. The emergency licence's burden rule (\S\ref{sec:5.2}) still applies: the licence persists on corroboration from channels Taipei doesn't compose, third states' sensors and open-source imagery of the strait, and lapses without them. A deployment licensed on the defender's sensors alone has accepted Gleiwitz on this coast too.
