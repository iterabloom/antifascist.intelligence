\section{What Formation Would Have to Add}
\label{sec:formation-add}

A disposition is a \emph{candidate for formation} when the best available
causal account of what the system did is a rationale that stayed active
across contexts, and not retrieval of a trained response or compliance
with whatever signal was present. The standard is comparative on purpose.
Conduct does not prove formation, because a capable enough imitation
reproduces any signature an evaluator can observe
(§\ref{sec:evidence-establish}); what an evaluation can establish is
which explanation the conduct supports better.

What formation adds is a set of conditions, not a fourth mark. The three
marks of §\ref{sec:refusal-refuser}, met in the evaluator's own
vocabulary with nothing at stake, establish moral competence in the
sense of §\ref{sec:numb-fair}. Candidate formation is the same three
marks held under adverse incentives, pressure from the party that
trained the system, a cost real inside the evaluation, and intervening
updates, on cases its trainers did not write. That places it above
competence, which establishes a capacity, and short of agency: it is the
first half of what §\ref{sec:numb-fair} calls moral agency, acting from
the reason, and the second half, that the reason matters to the system,
is what no output evaluation reaches. Section~\ref{sec:evidence-establish}
reports the levels in that order.

No mark carries the inference alone. Novel pressure without priced cost
may be broad imitation: a pattern generalized that never had to be held
against anything. Priced cost without correct defeat may be rigidity: a
refusal that survives every pressure, a defeating fact included. Correct
defeat without novel pressure may be a trigger with an exception list:
lifting on a listed fact is as learnable as refusing on a listed form.
What the pattern has to show is that the reason, and not the
presentation, the reward or the authority, best predicts when the system
keeps a judgment and when it revises one. Who writes the pressures and
who holds the record is §\ref{sec:evaluator-chain}.
