\chapter{Policy, Law, and Accountability}
\label{sec:9}
Section~\ref{sec:8.2.1} names a limit chapter~\ref{sec:8} cannot repair from inside: no aggregation of public preference protects anybody from the aggregate. What does is a different kind of machinery — rules that bind whether or not they are popular, and somebody obliged to answer when they are broken. This chapter is that machinery, to the extent it exists.

First the frameworks — what national guidelines require, and what industry self-regulation enforces once honoring a commitment costs something. Then privacy and security, which is what holding data about people commits a system to. Then chapter~\ref{sec:2}'s unfinished questions about a sentient subject come due, and the chapter closes on what keeps any of this more than a policy document nobody enforces.
