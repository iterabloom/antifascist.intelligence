\section{Emotional Intelligence, Affective Computing, and Altruism}
\label{sec:9.2}
Section~\ref{sec:2.3.2} covered affective computing as an engineering field: what it means for a system to recognize and respond to emotion. This section covers what that field has not settled. Section~\ref{sec:9.2.1} takes up emotional recognition and expression research specifically, including the open dispute over whether emotion is legible from a face or a voice at all. Section~\ref{sec:9.2.2} turns to self-awareness and emotional regulation — terms that need narrowing before they mean anything a system could be tested against — and what either would contribute to resisting manipulation.
