\section{Accountability, Legal Responsibility, and Antifascism in Sentient Machine Experimentation}
\label{sec:9.3}
Sections~\ref{sec:2.4.1}, \ref{sec:2.4.4} and \ref{sec:2.4.5} did the definitional and procedural work this section builds on, and each ended by handing it a specific unfinished question: what makes a review board's ruling binding rather than advisory, what a court actually does with a system whose legal status is unsettled, and who answers for harm a sentient system causes or suffers. This section takes those three in turn, then closes on what keeps the apparatus more than a policy document nobody enforces.
