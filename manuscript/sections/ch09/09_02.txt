9.2. Emotional Intelligence, Affective Computing, and Altruism
Section 2.3.2 covered affective computing as an engineering field: what it means for a system to recognize and respond to emotion. This section covers what that field has not settled. Section 9.2.1 takes up emotional recognition and expression research specifically, including the open dispute over whether emotion is legible from a face or a voice at all. Section 9.2.2 turns to self-awareness and emotional regulation — terms that need narrowing before they mean anything a system could be tested against — and what either would actually contribute to resisting manipulation.
