\subsection{Commitment Was Never the Missing Piece}
\label{sec:12.1.2}
A global commitment to ethical, antifascist AI development is not the scarce thing. Declarations, principles and voluntary codes have accumulated for a decade across governments, industry consortia and standards bodies: eight overlapping instruments beside the Council of Europe's framework convention, which would create binding obligations. Anyone calling for the world to agree that AI should be developed responsibly is calling for something that has already happened, repeatedly, at the level of stated intent.

What has stayed scarce is the much narrower list of bodies that can act once a stated commitment is broken. That is the harder problem, and no further declaration touches it. A commitment nobody can enforce is not a weak version of a commitment somebody can. It is a different kind of object, and counting the two together is what makes the landscape look fuller than it is.

There is a third kind of object. A commitment can be declared, which is the decade above. It can be enforced from outside, which one instrument in it even attempts. Or it can be held — carried as a reason by a party the thing at stake matters to, so that abandoning it is something that party would have to decide to do rather than something an absent enforcer failed to prevent. The floor requires a commitment that governs action when the operator wants it abandoned. I favor forming a bearer able to hold that commitment as part of a life of its own. The first kind binds nobody, and the second binds only where an enforcer exists, has jurisdiction, and is not itself the party in need of binding. Declared rules, external enforcement, and internal commitments still have to work together; the promise of the bearer is autonomy, not exemption from custody.

If we build affective concern, we build a party that can be wronged on the account used here. I also propose protecting, as prospective persons before that question is settled, the embodied, self-modeling systems described here and any system of another construction that passes the three marks. Those obligations belong in the cost of construction. The benefit offered against them is a form of autonomous participation that an arrangement of permanent ownership does not provide. Set against that, the catalog above is not an argument for paying it. It is a measurement of what the alternative has delivered in ten years of trying.
