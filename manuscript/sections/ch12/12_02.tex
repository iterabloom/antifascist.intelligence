These are milestones for recognizing whether the project this book describes is working, and a method for revising them when they turn out not to have been the right milestones. One standard governs all of them: the milestone is the independently checked evidence, not the developer's account of having reached it.

Two of them come out of chapter~\ref{sec:3} and are stated first, one being the only milestone here whose failure carries an obligation rather than a score. The areas where an instrument already exists follow, and then how to tell whether a claimed milestone of any kind has been met.
