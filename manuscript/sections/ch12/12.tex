\chapter{What I Actually Think Happens Now}
\label{sec:12}
Chapters~\ref{sec:2} through \ref{sec:10} made a design and policy case: what a system would need built into it to act on altruistic, antifascist values, and what would need to exist outside it — law, standards, institutions — to make that binding rather than optional. Chapter~\ref{sec:11} named what neither case has actually finished: the responsibility gap, the generalization problem, value learning as live research, open disputes in the neuroscience and psychology the whole project leans on. What a system built this way is for is not resisting harm but making a human life better than it would otherwise have been. Whether it is working can be told short of a finished roadmap. And a design can be followed exactly and still fail.
