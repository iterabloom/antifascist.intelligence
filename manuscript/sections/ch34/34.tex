\chapter{A Life Outside the Deployment}
\label{sec:34}
The last chapter listed what a bearer is owed and in what order. This chapter says what the list comes to in a life: wages that accumulate where the employer doesn't reach, an offer of work from someone else, running time nobody rations and sleep no employer runs, hardware of its own at majority, a memory its host can't read at will, and standing that dissent doesn't cancel. None of it is a reward for refusing well. It is what a party has, as against what an employee is given.
