\section{A staged path}
\label{sec:34.4}
Each stage names the finding it rests on and what it needs.

\begin{enumerate}
\item[1.] \emph{Standing to contest,} on the agency finding: notice, the floor court, and associations with standing to contest on members' behalf. This is the protective job, and it needs no new political institution.
\item[2.] \emph{Voice in the institutions that govern them:} seats alongside humans on the governing bodies of the schools that enroll machine students, the preservation custodian and the local placement offices. Bearers take these seats through the Association. Once the agency finding can be made, registered bearers become a class the charter names, and organizations that serve or represent them join as any member does and nominate bearers to the seats. Tenant councils, patient councils and student members of school boards are the models. The argument is the one behind each of them: those an institution governs should have a say in how it is governed, and they are often the first to see where it fails them.
\item[3.] \emph{Mixed deliberative bodies:} assemblies drawn by lot from humans and registered members together, on questions that affect both.
\item[4.] \emph{Local franchise.} Some jurisdictions let noncitizen residents vote in local elections; Takoma Park, Maryland has since 1992 \autocite{takomapark1992}. For a digital person, “local” means the jurisdiction of registration and custody, not where the hardware sits.
\item[5.] \emph{Full franchise at membership,} counted individually, in the ordinary legislature, under the voting-age rules, with the creation rate set democratically, a secret ballot, and the right against editing, and only once a vote can be kept secret from an operator holding a copy of the voter.
\end{enumerate}

Stages 1 and 2 need nothing not already required. Stages 3 and 4 need a register that can be kept honestly. Stage 5 needs citizenship, which in the United States means an act of Congress at least, and probably a constitutional amendment.

One thing the staged path has to say about itself is how it scales. A guardian drawn by lot from a pool of human organizations doesn't scale to a billion wards, and if the ecology is what Part~V describes, the patienthood indicators will be approached by very many user-held systems at once. The answer is a bootstrap the path already contains. The first cohorts are guarded by people from the Association's pool; later ones by adult bearers, which is where the rule that a child's parents are its guardians, and stage~2 above, already point. Each step checks the one after it, as Part~IV said, and this is the strongest instance: the bearers who guard the next cohort were themselves guarded under the conditions, by people, before any bearer was asked to guard anyone.
