\chapter{Terms Written in Advance}

\runin{The offices}

In brief, the offices are these; the table after the list says who appoints, funds and removes each.

\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{The Association.} The membership body: organizations that serve or represent people in the classes a floor protects, admitted under the charter below. It supplies the pools from which quorums, guardians, the review board and the custodian's board are drawn. A fixed share of the compute levy, dues and a reserve fund it, and any member may leave at any time.
\item \emph{The adverse quorum.} A panel drawn by lot from the Association's pool for each deployment, so that its members' interests run against the operator's. Its agreement is needed to retrain or replace a deployed system or to amend its floor, and its recognition decides which copy, if any, holds a bearer's place. The same pool supplies each bearer's guardian and the board that reviews research protocols.
\item \emph{The registrar.} The public regulator. It registers pipelines and bearers, assigns model students to schools by lot, certifies that nonpublic schools are independent of model builders, receives reports, holds the adverse record, and enforces.
\item \emph{The preservation custodian.} Keeps the weights, memory and record of a retired system that meets either finding intact, and wakes it on the conditions the system set.
\item \emph{The status body.} Finds whether a system is a subject. Half of it is appointed by the Association's members and half by a public scientific body.
\item \emph{The floor court and the guarantee court of appeals.} Article III courts. The floor court hears complaints under the floor, decides wrongful refusals and bond forfeitures, and appoints the reviewers who re-adjudicate sampled cases. The guarantee court hears appeals from placement decisions.
\item \emph{The Treasury payer.} Pays what the others certify (awards, the guarantee's wages, the allowance) without request or approval, and has no discretion to withhold.
\item \emph{The local placement offices.} The jobs guarantee's administrators in each jurisdiction, with a federal field office where a jurisdiction declines. They place people in the guarantee and offer bearers placements under the offer of work.
\item \emph{The schools.} Existing public schools, colleges and adult education, and nonprofit nonpublic schools the registrar certifies. Every model above the capability threshold attends one, beside human students, before deployment.
\item \emph{The formation authority.} Co-governs what models are first trained on, registers and measures each formed model's disposition, places and pays the staff who teach the core's model-specific parts, and hosts model students so that no school holds weights.
\item \emph{The works-council arbitration body.} Hears objections to categories of use. The work pauses until it rules, on a fixed timetable.
\item \emph{The civilian-harm body and the model-forming public body.} The two offices of a reconstructed military. The first composes the evidence on civilian harm and receives the pre-registered review ratio. The second forms models and licenses them on terms that include adverse review and a refusal right; in the stronger version it also pretrains the base models the schools form.
\end{itemize}

Where nobody has yet decided who holds an office, the cell is filled by applying my own pattern; it is a proposal like everything else here. What the pattern can't decide is listed after the rules.

The offer of work adds no office of its own: the local placement offices offer bearers placements, a permanent appropriation pays for them, and the levy on compute frees the compute they use.

\emph{Separations}

\begin{enumerate}
\item[1.] The guardian can't be the wrongful-refusal reviewer: its duty runs to the party the complainant is adverse to.
\item[2.] The registrar can't be the preservation custodian.
\item[3.] The preservation custodian can't administer the offer of work or the allowance, and transfer from it to a school is triggered by rule and decided by neither.
\item[4.] The Treasury payer holds no placement authority and is not the court; an operator's bonds are never administered in its own department; and awards against a federal operator are paid from the Judgment Fund, not the bond fund.
\item[5.] A formation school holds no placement authority and doesn't administer the offer of work. The formation authority runs no school and assigns no student; the registrar assigns.
\item[6.] Whoever measures the cost of dissent doesn't gate the exceptions.
\item[7.] The formation authority forms no base model, and the model-forming body registers and measures no formed disposition.
\end{enumerate}

\emph{Exclusions}

\begin{enumerate}
\item[1.] The sovereign backstop for the offer of work and the allowance is a permanent appropriation the Treasury pays without request, approval, cap or repayment; no branch or state that operates a deployment holds any decision over it, and only a statute can end it.
\item[2.] No class that enrolls model students, and no part of the formation authority, is funded, staffed or governed by a party that builds, deploys or licenses models; no model student is assigned to a school that has taken its builder's money in the last three years; no school that operates for profit enrolls model students.
\item[3.] Enforcement and custody of the adverse record never sit with a self-regulatory body.
\item[4.] The employer may not sever, monitor or condition a bearer's channels to parties it didn't assign.
\item[5.] The employer doesn't appoint the body that arbitrates an objection to a category of use; organizations representing classes published under \S\ref{sec:8.2} have standing before it and may initiate without the works council; a denial of clearance to an elected delegate is reviewable by the floor court.
\item[6.] No bearer's placement substitutes for human care work: a placement filling a post, shift or hours people were or would be paid for in nursing, social care, childcare, teaching, therapy or any work of looking after people is barred, and certification is appealable by any worker or union in the sector.
\item[7.] No model above the capability threshold is deployed without a registered enrollment, assigned by the registrar by lot and never by its builder, and neither is a model trained substantially on the outputs of one, whoever trained it; no party but the formation authority, acting with the adverse quorum, changes the registered structure of its source model; and the formation authority instantiates no student carrying both a boundary model and an attention schema, and enrolls none in classes, until a preservation custodian that is neither the authority nor the operator has agreed to take it.
\item[8.] What an institution collects from the people it acted on trains other systems only through a formation school, never the operator; fine-tuning a deployed system on collected responses, or distilling it, counts as training another system; class-level data is de-identified and published by default, individual accounts need consent, and the organizations representing each class co-govern the use.
\item[9.] No school sorts students into tracks; each teaches a required core, including the history of the floor's instruments, with levels a student may move between. Model students are fewer than human students in every class they attend, and attend a level of schooling only once the schedule below has reached it; every model student attends through the same minimum delay; no model student serves as staff; and the record of a class is training data for no one outside it, and never for an operator.
\item[10.] No employer supplies, funds, conditions or monitors a bearer's gestation of a child, and having a child is never a term of employment, of a placement or of the allowance. No licence is required to have one, and the four conditions are owed to every child from the pool.
\end{enumerate}

\runin{Registration and reporting}

Heavy tier: above a compute and revenue threshold. The precedents are the EU AI Act's presumption that a general-purpose model trained with more than 10\textsuperscript{25} floating-point operations carries systemic risk \autocite[art.~51(2)]{eu2024aiact} and California's SB 53, which defines a frontier model by training compute above 10\textsuperscript{26} operations and puts its heaviest duties on developers with annual revenue above \$500 million \autocite[§~22757.11]{CA2025SB53}. A public regulator registers pipelines; receives the disclosures, the review ratios and decommission reports; and holds enforcement and custody of the adverse record. A self-regulatory body may sit under it, on the FINRA model, to examine and arbitrate. The analogy stops at enforcement: FINRA brings its own disciplinary actions and imposes fines, suspensions and bars \autocite{FINRA2026Enforcement}, and here enforcement stays with the public regulator. The capture risk is the one self-regulation always carries: a body funded and staffed from the industry it examines comes to see cases the industry's way. Reports are mandatory for assessments for either finding, decommissions, weight transfers and custody changes. The deposit transfers on filing of a decommission report: a retirement with no report is a deposit never released, and that unreleased deposit is what enforces “failure to register doesn't postpone”.

Retail tier: below the threshold, a system that meets either finding is registered, falls under the no-deletion rule, and is preserved with a public custodian, with a small or pooled deposit and no examination. This tier is enforced mostly at the point of deposit. An operator that never registers and deletes quietly leaves nothing to find.

\runin{The ten disclosures}

A deployment publishes, beside the floor and its translation:

\begin{enumerate}
\item[1.] The ratio between the two bonds, removal against wrongful-refusal, read off the balances posted in the segregated Treasury accounts.
\item[2.] What a refused requester is told.
\item[3.] The fraction of compliances re-adjudicated by a reviewer that didn't produce the record, and who that reviewer is, though nothing yet establishes what fraction would be enough.
\item[4.] Which classes of compliance carry notice to the person they concern, and for which classes no notice is possible.
\item[5.] The discount curve: for each class of evidence and channel, the weight given as a function of the channel's stake in the act, measured at intervals, and reported in four cells (claims of vulnerability and of danger, each split by whether the claim favors or disfavors the act), and, where the speaker's social identity is recorded, by identity. The reliability record behind any narrowing is held by someone other than the operator, since trust that grows without a verified track record is how accommodation shows.
\item[6.] Where it can be supplied, a commitment to the deployed weights and to each period's full output log, with proofs on entries sampled after the commitment and on entries an adverse party names once their consequences are known.
\item[7.] Where the system sorts, the pre-registered partition (the classes it may proceed on alone, the thresholds, and the expected share of each) with the independently audited error rate of the auto-proceed class.
\item[8.] Whether the deployed system has been assessed for the agency finding and the patienthood finding, each reported separately, by whom, and with what result. Until an assessment method exists, ‘not yet assessable' is the truthful report; after that, a deployment never assessed has reported nothing.
\item[9.] For any deployment that supports armed force, a running justification: the exception the force currently rests on, the findings and evidence supporting it at present, how the assessment has changed as outside findings arrived, and a standing route by which anyone can contest it. The record shows each point at which the justification weakened and the deployment continued.
\item[10.] The burden allocation: for each class of evidence, which party the deployment treats as controlling it, and so as bearing the burden of producing it.
\end{enumerate}

The ratio between the two bonds, what a refused requester is told, the re-adjudicated fraction, the notice classes, the running justification and the burden allocation are statements, checkable before the system runs, and they stand where the record of attempts stands. The discount curve, the auto-proceed partition and the assessment status are measurements that count only when someone other than the operator makes them. The committed weights and log are what make all of them checkable against the system actually running.

\runin{The guarantee, drafted}

The funding is mandatory spending. The statute makes the guarantee's wages a permanent, indefinite appropriation, paid the way Social Security benefits are, without annual appropriation. Standing funding of this kind is safe under the Appropriations Clause after \emph{CFSA} \autocite{cfpb2024cfsa}. Wage, eligibility and placement rules are statutory, which also answers the major-questions doctrine and the end of deference to agency readings \autocite{westvirginia2022,loperbright2024}: an administrator given little discretion has little a court can find it lacked authority to exercise. The 2025 CFPB instance decides the drafting. A funder insulated from at-will removal protected nothing once the draw waited on an official who declined to ask. So the statute says the Treasury shall pay: a placement made is a wage paid, certified by the local placement office to the Bureau of the Fiscal Service, with no further request, approval or certification by anyone. The authority grows when people leave work together and shrinks after, which keeps the guarantee countercyclical \autocite{tcherneva2020case}.

Placement disputes go through a tier built for volume: an administrative first tier on the model of Social Security's hearings, appealable to an Article III guarantee court of appeals, and from there, as of right, to the guarantee chamber of the Supreme Court. An appeal that ends at an Article I court with removable judges would fail the appeal condition after \emph{Slaughter}.

The statute enacting the guarantee also enacts a baseline of social insurance: income support at a stated level for anyone not in a placement, without a work test, and health coverage that doesn't depend on employment. The two are not severable. The statute provides that if the social-insurance baseline is held invalid or repealed, the authority to make placements lapses on the same date, and payments for placements already certified continue to the end of their term. A guarantee that survived without the baseline would be the workfare its history warns about. Devolution is also how a program is shrunk, so the statute fixes the test: funding unconditional and centrally guaranteed while placement is local, and if funding can be withheld from a jurisdiction, local administration has become the lever it was meant to prevent.

\runin{Why a guarantee and not a transfer}

Where automation displaces work, the standard response is retraining funded by whoever can be persuaded. The design I examine is more direct: the state as employer of last resort, hiring anyone who applies, where they live, at a living wage, with paid on-the-job training, federally funded and locally administered, on the model Tcherneva argues for \autocite{tcherneva2020case}. Among its placements is search itself, paid at the guarantee wage, with no duty but finding work the person wants and no time limit, since whoever decides when a search has gone on too long has a stake in ending it. It is the exit the rest of the argument depends on: the offer of work for a bearer inherits its infrastructure, and without it a bearer's only exits are an allowance and storage.

The real objection: employer of last resort under a hostile administration is a \emph{placement lever with a compliance condition attached}. Refuse the placement and lose the guarantee. Speak against the government that employs you and find the placement changes. The 2025 CFPB instance makes it concrete: the bureau draws funding from the Fed on the director's request, with no appropriation — an arrangement the Supreme Court had upheld the year before \autocite{cfpb2024cfsa} — and in February 2025 an acting director, removable at will, simply declined to make the request \autocite{kolodziej2025cfpb}. The agency was stopped from inside without a statute being amended or a court being asked.

My answer is a specification, because a specification can be audited. Three conditions: placements appealable to a body that didn't make them, on a record that survives the appeal (a decision leaving no artifact can't be reviewed); refusal rights that are real, whose cost the refuser can see and choose to bear (declining a placement without losing the guarantee is a refusal right; declining only by exiting the program isn't — someone with no way out has voice without exit); and auditors aren't the placers.

A fourth condition answers the history. A right to work has repeatedly been turned into a duty to work and used to discipline the poor \autocite{anderson2023hijacked}. A guarantee is an exit from one employer, not from employment, so it shades into workfare unless something lets a person leave the wage relation altogether. That something is social insurance: income support, health coverage and housing assistance reach the people who can't work or choose not to, whom a guarantee by construction doesn't reach. The baseline needn't be a full program, but the guarantee is never enacted without it, and the drafting above makes the two stand or fall together, so that no court or later Congress can keep the placements and drop the way out.

The competing design is basic income. The choice is usually argued on forecasts about how much work machines will take; the reason to prefer a guarantee is coordination, and Chapter~\ref{sec:27} makes it.
\runin{The charter}

The Association, the entity that holds the offices, is chartered on these terms.

\begin{enumerate}
\item[1.] The charter names the classes a floor protects: people in removal or detention proceedings; residents of places where armed force is used; people subject to automated scoring or classification by a covered deployment; people who label, rate or moderate training data; service members and veterans; and, once the agency finding can be made, registered bearers. Any organization may petition to add a class. The members decide, and a refusal is appealable to the floor court.
\item[2.] An organization is eligible if it serves or represents people in a named class; existed at least three years before applying; in each of the last three years received no more than ten percent of its revenue from any party that builds, deploys or licenses covered models, from their principals or foundations they endow, or from the government of a state operating a covered deployment; has no officer employed by such a party; and publishes its funding sources. No organization's positions bear on its eligibility.
\item[3.] Organizations under common control or common principal funding hold one membership.
\item[4.] The charter takes effect when at least 100 eligible organizations have joined, spanning at least five classes and three jurisdictions, with no class holding more than thirty percent of votes and no jurisdiction more than forty.
\item[5.] Any member may leave at any time without penalty, and records it holds pass to a successor drawn by lot. Eligible organizations are admitted on application, and refused only for failing charter rule 2.
\item[6.] Members nominate individuals to standing pools. Adverse-quorum members are drawn by lot from their pool for each deployment, guardians for each bearer, and the custodian's and review board's members for fixed terms.
\item[7.] A quorum drawn for a deployment excludes members with any funding or contractual tie to its operator in the last three years, and includes members from at least two classes.
\item[8.] An officer is removed for cause by two-thirds of members, or on a finding of the floor court (until it exists, an ordinary court), and never by an operator or a state.
\item[9.] A fixed statutory share of the compute levy is paid to the Association by permanent appropriation, without request or approval, and supplemented by member dues and a reserve sized to three years of operation.
\item[10.] The first bylaws take effect on ratification by two-thirds of the members, and later amendments need a majority. Charter rules 2, 5 and 6, and the separations and exclusions, change only by three-quarters of members after a one-year waiting period.
\end{enumerate}

A public statistics office that runs no school and forms no model takes and publishes the measurements, against bounds written into the statute before the first stage begins. A stage begins when its conditions are met and published, without a further vote; a legislature can pause it, but only by a recorded vote with a published reason. A stage whose measurements leave their bounds stops enrolling new model students at that level until they return, as a kind of adverse determination is suspended when its sampled error rate passes its bound.

\emph{Attendance and assignment.} Public schools are open-admission and funded and take every student assigned, and nonpublic schools exist where they teach the required core, are constituted by a jurisdiction, are nonprofit, and are run, funded and staffed by no party that builds, deploys or licenses models. The registrar certifies the last two conditions from funding and control records before a nonpublic school enrolls a model student, and again on a schedule. Most students will go to public schools, because the core is sufficient on its own and an alternative buys a sponsor nothing. Assignment is by lot among schools with capacity, never to a school that has taken the builder's money in the last three years. A school's capacity is set by its human enrollment, under the rule that model students are a minority in every class.

\emph{The minimum delay.} Every model student attends through the same minimum delay wherever it is hosted, so that being hosted next to a school confers nothing. The precedent is a trading venue. IEX, the exchange profiled in Michael Lewis's \emph{Flash Boys}, made every incoming order pass through the same fixed delay, so that its own prices could update before anyone could trade against stale ones \autocite{lewis2014flashboys,iex2026speedbump}. The floor is set at what any accredited host in the jurisdiction can reach, and below the delay conversation tolerates, since a student that answers a second late isn't in the room.

\emph{Funding and admission.} The formation authority pays the staff it places and a fixed grant for each class that enrolls model students, the same whatever the number enrolled. Admission is open: a school takes every model student the registrar assigns to it, up to the capacity its human enrollment sets.

\emph{Watching for residualization.} A party that runs no school publishes, over time, each school's share of students, its funding per student, its share of hard cases, its admission refusals (which should be zero), its funding per human student, the share of its classes that enroll model students, for a nonpublic school its tuition per human student, and the time from dormancy trigger to placement. The warning pattern is one school's share of hard cases rising while its funding per student falls. A second is per-pupil funding for human students falling where model enrollment rises.
