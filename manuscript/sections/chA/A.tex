\chapter{The Instruments, Translated}
\label{sec:A}
The floor takes its instruments entire, and a deployment translates them into terms for its own pipeline (Chapter~\ref{sec:5}). This appendix holds the translations the book runs: the Maven pipeline (\S\ref{sec:A.1}), the four Geneva Conventions and Additional Protocol I for that pipeline (\S\ref{sec:A.2}), and the abduction and forcible transfer pipeline, with the two tables chapter~\ref{sec:11} reads against it, the drafts that would end the agency and the money it runs on (\S\ref{sec:A.3}).
