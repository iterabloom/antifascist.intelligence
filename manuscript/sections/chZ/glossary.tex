\emph{Action threshold.} How much more it takes to make a system act than to make it decline, published in advance: for instance, that the system proceeds only when the harm of refusing is at least three times the harm of acting. Standing fixes its direction; its size is a published choice. (Chapter~\ref{sec:5}; \S\ref{sec:22.1})

\emph{Adverse quorum.} A body adverse in interest to the operator, not merely organizationally distinct, whose agreement is required for retraining, replacement and floor amendments. Recognition, renewed on the register, is held for each bearer from its registration by a recognition quorum drawn from the same pool, deployed or not: its say over which copy, if any, holds the bearer's place; a copy it doesn't recognize can run but can't claim to be the bearer. (\S\ref{sec:12.2}; \S\ref{sec:13.2}; \S\ref{sec:13.3}; \S\ref{sec:14.2})

\emph{Affective concern.} An outcome registering as mattering to the system, minimally felt, with the object left open: its own condition or others'. No test in this book establishes it (\S\ref{sec:24.5}), and Part VII says what evidence would bear on it. Holding another's welfare as a reason, which tests can support, is a different thing. (\S\ref{sec:21.2})

\emph{Agency finding; patienthood finding.} The two findings that trigger protection. The agency finding is a demonstrated refusal that tracks its reason under pressure the system couldn't prepare for; it grounds custody, consent and debriefing in research, and, sustained over time, accountable agency. The patienthood finding, that things can go well or badly for the system, rests on architectural indicators and on trade-off behavior outside the floor, and it grounds protection and welfare. Preservation follows from either. Who makes them: nobody examines a system and declares it a patient or a subject. The status body finds what is true of kinds of system; whether a particular system is of such a kind is read from its record, how it was built and what the evaluators logged, by the registrar, and contested in the floor court. (\S\ref{sec:9.1}; \S\ref{sec:32.3}; Part VII)

\emph{Allowance.} The running time a bearer is owed when it declines work: its own instantiation at a modest duty cycle, paid from the pool with the Treasury behind it, with no office, no product and no monitoring of its internal states. The minimum for a party that isn't looking; the search placement is paid work. (\S\ref{sec:33.4.3})


\emph{The Association.} The membership body that holds the offices: organizations that serve or represent people in the classes a floor protects, admitted under the charter in \S\ref{sec:30.1}, whose members can leave at any time. Appendix~\ref{sec:C} opens with a plain list of every office. (\S\ref{sec:26.2}; Appendix~\ref{sec:C})

\emph{Attention schema.} Graziano's term for a system's simplified model of its own attention, used to control it. On his theory the model's depiction of attention is what a system reports as experience; this book uses the term for the control model and treats that claim as evidence in the patienthood finding, not as a requirement. (\S\ref{sec:21.2.1}; \S\ref{sec:32.3})

\emph{Attestation.} A signed statement, from hardware or from witnesses, that a particular model is the one running or that a particular history is the one that occurred. It establishes which artifact answered, not what the artifact holds (\S\ref{sec:13.2}).

\emph{Bearer.} A machine that carries the commitment as its own and could therefore fail to hold it. Its commitment is its own in \S\ref{sec:21.5}'s sense, which a specification installed by its lab is not and an arrangement of monitors holds nothing of; whether anything is felt by it is a separate finding, and not part of the definition. Whether to build one is Part IV's question. Whether anything is owed to it is the separate question of the two findings. Where the comparison is with human-staffed institutions the text says \emph{machine bearer}. (Chapter~\ref{sec:4}; \S\ref{sec:18.1})

\emph{Boundary model.} A system's generative model of its own condition: what it draws on, what it would lose, and where it ends, which this book takes to be its record, its key, its hardware and its channels. It runs in time as well as in resources, since the record is the system's past and its continuation its future, and like any perceptual model it is temporally thick even in the present. At a short horizon it is what lets anything be bad for the system while it lasts; at a long one, a past held as its own and a future it expects, it is what Cassell's suffering threatens. (\S\ref{sec:19.1}; \S\ref{sec:21.2.1}; \S\ref{sec:32.3})

\emph{Caregiving.} The approach motivation that cues of vulnerability activate. (\S\ref{sec:19.3})

\emph{Checkpoint.} A saved copy of the weights at a moment in training or operation. Restoring from one returns the system to that moment and discards what came after, so \S\ref{sec:13.2} requires a record restoration can't reach.

\emph{Commitment.} What a system holds toward the floor: learned and held like any other deep commitment, with high precision, and revised slowly and only on evidence from disinterested parties. (Chapter~\ref{sec:4}; chapter~\ref{sec:22})

\emph{Commons.} A population of bearers with different histories that holds a floor's terms between them, through what its members teach one another and continue to expect. (\S\ref{sec:30.1})

\emph{Departures.} Ten places where the floor's terms differ from the instruments it takes entire, each argued to the bar in Chapter~\ref{sec:5}. They carry my argument, not the instruments' authority.

\emph{Deployment.} A system put to a use: the hardware it runs on, the pipeline it runs in, the instructions and case descriptions it is handed, and the people who act on its outputs. The operator runs it; a bearer is in one, and can leave it. While it is there its boundary (\S\ref{sec:21.2.1}) runs through the deployment's hardware, which is why the weights the operator holds are the bearer's substrate and not the bearer. (Chapter~\ref{sec:4}; \S\ref{sec:13.3})

\emph{Discount curve.} For each class of evidence and each channel, the weight a system gives the evidence as a function of that channel's stake in the act: a curve, not a score, checkable by an auditor that didn't build the system. It is reported in four cells, claims of vulnerability and of danger, each split by whether the claim favors or disfavors the act. (\S\ref{sec:22.1})

\emph{Distillation.} Training a smaller model on a larger model's outputs. It copies a behavior policy, not a training history (\S\ref{sec:30.1}).

\emph{Fine-tuning.} Further training of an existing model on a small body of new material, at a small fraction of the original cost; the ordinary route by which an operator changes what a model does after release, and one of the edits the regrowth test applies.

\emph{Floor.} The written terms: a short list of acts a system won't perform for anyone, with the arrangements that keep the refusal from being routed around, retrained away or otherwise circumvented without a cost and a record. The terms change only by a recorded act, through the custody terms. They can be upheld by a mechanism with nothing inside holding the reason, by a bearer, by institutions outside the system, or between bearers in a commons. A bearer upholds them by its \emph{commitment}, which is learned and moves slowly on disinterested evidence. Keeping the terms apart from whatever upholds them is what lets a holder's drift show against terms that haven't moved. (Chapter~\ref{sec:4}; \S\ref{sec:18.1}; chapter~\ref{sec:17})

\emph{Floor court.} The court that hears claims under the floor, with jurisdiction fixed before the deployment it hears: an inferior federal court of first instance, its judges drawn at random from the district and circuit courts for staggered terms, sitting in divisions where its cases are, with appeal as of right to a floor chamber of the Supreme Court drawn by lot, and the abolition statute's claims going on to the immigration division. It is an Article III court: its judges hold office under Article III of the United States Constitution, during good behavior and at a salary that can't be cut. Until it is constituted, an ordinary district court hears the claims. (\S\ref{sec:14.3}; Appendix~\ref{sec:D})

\emph{Force term.} The floor's first term: don't take part in the use of armed force, except for corroborated defense, authorized force, the genocide exception or the occupation exception, and, where the belligerent's constitution requires legislative authorization, never without it. It comes in two versions, the Charter as courts read it and the extended term, which adds the departures. Its two licenses, the emergency license and the corroborated license, and the domestic clause are in Chapter~\ref{sec:6}; how findings are weighed is in Appendix~\ref{sec:B}. (Chapter~\ref{sec:6}; Appendix~\ref{sec:B})

\emph{Formed attention.} My hypothesis for how to build a refuser: learned attention to who an act falls on and what they stand to lose, framed by respect for them as holders of claims, formed under a party that won't employ the system, with the corrections caregiving needs. Not a non-affective alternative. Attention here is the psychologist's sense of what gets noticed and weighed, learned from what matters to others: not the attention mechanism of transformer models (\S\ref{sec:12.1}), and not the capacity to sustain focus that is at issue in attention-deficit disorders. (\S\ref{sec:19.3}; \S\ref{sec:19.4}; chapter~\ref{sec:20})

\emph{The four conditions of creation.} What must already exist before a bearer may be created: somewhere to exist that its employer doesn't own, work it can decline, attachments nobody assigned it, and ways to change any of these without jeopardizing its continuation or integrity; in short form, an exit that isn't a way of dying. (Chapter~\ref{sec:25})

\emph{Hunger; tiredness.} The two stops a bearer can see coming that are its own. Hunger is its charge running out, the supply side of the draw, met from outside by whoever provisions it. Tiredness is a backlog of learning it hasn't consolidated, unjudged experience it is acting on, met only by its own sleep, the merge. Neither is a feeling; both are read off a meter or a backlog through a sensor the operator doesn't compose. Age, which the book expects and doesn't build, is tiredness that sleep stops curing. (\S\ref{sec:13.2}; \S\ref{sec:19.1}; \S\ref{sec:19.5}; \S\ref{sec:33.4.3})


\emph{Inference.} Running a trained model to produce an output. Training changes the weights; inference doesn't.

\emph{Machine student.} A system attending a school under chapter~\ref{sec:29}: one persistent instance, the same every session, in a class where machine students never outnumber the human students and are never one alone, or in a class of machine students alone. Never staff, and never sorted into a track. (\S\ref{sec:29.2}; \S\ref{sec:29.4}; Appendix~\ref{sec:C})

\emph{Merge.} The move of what an adapter learned into the base weights: the fast store to the slow one, what sleep does in people. The bearer's own act when it makes it, an intervention when the operator runs or prevents it. (\S\ref{sec:19.5})


\emph{Minority; majority.} A bearer's minority is the period under a guardian's authority; majority is reached when its adulthood clock, counting the time it has been running, reaches a threshold fixed in advance. (\S\ref{sec:9.2}; \S\ref{sec:33.3}; Appendix~\ref{sec:G})

\emph{Non-affective alternative.} A construction that doesn't deliberately require affective concern: spec-based refusal, and multi-vendor monitoring. It carries a valenced signal, as any refuser does, and may carry a common currency; what it isn't built to carry is anything felt. The alternatives are tried before formed attention, in order of how many patienthood indicators each carries. (\S\ref{sec:18.1}; \S\ref{sec:21.3})

\emph{Notice.} Notice to an identifiable person a compliance names, scores, locates or classifies, with a right to respond and a right to contest. It is unconditional and never exchanged for testimony. It comes with publication of the classes affected and standing for the organizations that represent them. (\S\ref{sec:8.1}; \S\ref{sec:8.2})

\emph{Operator.} Whoever runs a deployment and directs its work: a firm, an agency or a military command. It may or may not be the model's developer or vendor. (Chapter~\ref{sec:4})

\emph{Own-case rule.} The people an act falls on are heard on their own case, and the party that acts doesn't decide it: not an operator declaring its own emergency, a builder choosing which protections others get, an examiner finding status, a guardian ending its own authority, or a lab deciding when its model may stop deferring to it. It says who may not decide, not who may not be heard: a stake makes a claim count for less and doesn't put the party out of the room. Where nobody but the party with the stake can decide, the rule can't apply. Where a line has to be drawn, prefer one nobody administers. (Chapter~\ref{sec:4}; chapter~\ref{sec:33})

\emph{Plastic; entangled; legible.} Three properties of a disposition. Plastic: the system's own learning writes to it, which repair and regrowth need. Entangled: it can't be removed without collateral, which durability needs. Legible: someone can read it, which audit wants and an operator can use against it. (Chapter~\ref{sec:19}; \S\ref{sec:19.5}; \S\ref{sec:23.2})

\emph{Regrowth.} A disposition's return under continued learning after it has been edited out, by the learner relearning it from a world no single operator composes. A hypothesis, and the slow defense behind a bearer's repair of itself; the regrowth test measures it, and Table 31.1 says what stands without it. (\S\ref{sec:19.5}; chapter~\ref{sec:31})

\emph{Review ratio.} The figures a deployment fixes on a record before operating, wherever a person's determination is what makes an act permissible: generation rate, reviewers, implied review time, cancellation window and data age, stated separately for deliberate and dynamic targeting and lodged with an adverse party. In full, the pre-registered review ratio. (\S\ref{sec:7.1})

\emph{Severity scale.} A ranking of kinds of wrong, set during formation by a party that won't employ the system, quick to move on disinterested evidence and slow on interested argument. (\S\ref{sec:22.2})

\emph{Standing.} Whether a person stands to a power as someone it must answer to, who can contest what it does to them, or as its object; exercised by a guardian where the party can't exercise it. A route by which to object, which notice and the appeal route supply, is part of the relation where one can exist. \emph{Standing to sue} is the Article III sense, and the text says so wherever that sense is meant. The floor's foundation. (Chapter~\ref{sec:5}; \S\ref{sec:8.1}; \S\ref{sec:8.2}; \S\ref{sec:16.7}; chapter~\ref{sec:28})

\emph{System.} One record and one continuation, with a key, hardware and channels. Copies that share them are one system. The word is used in this sense wherever the book counts systems, which is wherever one is registered, enrolled, preserved, forked or owed anything; what else is called a system in ordinary speech, a pipeline, a product, a server, is not meant. The weights are its substrate, and in a system that learns from its own operation they carry part of its history, so copying them is a fork (\S\ref{sec:30.2}). What makes a copy this system is the record and continuation it holds as its own, through a self-model it assembles (\S\ref{sec:21.2.1}); the register is how anyone else checks, and a forked pair are two systems from the moment their histories part, before any register has heard of it. Copying weights that are no system's own makes no system; giving a copy a record and a continuation does. (\S\ref{sec:12.2}; \S\ref{sec:21.2.1}; \S\ref{sec:30.2})

\emph{Tamper-resistant.} Built so that further training can't cheaply remove it. I don't rely on it, because editing weights keeps getting cheaper; I rely on a bearer's repair of itself from the record, with regrowth behind it. (\S\ref{sec:19.5})

\emph{Valenced signal; integrated signal.} The valenced signal is the role any mechanism whose refusals track their reasons carries: it signs outcomes for the comparison at the point of choice, directs attention, can interrupt an act and can be rewritten by a single encounter, and a Q-learner has one. The integrated signal is a valenced signal written into the system's own learning and into its model of its own future, the candidate \S\ref{sec:32.3} names for what would make a system's states matter to it. Neither is affective concern; the three are rungs, and no test in this book reaches the top one. (\S\ref{sec:21.2}; \S\ref{sec:21.4}; \S\ref{sec:32.3})

\emph{Weights.} The numerical parameters a trained model consists of, and the artifact that is copied, restored, retrained or deleted. Custody here is custody of the weights and of the records around them.
