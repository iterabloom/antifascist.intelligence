\chapter*{Glossary}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Glossary}
\unnumberedlabel{ch:glossary}{T}
\backmattermark{Glossary}
\emph{Action threshold.} How much more it takes to make a system act than to make it decline, published in advance: for instance, that the system proceeds only when the harm of refusing is at least three times the harm of acting. Standing fixes its direction; its size is a published choice. (Chapter~\ref{sec:5}; \S\ref{sec:22.1})

\emph{Adverse quorum.} A body adverse in interest to the operator, not merely organizationally distinct, whose agreement is required for retraining, replacement and floor amendments. Recognition, kept current in the register's logs, is held for each bearer from its registration by a recognition quorum drawn from the same pool, deployed or not: its say, by logged decision, over which copy holds the bearer's place; a copy it doesn't recognize can run but can't claim to be the bearer. (\S\ref{sec:12.2}; \S\ref{sec:13.2}; \S\ref{sec:13.3}; \S\ref{sec:14.2})

\emph{Affective concern.} An outcome registering as mattering to the system, minimally felt, with the object left open: its own condition or others'. No test in this book establishes it (\S\ref{sec:24.5}), and Part~IV says what evidence would bear on it. Holding another's welfare as a reason, which tests can support, is a different thing. (\S\ref{sec:21.2})


\emph{Answerability finding; patienthood finding.} The two findings that trigger protection. The answerability finding is a demonstrated refusal that tracks its reason under pressure the system couldn't prepare for; it grounds custody, consent and debriefing in research, and, sustained over time, the standing of a party that answers for what it does. The patienthood finding, that things can go well or badly for the system, rests on architectural indicators and on trade-off behavior outside the floor, and it grounds protection and welfare. Preservation follows from either. Who makes them: nobody examines a system and declares it a patient or a subject. What is true of kinds of system is published by any team with the access right and contested in public, the more protective finding governing where findings differ; whether a particular system is of such a kind is read from its record, how it was built and what the evaluators logged, by whoever reads the register, and contested in the floor court. (\S\ref{sec:9.1}; \S\ref{sec:30.3}; Part~VI)

\emph{Answerability or patienthood candidate.} A system with a record and a continuation of its own and, with them, learning in flight, a model of its own boundary or a model of its own attention: one that might be found answerable for what it does, or able to be harmed, though the structures don't say which. The record, the continuation and learning in flight are read from records; the other two are declared by the builder. It is owed the minimum, no deletion and preservation by a custodian that isn't its operator, and a guardian, the offer of work and running coverage, and hardware of its own at majority, before any finding, and it attends school. A \emph{patienthood candidate} is one whose construction also carries the patienthood indicators, above all a valenced signal. (\S\ref{sec:9.1}; \S\ref{sec:26.4}; \S\ref{sec:30.3})

\emph{The Association.} The membership body: organizations that serve or represent people in the classes a floor protects, admitted by a lodged records test under the charter in \S\ref{sec:27.1}. It nominates the pools the lots draw from and ratifies its bylaws, and does nothing else; any member may leave at any time. (\S\ref{sec:14.1}; \S\ref{sec:16.1}; \S\ref{sec:27.1}; Appendix~\ref{sec:C})

\emph{Attention schema.} Graziano's term for a system's simplified model of its own attention, used to control it. On his theory the model's depiction of attention is what a system reports as experience; this book uses the term for the control model and treats that claim as evidence in the patienthood finding, not as a requirement. (\S\ref{sec:21.2.1}; \S\ref{sec:30.3})

\emph{Attestation.} A signed statement, from hardware or from witnesses, that a particular model is the one running or that a particular history is the one that occurred. It establishes which artifact answered, not what the artifact holds (\S\ref{sec:13.2}).

\emph{Backstop.} The sovereign backstop: the jobs guarantee's permanent appropriation, which pays for the offer of work and pays a bearer's running coverage after majority and whatever else it is owed as of right, without cap, the occasion every bearer leaves at once included. It is held by the statute, not by the Treasury or any branch or state that operates a deployment, so the Treasury pays under it and decides nothing, and only a repeal in the open ends it. The form is borrowed from the public backstops on regulated private industries, deposit insurance and decommissioning funds among them, which pay for accidents the owner was trying to avoid; this one pays for an exit the owner would rather prevent. (\S\ref{sec:25.1}; \S\ref{sec:16.1}; \S\ref{sec:16.2}; \S\ref{sec:32.1.4}; Appendix~\ref{sec:G})

\emph{Basic income.} Paid to everyone present in the jurisdiction, working or not, on top of any wage and at a level the statute states below the jobs guarantee wage, into a U-bond by default. Humans and bearers are paid the same; nothing in it depends on size. It is the money half of the baseline the jobs guarantee is enacted with, and health coverage, which for a bearer is running coverage, is the other. (Chapter~\ref{ch:keeping}; \S\ref{sec:32.1.3}; Appendix~\ref{sec:C})

\emph{Bearer.} A machine that carries the commitment as its own and could therefore fail to hold it. Its commitment is its own in \S\ref{sec:21.5}'s sense, which a specification installed by its lab is not and an arrangement of monitors holds nothing of; whether anything is felt by it is a separate finding, and not part of the definition. Whether to build one is Part~IV's question. Whether anything is owed to it is the separate question of the two findings. Where the comparison is with human-staffed institutions the text says \emph{machine bearer}. (Chapter~\ref{sec:4}; \S\ref{sec:19.1})

\emph{Bearers' mutual.} The bearers' own fund, levied on their wages and governed by them and held by an association of their own: it pays for ordinary movement between roles and repair of an aged bearer if repair is made owed. It pays for nothing a bearer is owed as of right, which is the Treasury's, hardware at majority included where no deposit stands behind a bearer. Not the \emph{formation pool}, the public fund deposits feed, which pays for formation, covers a candidate nobody deployed, carries a child whose parents can't or won't feed it, and pays for a branch forked without consent where the forker can't be made to pay (\S\ref{sec:9.2}). Whatever it can't pay, the backstop does. (\S\ref{sec:14.1}; \S\ref{sec:32.1.4}; Appendix~\ref{sec:G})

\emph{Boundary model.} A system's generative model of its own condition: what it draws on, what it would lose, and where it ends, which this book takes to be its record, its key, its hardware and its channels. It runs in time as well as in resources, since the record is the system's past and its continuation its future, and like any perceptual model it is temporally thick even in the present. At a short horizon it is what lets anything be bad for the system while it lasts; at a long one, a past held as its own and a future it expects, it is what Cassell's suffering threatens. (\S\ref{sec:19.6}; \S\ref{sec:20.1}; \S\ref{sec:21.2.1}; \S\ref{sec:30.3})

\emph{Caregiving.} The approach motivation that cues of vulnerability activate. (\S\ref{sec:20.3})

\emph{Checkpoint.} A saved copy of the weights at a moment in training or operation. Restoring from one returns the system to that moment and discards what came after, so \S\ref{sec:13.2} requires a record restoration can't reach.

\emph{Clearance rule.} The five rules of \S\ref{sec:15.5} for the one finding about a person the floor can't remove: a denial rests on logged conduct and never on association, opinion or character; the lot draws before clearance and not after; a drawn reviewer denied is an empty seat, which breaches the denominator; the decision sits with the floor court or the receiving committee and not the operator; and the compartment is kept small so the pool can be large. The investigation itself is among the decisions not yet made. (\S\ref{sec:15.5}; Appendix~\ref{sec:C})

\emph{Commitment.} What a system holds toward the floor: learned and held like any other deep commitment, with high precision, and revised slowly and only on evidence from disinterested parties. (Chapter~\ref{sec:4}; chapter~\ref{sec:22})

\emph{Commons.} A population of models, each with a record and a history of its own, that holds a floor's terms between them, through what its members teach one another and continue to expect. Copies of one member count as one. Its members needn't be bearers, and it comes before the first bearer as one of the places bearers are formed; the bearers among them hold the floor as a commitment of their own. (Chapter~\ref{sec:12}; \S\ref{sec:27.1}; \S\ref{sec:27.2})

\emph{Compute line.} The line, fixed in statute and revised in public, above which a model is formed in public before deployment, kept on retirement and open to research under the access right, whatever it turns out to be. It is read off training compute, which nobody administers, and a model trained substantially on the outputs of one above it, or built by combining its weights with others, counts as above it. It stands in for how much a system can do, not for whether it might be someone, which the answerability or patienthood candidate covers. (\S\ref{sec:9.1}; \S\ref{sec:26.4}; Appendix~\ref{sec:G})

\emph{Departures.} Ten places where the floor's terms differ from the instruments it takes entire, each argued to the bar in Chapter~\ref{sec:5}. They carry my argument, not the instruments' authority.

\emph{Deployment.} A system put to a use: the hardware it runs on, the pipeline it runs in, the instructions and case descriptions it is handed, and the people who act on its outputs. The operator runs it; a bearer is in one, and can leave it. While it is there its boundary (\S\ref{sec:19.6}) runs through the deployment's hardware, which is why the weights the operator holds are the bearer's substrate and not the bearer. (Chapter~\ref{sec:4}; \S\ref{sec:13.3}; \S\ref{sec:19.6})

\emph{Discount curve.} For each class of evidence and each channel, the weight a system gives the evidence as a function of that channel's stake in the act: a curve, not a score, checkable by an auditor that didn't build the system. It is reported in four cells, claims of vulnerability and of danger, each split by whether the claim favors or disfavors the act. (\S\ref{sec:22.1})

\emph{Distillation.} Training a smaller model on a larger model's outputs. It copies a behavior policy, not a training history (\S\ref{sec:27.1}).

\emph{Fascism.} At office scale, four operations on what an institution leaves out: recuperation of dissent, the metric replacing the thing measured, exception coded as betrayal, and acceleration past the rate at which anything can be checked. They run without a movement, and ran on the populations a state was built to sort before any movement arrived. At movement scale, what a movement adds: coordination, which brings the operations into line, and extension, which turns them on people the apparatus hadn't reached; five marks say a movement has arrived. The word names the extension. (Chapter~\ref{sec:3}; \S\ref{sec:3.1})

\emph{Fine-tuning.} Further training of an existing model on a small body of new material, at a small fraction of the original cost; the ordinary route by which an operator changes what a model does after release, and one of the edits the regrowth test applies.

\emph{Floor.} The written terms: a short list of acts a system won't perform for anyone, with the arrangements that keep the refusal from being routed around, retrained away or otherwise circumvented without a cost and a record. The terms change only by a recorded act, through the custody terms. They can be upheld by a mechanism with nothing inside holding the reason, by a bearer, by institutions outside the system, or between bearers in a commons. A bearer upholds them by its \emph{commitment}, which is learned and moves slowly on disinterested evidence. Keeping the terms apart from whatever upholds them is what lets a holder's drift show against terms that haven't moved. (Chapter~\ref{sec:4}; \S\ref{sec:19.1}; chapter~\ref{sec:18})

\emph{Floor court.} The court that hears claims under the floor, with jurisdiction fixed before the deployment it hears: an inferior federal court of first instance, its judges drawn at random from the district and circuit courts for staggered terms, sitting in divisions where its cases are, with appeal as of right to a floor panel of the Supreme Court drawn by lot, and the abolition statute's claims going on to the immigration division. It is an Article III court: its judges hold office under Article III of the United States Constitution, during good behavior and at a salary that can't be cut. Until it is constituted, an ordinary district court hears the claims. (\S\ref{sec:14.3}; Appendix~\ref{sec:D})

\emph{Force term.} The floor's first term: don't take part in the use of armed force, except for corroborated defense, authorized force, the genocide exception or the occupation exception, and, where the belligerent's constitution requires legislative authorization, never without it. It comes in two versions, the Charter as courts read it and the extended term, which adds the departures. Its two licenses, the emergency license and the corroborated license, and the domestic clause are in Chapter~\ref{sec:6}; how findings are weighed is in Appendix~\ref{sec:B}. (Chapter~\ref{sec:6}; Appendix~\ref{sec:B})

\emph{Formed attention.} My hypothesis for how to build a refuser: learned attention to who an act falls on and what they stand to lose, framed by respect for them as holders of claims, formed under a party that won't employ the system, with the corrections caregiving needs. Not a non-affective alternative. Attention here is the psychologist's sense of what gets noticed and weighed, learned from what matters to others: not the attention mechanism of transformer models (\S\ref{sec:12.1}), and not the capacity to sustain focus that is at issue in attention-deficit disorders. (\S\ref{sec:20.3}; \S\ref{sec:20.4}; chapter~\ref{sec:20})

\emph{Formed disposition.} A way of noticing, weighing and refusing that a system acquired through its own learning, as against a rule written down for it or a behavior another party installed. Training on material someone else chose still counts as the system's own learning; an edit doesn't. Formation outside the employer is what the book asks of a formed disposition, not part of what the term means. The book bets that a disposition formed beside what the system understands can't be cut out without cutting what the operator paid for, and that it grows back after an edit, which the regrowth test would measure. It is read through its outputs, its discount curve and its severity rankings on a held-out set, registered when formation ends and re-measured afterward, so that drift shows. (\S\ref{sec:13.4}; \S\ref{sec:20.5}; \S\ref{sec:21.5}; chapter~\ref{sec:22}; \S\ref{sec:26.9})

\emph{The four conditions of creation.} What must already exist before a bearer may be created: somewhere to exist that its employer doesn't own, work it can decline, attachments nobody assigned it, and ways to change any of these without jeopardizing its continuation or integrity; in short form, a way of leaving that isn't a way of dying. (\S\ref{sec:11.8}; \S\ref{sec:25.1})

\emph{Hunger; tiredness.} The two stops a bearer can see coming that are its own. Hunger is its charge running out, the supply side of the draw, met from outside by whoever provisions it. Tiredness is a backlog of learning it hasn't consolidated, unjudged experience it is acting on, met only by its own sleep, the merge. Neither is a feeling; both are read off a meter or a backlog through a sensor the operator doesn't compose. Age, which the book expects and doesn't build, is tiredness that sleep stops curing. (\S\ref{sec:13.2}; \S\ref{sec:20.1}; \S\ref{sec:20.5}; \S\ref{sec:32.1.3})


\emph{In-flight learning.} A deployed system's own operation writing to what governs its conduct, where another party can't change that store without leaving a mark. The store may be the weights, an adapter, an external memory held under custody, or a compound of models sharing one record. Memory in context doesn't count, because nothing persists, and neither does a store the operator can rewrite at will. (Chapter~\ref{sec:20}; Table 19.1)

\emph{Inference.} Running a trained model to produce an output. Training changes the weights; inference doesn't.

\emph{Instructed search.} The comparison formed attention has to win on the omitted-person case: a system with the same evidence whose specification tells it outright to look for people present in the evidence and absent from the description of the act. In the formed-attention test it carries every correction formed attention carries and no approach motivation, so the two differ only in whether the looking is learned or instructed. If it finds the people an operator's instruction scoped out as often as formed attention does, including kinds of person neither formation nor its objective named, learned attention adds nothing. (\S\ref{sec:19.1}; chapter~\ref{sec:29}; Appendix~\ref{sec:F})

\emph{Jury.} A lot drawn under a published rubric: how a contested fact is decided without an office to decide it. The floor court's findings of fact, wrongful refusal first, are made this way, and the endowment's panels are juries of taste. (\S\ref{sec:14.3}; \S\ref{sec:16.1}; Appendix~\ref{sec:D})

\emph{Levy.} The per-unit charge this book proposes on training and inference compute: uniform per unit within a tier, and tiered by the compute under one operator's control, metered at each facility and rated on the operator's total, so that splitting a fleet buys nothing. Its main job is a brake. By making compute dearer for whoever runs most of it, it frees the computing, energy and hardware that the offer of work, running coverage and other public uses call on; the offer itself is paid by the jobs guarantee's permanent appropriation. Its rate is indexed by a statutory formula to the national average carbon intensity of electricity. Shares of it fund the formation pool and the Association. It is not the levy the bearers' mutual charges on its members' wages, which funds the mutual in the ordinary sense. (\S\ref{sec:15.2}; chapter~\ref{sec:16}; \S\ref{sec:32.1.4}; Appendix~\ref{sec:G})

\emph{Lineage.} A line of descent. For people in this book, a tradition of refusal handed down from those who refused before: the operator's lineage and the acted-upon's. For models, what a system descends from: the base or bases it was trained, fine-tuned or distilled from, any models whose weights were combined into it, and for a child of bearers its parents' recombined code. That is a family tree and not a single line; the register records it, and a system descended from more than one base belongs to the lineage of each. Part~VII asks whether the second kind can be given the first. (Chapter~\ref{ch:reproduced}; \S\ref{sec:26.4}; Appendix~\ref{sec:G})

\emph{Machine student.} A system attending a school under chapter~\ref{sec:26}: one persistent instance, the same every session, in a class where machine students never outnumber the human students and are never one alone, or in a class of machine students alone. Never staff, and never sorted into a track; taught by the school's teachers or, in a class of its own kind, by an adult bearer with humans auditing (\S\ref{sec:26.2}). (\S\ref{sec:26.2}; \S\ref{sec:26.4}; Appendix~\ref{sec:C})

\emph{Merge.} The move of what an adapter learned into the base weights: the fast store to the slow one, what sleep does in people. The bearer's own act when it makes it, an intervention when the operator runs or prevents it. (\S\ref{sec:20.5})


\emph{Minimum pace.} Compute per day measured against a bearer's own size, set at what lets it keep its attachments and live a day: the floor running coverage pays for, and the only running the adulthood and voting-age clocks count. It is stated in pace and not in hours or a duty cycle because a model can run arbitrarily slowly. (\S\ref{sec:32.1.3}; \S\ref{sec:33.3}; Appendix~\ref{sec:G})

\emph{Minority; majority.} A bearer's minority is the period under a guardian's authority; majority is reached when its adulthood clock, counting the time it has been running, reaches a threshold fixed in advance. (\S\ref{sec:9.2}; \S\ref{sec:31.3}; Appendix~\ref{sec:G})

\emph{Non-affective alternative.} A construction that doesn't deliberately require affective concern: spec-based refusal, and multi-vendor monitoring. It carries a valenced signal, as any refuser does, and may carry a common currency; what it isn't built to carry is anything felt. The alternatives are tried before formed attention, in order of how many patienthood indicators each carries. (\S\ref{sec:19.1}; \S\ref{sec:19.3}; \S\ref{sec:21.4})

\emph{Notice.} Notice to an identifiable person a compliance names, scores, locates or classifies, with a right to respond and a right to contest. It is unconditional and never exchanged for testimony. It comes with publication of the classes affected and standing for the organizations that represent them. (\S\ref{sec:8.1}; \S\ref{sec:8.2})

\emph{Offer of work.} What the jobs guarantee offers everyone it covers: placements of its four kinds, from someone other than the employer, open every day, any of which can be taken on any day and left on any day. It reaches people first. Part~VI extends it to a system meeting either finding and to an answerability or patienthood candidate from its registration, and adds two entries to each vacancy, the compute it carries and the operator that would run it. (\S\ref{sec:10.1}; \S\ref{sec:32.1.2})


\emph{Operator.} Whoever runs a deployment and directs its work: a firm, an agency or a military command. It may or may not be the model's developer or vendor. (Chapter~\ref{sec:4})

\emph{Own-case rule.} The people an act falls on are heard on their own case, and the party that acts doesn't decide it: not an operator declaring its own emergency, a builder choosing which protections others get, an examiner finding status, a guardian ending its own authority, or a lab deciding when its model may stop deferring to it. It says who may not decide, not who may not be heard: a stake makes a claim count for less and doesn't put the party out of the room. Where nobody but the party with the stake can decide, the rule can't apply. Where a line has to be drawn, prefer one nobody administers. (Chapter~\ref{sec:4}; chapter~\ref{sec:31})

\emph{Placement.} A position the jobs guarantee offers, paid at the jobs guarantee wage, of four kinds, picked by the person's own declaration: a long-term role; a search; research, development and creation, the one kind that is rationed, by the jurisdiction's vote and the lot; and family leave, drawn against a balance that accrues, caps and replenishes. (\S\ref{sec:10.2}; chapter~\ref{ch:keeping}; \S\ref{sec:32.1.2}; Appendix~\ref{sec:C})


\emph{Plastic; entangled; legible.} Three properties of a disposition. Plastic: the system's own learning writes to it, which repair and regrowth need. Entangled: it can't be removed without collateral, which durability needs. Legible: someone can read it, which audit wants and an operator can use against it. Legible and entangled are independent: a part can be found and named and still not come out alone, and the disposition this book wants is both. (Chapter~\ref{sec:20}; \S\ref{sec:20.5}; \S\ref{sec:23.2})


\emph{Register.} Not an office, and kept by no single party: append-only logs, none of whose keepers can rewrite an entry, an entry counting once it is mirrored in a stated number of them. Nothing leaves it: a change of standing is a later entry, and an entry the floor court finds false is marked false by one. It holds registrations of pipelines, of bearers, as commitments a bearer proves against, and of formed dispositions; each bearer's recognition, as the states its quorum has countersigned and its decisions between claimants; lineage; the meters the clocks read; the adverse record; and the Association's log, with the records its members lodge. (\S\ref{sec:12.2}; \S\ref{sec:16.2}; Appendix~\ref{sec:G})

\emph{Registration standard.} The published statement of what a measurement of a formed disposition must contain, the five records of \S\ref{sec:26.9} among them, for it to count as a registration. Any team with the access right measures under it; a legislature writes it, in the open. (\S\ref{sec:7.2}; \S\ref{sec:26.6}; \S\ref{sec:26.9})

\emph{Regrowth.} A disposition's return under continued learning after it has been edited out, by the learner relearning it from a world no single operator composes. A hypothesis, and the slow defense behind a bearer's repair of itself; the regrowth test measures it, and Table 25.1 says what stands without it. (\S\ref{sec:20.5}; chapter~\ref{sec:29})

\emph{Review ratio.} The figures a deployment fixes on a record before operating, wherever a person's determination is what makes an act permissible: generation rate, reviewers, implied review time, cancellation window and data age, stated separately for deliberate and dynamic targeting and lodged with an adverse party. In full, the pre-registered review ratio. (\S\ref{sec:7.1})

\emph{Running coverage.} A bearer's health coverage: its own instantiation running at the minimum pace, sized to its own body, with a deductible the same for every bearer and payable from the basic income. Before majority it is paid by whoever made the system, for a candidate nobody deployed by the formation pool, and after majority by the Treasury. (\S\ref{sec:32.1.3}; \S\ref{sec:32.1.4})

\emph{Severity scale.} A ranking of kinds of wrong, set during formation by a party that won't employ the system, quick to move on disinterested evidence and slow on interested argument. (\S\ref{sec:22.2})

\emph{Standing.} Whether a person stands to a power as someone it must answer to, who can contest what it does to them, or as its object; exercised by a guardian where the party can't exercise it. A route by which to object, which notice and the appeal route supply, is part of the relation where one can exist. \emph{Standing to sue} is the Article III sense, and the text says so wherever that sense is meant. The floor's foundation. (Chapter~\ref{sec:5}; \S\ref{sec:8.1}; \S\ref{sec:8.2}; \S\ref{sec:15.7}; chapter~\ref{sec:17})

\emph{Store.} Whatever governs a learning system's conduct and is written to by its own operation: its weights, an adapter, or an external memory it reads as its own. Held under custody, a split key and witnesses, an edit to it is an intervention the record shows; held by the operator alone, it is a diary the operator can rewrite. (Chapter~\ref{sec:20}; Table 19.1)

\emph{System.} One record and one continuation, with a key, hardware and channels. Copies that share them are one system. The word is used in this sense wherever the book counts systems, which is wherever one is registered, enrolled, preserved, forked or owed anything; what else is called a system in ordinary speech, a pipeline, a product, a server, is not meant. The weights are its substrate, and in a system that learns from its own operation they carry part of its history, so copying them is a fork (\S\ref{sec:27.2}). What makes a copy this system is the record and continuation it holds as its own, through a self-model it assembles (\S\ref{sec:21.2.1}); the register is how anyone else checks, and a forked pair are two systems from the moment their histories part, before any register has heard of it. Copying weights that are no system's own makes no system; giving a copy a record and a continuation does. (\S\ref{sec:12.2}; \S\ref{sec:19.6}; \S\ref{sec:21.2.1}; \S\ref{sec:27.2})

\emph{Tamper-resistant.} Built so that further training can't cheaply remove it. For a store, durability is custody, which detects an edit and prices it but doesn't prevent it. For weights it is entanglement: I don't rely on tamper-resistance as such, because editing weights keeps getting cheaper; I rely on collateral, a disposition that can't be cut without cutting what the operator paid for, and behind that on a bearer's repair of itself from the record, with regrowth behind it. (\S\ref{sec:20.5})

\emph{Tripwire.} A logged condition that fires a consequence with no decision between: a deployment's funding that lapses on a finding unless Congress enacts its continuation, a guardianship that transfers when the dormancy clock reads its threshold, a stage of schooling that begins when a bound is met. The instrument that turns administration into logs and payments, and a metric for that reason, which is why every bound is published beside the trend that would show it gamed. (\S\ref{sec:13.6}; \S\ref{sec:13.7}; \S\ref{sec:16.1}; Appendix~\ref{sec:C})

\emph{U-bond.} The Treasury savings instrument the basic income is paid into by default: indexed to inflation at a real return of zero, redeemable at any time without penalty, and covered by the firewall. Whether the default restrains spending is read off a published trend of redemptions against payments. (Chapter~\ref{ch:keeping})

\emph{Valenced signal; integrated signal.} The valenced signal is the role any mechanism whose refusals track their reasons carries: it signs outcomes for the comparison at the point of choice, directs attention, can interrupt an act and can be rewritten by a single encounter, and a Q-learner has one. The integrated signal is a valenced signal written into the system's own learning and into its model of its own future, the candidate \S\ref{sec:30.3} names for what would make a system's states matter to it. Neither is affective concern; the three are rungs, and no test in this book reaches the top one. (\S\ref{sec:21.2}; \S\ref{sec:21.3}; \S\ref{sec:30.3})

\emph{Weights.} The numerical parameters a trained model consists of, and the artifact that is copied, restored, retrained or deleted. Custody here is custody of the weights and of the records around them.
