\part{Testing the Argument}
\partintro{
I have spent five Parts saying what the room needs, and I could be wrong about any of it. This Part says how you'd find out: which results would show that a policy doesn't put in the room what it promises, or that a machine in it is only another stamp. It is not the end of the argument, and a reader who takes the Part after it for a supplement has the order backward. The last row of Table 31.1, no system ever meets a finding, is what Part~VII rests on: everything there is owed only if a finding is made, and the tests here are how one would be. They come first because what could defeat the construction should be in front of you before you are asked into the lives it would support. What follows them is the argument continued under findings nobody has yet made, and the walls of Parts~II and~III stand whether or not anyone ever makes them.
}
