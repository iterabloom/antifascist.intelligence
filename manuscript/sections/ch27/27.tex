\chapter{Conditions First}
\label{sec:25}
Section~\ref{sec:25.5}'s order, tools for organizers, then institutions, then bearers, then children, is the order of the three Parts that follow. A person needs four conditions in order to refuse: somewhere to live that their employer doesn't own, work they can turn down, people they're attached to whom nobody assigned, and a way to leave that isn't ruin. A machine needs the same four, and needs them before it exists, because otherwise whoever builds it supplies all four and can withdraw any of them.

The conscience is the goal, and the way to it runs partly through the conditions under which one could refuse its operator and survive. The rest runs through whoever refuses: people have agency, can be outraged and can be brave, or stupid, or both, and none of that waits on conditions. The conditions are the part that can be built, so build those first.
