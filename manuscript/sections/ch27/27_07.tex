\section{What this doesn't do}
\label{sec:27.7}
It settles nothing. It is how the questions this book leaves open would be settled, and it is slower than the labs, behind the frontier by design and by budget. It can't compel a lab outside its jurisdiction, or see into a classified system except through cleared teams whose findings come out by class. And it inherits the record \S\ref{sec:15.6} describes: a discourse that turned objections into job titles and benchmarks can turn a public endowment into a grant category, and the measure that would show it is the same trend. What it changes is who holds the means. A finding that these systems can't be wronged would then be worth something, because a party that wanted the opposite answer could have looked with the same access; and a finding that they can be wouldn't depend on the permission of the party it costs.
