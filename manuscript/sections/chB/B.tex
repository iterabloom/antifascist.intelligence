\chapter{The Court Program}
\label{sec:B}
One holding stands between the record and the commander at the top. \emph{Trump v. United States} makes a President presumptively immune for official acts, commanding the armed forces among them, and bars testimony probing immune conduct from a jury \autocite{trump2024immunity}. Every instrument I propose takes effect through procurement, appropriation, coalition conditions or collective agreement, and none waits on changing that. What changing it would take is a program that no ally can be bound by, run at a pace my own fourth operation condemns; I set it out in one paragraph so that the omission shows.

\emph{Size by schedule:} once there is a trifecta and the filibuster is gone, appointments to the Court come on a fixed schedule of not fewer than two a year, more if the enacting majority can pass it, because size is the one step the Court cannot review. \emph{Two courts and two chambers:} an inferior Article III floor court, its judges drawn at random from the district and circuit courts for staggered terms, hears claims under the floor statute at first instance, holds classified records under the Foreign Intelligence Surveillance Court's procedures and grants interim relief, with appeal as of right to a floor chamber of the Supreme Court drawn by lot, while placement disputes under the jobs guarantee run through an administrative tier and an Article III guarantee court of appeals to a separate guarantee chamber. \emph{An adversary as of right:} counsel for the people a deployment affects appear in the floor court with clearance and standing as of right and receive the records lodged with an adverse party. \emph{Move a class of cases, never leave it with none:} Congress may move a class of cases from one Article III forum to another and may never leave a class with no Article III forum at all. \emph{The statute, then the amendment:} once the Court has changed, a statute removes the immunity, and an amendment reverses the holding \autocite{biden2024amendment} and entrenches the schedule, eighteen-year active terms, the chambers and the channeling rule, coming last because it is the one step no later Congress or Court can undo.
