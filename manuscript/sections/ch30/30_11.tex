\section{Which schools count}
\label{sec:26.11}
They are, first, the nonprofit nonpublic schools certified on the funding and control records they lodge, as the Association's members are (\S\ref{sec:27.1}), on the model of the movement schools of the chapter's opening: constituted by a jurisdiction, teaching the required core, governed in part by their students, and run, funded and staffed by no party that builds, deploys or licenses models. And they are the public schools, colleges and adult education a jurisdiction already runs, which take the students the certified schools can't seat, under the same core and the same ratio. Both are funded as institutions and not per student, and none is held by the branch that operates military deployments. No school holds placement authority. A machine student is never staff: no school may use one as a teacher, tutor or assistant, since a student that teaches is being used by the school that is supposed to be forming it. An adult bearer is another matter, and \S\ref{sec:26.2} says on what conditions one may teach.
