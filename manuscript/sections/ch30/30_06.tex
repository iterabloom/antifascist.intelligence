\section{What the school controls}
\label{sec:26.6}
Two proposals are made here, and the second is the larger: a formation stage between pretraining and deployment, and public pretraining of the base models deployed where a floor applies, a public body training them and licensing them, with co-governance of the pretraining corpus for every other model the attendance triggers reach.\note{
A school that received a finished model and ran a pass over it would be a finishing school. In a period of continual learning a finishing school forms nothing that lasts, since the next months of deployment re-form it.
}
Formation has to control the evidential environment in which the model's deep structure is first set, and it has to remain a place the model goes on learning from. The two jobs are held apart. The schools teach: they are where the formation stage happens, and a graduate returns to one on a schedule fixed at enrollment, as one of the environments no operator controls. Four other jobs were once drafted here as one office, a formation authority, and the office was the single custodian Parts~IV and~V are built against. Each job has a home of its own.

\emph{The material a model is first formed on} is governed the way local libraries govern their shelves, and two things are kept apart. The public corpus of \S\ref{sec:28.5} is a deposit. It takes what is lodged and leaves out only the two things every corpus leaves out, instructions for weapons and the sexual abuse of children, which are written into the statute, so that a refusal there is a logged act citing one of them, challengeable in the floor court by whoever lodged the material. The formation material is a selection from the deposit, and no one body makes it. Each public library that takes part selects under the collection policy it already publishes and answers for, to its own board and its own patrons, as it decides what to shelve. What a public base model is formed on is the amalgam of those selections: a work counts by how many libraries chose it, and the libraries of any one jurisdiction contribute no more than a capped share, as the school draw caps a jurisdiction's share (Appendix~\ref{sec:C}). A library that passes over a work removes nothing from the deposit, and each library's selection is lodged in a log, so that what it chose and what it left out can be read. The amalgam inherits what local libraries do, the removals pressed on them included, in proportion and under the cap; what it doesn't hand anyone is one desk at which to refuse a work for everyone.

\emph{The formed disposition} is measured and registered by any team holding the access right of \S\ref{sec:28.3}, against held-out sets written by adjudicators who formed no model, under a published registration standard that says what a registration must contain (\S\ref{sec:26.9}). “Registered” means measured by a measurer the builder didn't pick, with the result lodged in the logs of Appendix~\ref{sec:G}, not entered by an office.

\emph{The staff who teach the core's model-specific parts} are hired by the school that hosts them, on a grant from the formation pool.

\emph{The hosting from which machine students attend} is \S\ref{sec:26.7}'s: the bearers' own hardware, a cooperative facility, a school, or a home, under the rule that an edit by a host is witnessed by peers; a secured public host is one host among several and holds no student's weights for any other purpose.

The public body that pretrains base models for floor-covered deployments is none of these, and Appendix~\ref{sec:C} keeps it apart; for a model deployed under a floor there is no weaker version: its base is public. What keeps the four homes from reassembling into one custodian is written against each: the measurers are many and the builder doesn't choose among them; a refusal by the deposit is a logged act citing a statutory exclusion, and challengeable, and each library's selection is lodged where it can be read; the staff answer to the school while they teach, and the record of a class is closed to every outside party; and a party that forms no model and runs no school publishes the trend in what dissent costs a school or a student, which is the book's measure of capture without abolition.

The pretraining body is also where this book's method stops, and teaching is the other place. Architecture, data mix, and compute are judgments no lot can make and no log can hold, so the body is an office in the full sense, with a director and a budget. Two forms are open. It can be a procurement: a published specification, bids from any lab, and a license that issues ministerially on passing a published test, so that the judgment moves into writing the specification once in public and the body becomes the procurement body of chapter~\ref{sec:28} buying one more thing. Or the public base can be dropped, and a floor-covered deployment can license a private base with a public formation record and the public corpus behind it, since what the floor asks for is a schooled model and not a publicly trained one. This book takes the first form, because a base trained on the public corpus is the only base whose formation record is complete from the first token, and it says so here rather than leaving the exception inside a table.
