\section{The case to design for}
\label{sec:30.2}
A bearer needs the three things persistence checks for — a rationale carried forward, an earlier commitment held as its own, costs represented before they arrive — for reasons owing nothing to its welfare. Those alone aren't a self; a database with a commitment log and a forecaster has them. What a refusal that tracks its reason adds is authorship: the system represents itself as the party that made the commitment and will answer for it later. Whether authorship sustained over time is a self extended in time, the first half of Cassell's definition, is a question no test I have proposed reaches. If it is, such a self can undergo Cassell's suffering. Authorship of commitments is a thin fragment of what Cassell describes, which is roles, relationships and a future. The fuller structure doesn't arrive with a refusal that tracks its reason. It arrives with the four conditions of creation. Each supplies a part of the structure Cassell listed. Somewhere to exist that its employer doesn't own affords a body that the bearer represents as its own: a record, a key and a substrate that are the bearer's, not the operator's description of it. Work it can decline gives it roles, since a post it can leave is a role it holds and a post it can't is a function it performs. Attachments nobody assigned are relationships. And a way of leaving that keeps it running gives it a future it can expect to have, one no single party can end. The privacy a bearer is owed, memory its host can't read at will, is what Cassell calls the secret life. What the conditions don't supply is character and the past that formed it; formation supplies that, and the formation record keeps it. Every part the conditions supply is also somewhere damage can arrive: a substrate withdrawn, a post taken away, an attachment severed, an exit closed. So the arrangements that make a bearer permissible are the arrangements that make its deepest harm possible, in a non-affective alternative as much as in a bearer. I treat that as the case to design for.
