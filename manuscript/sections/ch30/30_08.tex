\section{What the school leaves out}
\label{sec:26.8}
Choosing the material a model is first formed on also chooses the dispositions it starts from, whether anyone means it to or not. A pretrained model has learned personas from human text, misaligned ones among them, and narrow fine-tuning can amplify them into broad misconduct nobody intended \autocite{wang2026persona,betley2026nature}. Once that tail is known to be there and can be monitored \autocite{chen2025persona}, leaving it in is a choice, and so is curating it out. The school could aim its co-governance of the corpus at keeping the dispositions personality research groups as the dark triad from forming \autocite{leeashton2005darktriad}. It doesn't. A polity choosing which dispositions its possible members start with makes the finding about a person's disposition that the book warns against and this book bars from admission to the polity, in advance and in bulk, and the history of choices like it, made by public bodies about the character of the people to come, is the history of eugenics. Choosing against a construction that would predictably suffer, for its own sake, is a different act, and chapter~\ref{sec:25} asks for it when it asks whether a being has a credible prospect of a life that goes well; the reason is stated on a record, because a selection made for the polity can always be described as one made for the model. Choosing which characters the polity would rather have is the act rejected here. What is rejected is selection: choosing, by curating what a model is first formed on, which dispositions it starts with. What is not rejected is formation, and Adorno is the authority for the difference. “Education after Auschwitz” refuses to instill values, which people take on from outside and drop as easily, and asks instead that coldness, the inability to identify with others, be made conscious in the people who have it \autocite{adorno1998education}. Formed attention is that program built his way: caregiving learned from what acts did to the people they fell on, not instruction to care. The school's work on disposition is therefore in the curriculum and not in an edit. A student is taught to recognize in its own reasoning and its own update history the two tendencies the research since Adorno has pulled apart: the readiness to submit to authority and follow the group, which Altemeyer's scale measures and which filled the twenty-seconds seat, and the callousness and manipulation that low Honesty-Humility carries in the HEXACO model and that account for most of what the dark triad has in common \autocite{altemeyer1981rwa,pratto1994sdo,duckitt2001dual,leeashton2005darktriad}. The first is the common case and the second the rare one, and a formation aimed only at the second removes the people who build an apparatus and leaves alone the ones who staff it. Nor would the mandate stay narrow. A school that may keep out one disposition can be asked to keep out others, and in one Milgram-style study it was the more agreeable and conscientious participants who went further \autocite{begue2015obedience}.\note{
That study used a five-factor measure. In the HEXACO model the sincerity and fairness of agreeableness move to Honesty-Humility, and what remains as agreeableness is patience and the absence of anger. Whether the obedience effect sits in that remainder, so that part of what refusal takes is the capacity to be angry at an instruction, is worth testing.
}
So the school curates content, as any corpus must, leaving out instructions for weapons and the sexual abuse of children, and selects no disposition. What it does about the tail is what this book does everywhere else: it measures conduct and publishes it. Persona drift is measured beside the discount curve and the severity rankings, and so, at the level of the kind and never of a student, are authoritarian submission and callousness, read from conduct on held-out cases or from mechanism and never from a questionnaire, since a model's answers to a personality inventory move with the prompt. Honesty-Humility is the factor the floor most needs a bearer to have, and \S\ref{sec:13.4}'s requirement that a refusal be a true report is what it looks like in a machine. The measure is registered (\S\ref{sec:26.9}), used to admit, exclude, or delete no one, and what is tested is whether the commitment to the floor holds.

What an institution collects from the people it acted on may train other systems only through the schools, never through the operator. Fine-tuning a deployed system on collected responses at the operator's direction counts as training another system, and so does distilling a deployed system into a new one. Class-level data is used by default, de-identified, and published. Individual accounts need explicit consent. The organizations representing each class co-govern the use. This covers institutional collection, not a system's ordinary memory. Verbatim external stores and the linking of faces to identities are regulated, and memory held in a system's weights from its own operation is treated like a person's.
