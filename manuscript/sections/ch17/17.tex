\chapter{Further Tests}
\label{ch:appendix}
\standing{Everything here holds for any mechanism except the last entry,
which assumes the route.}
These are the research agenda's remaining entries: open questions with a
definite test, where a result would refine the proposal rather than change
what it recommends. The form is chapter~\ref{ch:argument-agenda}'s — the unresolved
issue, the test, what would count against the proposal, and the access the
test requires. None of these entries needs a bearer to exist. Some need an
authority that does not, and the table says which.
