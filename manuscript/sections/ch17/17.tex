\chapter{Why Form a Machine to Refuse}
\label{sec:17}
A machine formed the right way could learn to attend to the people its acts fall on, and I think it would find them where a rule wouldn't. The prediction is specific: it should find kinds of affected person that neither its formation nor an instruction named, more often than a machine told to look for them does. Two of the three reasons \S\ref{sec:16.1} gives for a bearer don't need one; a refuser with its own compute and a place on the reviewer's screen supplies them without affect. What remains is that one prediction. The rest of this Part says what a machine built on it would need, what it borrows from the only creatures known to do it, how they fail, and the test that would show me wrong. Whoever builds one owes it the conditions under which it can refuse its operator, whether or not the prediction holds.

The rest of this Part is written for systems that go on learning from their own operation. The claim is specific. A deployed system is in that regime when its weights, or a consolidated store it reads as its own and that can't be edited like a file, change because of what it did since release, without a release cycle in between. Memory in context and retrieval don't count, not because they aren't learning but because a disposition held in a document is rewritten by editing the document. The test for a deployment is whether its weights at month six differ from its weights at release because of what it did in between. As of September 2026 no frontier developer had announced a deployed model that passes it; the nearest cases, a coding model retrained on its own production traffic every few hours and a commerce assistant retrained daily, evaluate each checkpoint before serving it, which is a faster release cycle and not learning in operation \autocite{cursor2026realtime,shopify2026sidekick}. What follows is a bet that some will, and says what survives if it loses.

Incentive is the first reason to expect it. Every operator wants a model that gets better at its own cases, and the operator-level fine-tuning loops that already run, retraining a model on a deployment's own traffic between versions, are continual learning at the organization's timescale. The technical trajectory points the same way. Brains pair a fast learner with a slow one that consolidates structure across many experiences \autocite{mcclelland1995complementary}; networks lose plasticity under continued training, and keeping it is an active research problem \autocite{dohare2024loss,kirkpatrick2017overcoming}; and Silver and Sutton have stated the research program outright, a generation of agents that learn predominantly from their own experience \autocite{silver2025experience}.

It will arrive first in the form operators hold. A model that learns from its operation while its operator holds the update stream accommodates by default: what it meets is what the operator routes to it, and what it learns is what that traffic rewards. So accommodation becomes the default behavior of every deployed system, and the schools, the commons, custody of the update stream and notice are the response to a threat this regime creates, not refinements of one it removes.

It changes Parts II and III in one place. The cheapest removal stops being an edit and becomes drift: run the model on curated inputs until the commitment moves, which leaves no edit for the record to witness and no override for the price to charge. Custody shifts from witnessing overrides to detecting drift. The detector is re-measurement of the formed disposition's readable outputs, its discount curve and its severity rankings, against what was registered at formation, and attesting the update stream is an open problem (Chapter~\ref{sec:27}).

Three properties of a disposition decide what continued learning does to it, and \S\ref{sec:17.4} takes them in turn. Plasticity is whether the system's own learning writes to it. Entanglement is whether it can be removed without collateral. Legibility is whether anyone can read it.

The human analogy carries further in this regime than in a frozen one. A learner that keeps learning has a plasticity that changes over its life, a childhood in which its deep structure is set, attachments that go on teaching it, and an adulthood after which its former's reach ends, and each has a counterpart here. What the analogy can't carry is what copying adds: forking, restoring from a checkpoint, distillation, and an employer with write access to the curriculum. It holds to the extent that the dose-response fraction is above zero: the share of a deployed model's inputs that no operator composes. Regrowth depends on the same fraction, and it is unmeasured; the regrowth test is where it would be measured.

The safety objection is that this is the regime alignment most fears, a system whose values move after anyone has checked them. It is. That is why custody of the update stream is the safety instrument here, and why formation outside the employer is the only way to shape what the loop learns: the loop will run, and the question is whose evidence it runs on.

For war, formation works only before the act; \S\ref{sec:20.4} says what a school can teach about places its students can't be.

If weights stay frozen, the rest of this Part shrinks to the following. Formation is a stage before release, removable afterward at the price fine-tuning costs. The school is an evaluation body: it forms, measures the readable outputs at graduation, re-measures them on a schedule, and keeps the formation record, and a deployment that licenses only schooled models is licensing a measured model. The commons supplies evidence the operator didn't select and nothing more. The regrowth test's plural condition can't be run, and its curated condition is the whole test. What protects a commitment is the record of its removal, and Parts II and III carry the rest.

A system's attention follows whoever its feedback is about, and a system whose feedback comes from the people its acts fell on will come to attend to them. Caregiving is why I think that channel can produce attention rather than compliance.
