\part{Machines That Might Refuse}
\partintro{
Every instrument in this book ends at the limit Part III ends on: a constraint that holds against the party in possession has to be held by something that party can't instruct, and under total capture nothing is. People don't end there. Under the most complete terror of the last century most people complied, some did not, and some were left to tell the story. The five measures put people back in the positions the pipelines emptied. Where no person can be in time, the position stays empty, and this Part asks whether a machine could be formed to hold it: someone in the room, and not another stamp.
}
