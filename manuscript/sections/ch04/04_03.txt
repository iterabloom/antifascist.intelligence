4.3. Integrating Moral Intuitions and Deliberation
Building an AI system's moral judgment means deciding how much weight a fast, intuitive read of a situation carries against a slower, deliberate one — and, as section 4.3.1 argues, the popular shorthand for that trade-off is less settled than it looks. Section 4.3.2 takes up what a defensible balance between the two looks like in practice. Section 4.3.3 turns to a related problem: what an AI system does when its judgment conflicts with someone else's, human or artificial.
