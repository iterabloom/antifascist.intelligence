4.4. Moral Ecosystem Approach in AI Development
A holistic vision of AI development, inspired by Urie Bronfenbrenner's Ecological Systems Theory (EST), guides the proposed Moral Ecosystem Approach. EST, which emerged from developmental psychology, asserts that the evolution of an individual is subject to various interwoven environmental systems. As we extend this to the domain of artificial intelligence, we envisage the interactions of AI development and alignment with human values as undergirded by a multitude of interconnected factors. 

Appreciating the complexity of these influences necessitates a layered representation, akin to the concentric circles of an ecosystem wherein each ring interacts with the others to shape overall development.
