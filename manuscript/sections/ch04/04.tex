\chapter{Where the Caring Gets Built}
\label{sec:4}
\standing{This chapter concerns the route. Its first section holds for
any mechanism; the rest assumes a bearer formed rather than installed.}
The formation process proposed here combines commitments that influence action, limits on who can alter them, and persistent interaction. The claims for that combination are comparative and empirical. The proposal specifies necessary properties and a candidate formation process. It does not establish a method for making a machine care.

Three kinds of thing are proposed here and they are not one. A body schema and an attention schema are architecture: facts about how the system is built, checkable from outside by anyone with access and administered by nobody, which is what the precautionary trigger rests on. Continuing learning and persistent interaction with differently formed parties are a training regime and a curriculum, run by whoever runs them. External records and plural formation are institutional conditions, held by whoever holds them. Where this chapter says architecture it means the first, and the checkability claimed for the first is not claimed for the other two.
