\chapter{The Pre-Registered Review Ratio}
\label{sec:4}
\begin{quote}
We [humans] cannot process so much information... you still cannot produce enough targets per day.

— Brigadier General Y.S., \emph{The Human-Machine Team: How to Create Synergy Between Human and Artificial Intelligence That Will Revolutionize Our World}, 2021 \autocite{ys2021humanmachine}
\end{quote}

The epigraph is a design document, not a critique. Its author, writing pseudonymously, commanded Israel's signals-intelligence unit, Unit 8200, and argued for machines that resolve “a human bottleneck for both locating the new targets and decision-making to approve the targets.”
