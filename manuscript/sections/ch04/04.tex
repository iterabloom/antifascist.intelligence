\chapter{Where the Caring Gets Built}
\label{sec:4}
\standing{This chapter concerns the route. Its first section holds for
any mechanism; the rest assumes a bearer formed rather than installed.}
The formation process proposed here combines commitments that influence action, limits on who can alter them, and persistent interaction. Embodiment, attention and body schemas, and continuing learning are the architecture's proposed means of developing autonomy. The claims for that combination are comparative and empirical. The proposal specifies necessary properties and a candidate formation process. It does not establish a method for making a machine care.
