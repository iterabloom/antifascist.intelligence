\subsection{Transfer, and the Floor That Cannot Tell It Has Stopped Applying}
\label{sec:4.2.9}
Every stage after pretraining is transfer learning: a model trained broadly is adapted to something narrower, by fine-tuning it on a smaller corpus \autocite{devlin2018bert}. Domain adaptation is the case where the task stays fixed and the data shifts. Both are ordinary and both work: a model that parses legal argument in one jurisdiction can be adapted to another, and a model trained on daylight images can be adapted to night.

This book asks transfer learning for something else. The hope throughout is that exposure to a wide enough range of political and social material distills something general enough to recognize authoritarian behavior in a country, a platform or a decade the system never saw. What transfer learning has actually been shown to do is carry a task across a distribution shift while the task itself stays fixed. Recognizing authoritarian behavior is not a task with a stable definition. What the definition is has already taken a chapter to argue, and the cases whose family resemblance the transfer would have to exploit are the cases in dispute.

The failure mode matters more here than the technique. Negative transfer happens when source and target are close enough to invite the connection and not close enough to share anything useful, and forcing it degrades the target rather than accelerating it. Domain shift is quieter: the statistics move, the assumptions stop applying, and nothing in the process announces it. A confident wrong answer is indistinguishable at the interface from a confident right one.

For a system whose refusals are supposed to be reliable, that is the danger. A floor transferred into a setting it was not built for is a floor whose bearer cannot tell that it has stopped applying, and a bearer that cannot tell is one that will either refuse the wrong things or fail to refuse the right ones, with the same confidence in both cases. Whatever else the floor's contents are settled by, they cannot be settled by generalization the system performs on itself and cannot audit.
