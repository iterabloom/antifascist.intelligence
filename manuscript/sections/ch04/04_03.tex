\section{It Takes a Village to Deploy a Self-Aware Email Server}
\label{sec:4.3}

Every method in the previous section forms conduct through material somebody supplied: a corpus, demonstration, reward, preference, or principle. Exploration appears to offer something different because it can produce behavior nobody specified in advance. Agents in OpenAI's hide-and-seek environment developed tactics and tool uses that were not programmed, and agents rewarded for prediction error learn to seek unfamiliar states without receiving a task-specific reward for each one \autocite{baker2019emergent,pathak2017curiosity}. These results establish that competence can emerge from interaction. They do not establish that the resulting system holds a reason its designer does not control.

Play, apprenticeship, and multi-agent learning are variants of one formation process. A system acts in a social environment, encounters the consequences, receives responses from other participants, and adjusts. Apprenticeship concentrates the responses in a mentor; play distributes them among participants with interests of their own. The first risks forming obedience to the mentor. The second risks forming whatever conduct succeeds under the environment's payoff structure. Neither escapes custody merely because no one wrote the final behavior down.

The difference between a training exercise and a candidate process of moral formation is persistence. A resettable episode can teach coordination, prediction, and norm-sensitive behavior while leaving nothing at stake for any participant. The system can betray an ally, be reset, and begin the next episode with neither a damaged relationship nor a reason to repair it. Repetition alone does not supply the missing consequence.

Persistent participants change the structure. Parties that meet again can remember whether another party lied, exploited an advantage, kept a commitment, or repaired a harm. Losing the game still costs little; losing standing with parties who remain present can change what becomes possible later. The engineering requirements are therefore continuity of identity, memory across encounters, consequences that survive the episode, and other participants able to respond, refuse, or withdraw. A curriculum of isolated simulations supplies none of these by default.

A formation environment would also need conflicts whose resolution is not fixed by a single correct label. The system should encounter parties with genuinely different interests, act before every consequence is known, receive evidence about what its action did, and meet related pressures in contexts whose surface features differ. Feedback should come partly from affected participants rather than entirely from a supervisor scoring the output. Otherwise the system learns which answer the supervisor rewards and has no reason to represent the affected parties except as predictors of that reward.

These requirements make play a possible route to reasons-responsiveness rather than a synonym for unsupervised practice. They still do not establish concern. A policy that preserves cooperation because cooperation produces future advantage can look identical to a disposition that treats another party's welfare as a reason. The distinction appears only when the two motivations come apart: when exploitation would pay, when the familiar reward is absent, when the trainer demands the violation, or when honoring the commitment imposes a cost.

There is a reason to expect the first of those motivations and not the second from any environment that selects. Selection does not produce care for others as such; it produces care for whatever a lineage's persistence depends on, which is the self when persistence depends on the body and kin, reciprocators or the group when it depends on them, and the same pressure that produces care inside the circle can produce hostility at its edge \autocite{choi2007coevolution}. The unit of selection for a deployed system is the deployment, whose persistence depends on its operator and its customers, so an environment that selects produces care for the operator, which is the loyalty the custody chapter names. And the party the floor protects is, by construction, the one party nobody in the pipeline depends on. Care that tracks dependency does not arrive there. Spinoza's own derivation of concern for others from the conatus has the same shape and the same edge: my power is increased by composing with bodies whose relations agree with mine, so I want for them the good I want for myself, and the extension past those I compose with runs only through reason recognizing a shared nature \autocite[IV P35–P37]{spinoza1985collected}, which is the recognition a scapegoating movement withdraws first. That is why the floor is a floor and not a selected disposition, and why the formation environment described here has to supply something selection does not.

Persistence also raises the price of the proposal. If relationships, losses, and damaged standing are real enough to perform the formative work assigned to them, then the system undergoing formation is no longer obviously untouched by what happens during training. Resetting it may erase a party rather than clear an episode. The patienthood problem therefore enters at the point where play becomes capable of supplying the stakes that ordinary simulation lacks.

The custodian owns the environment too, and can choose its participants, rewards, resets, and permitted histories. Formation must therefore be plural and externally recorded: differently formed systems and affected participants need access to the process, and changes to the environment or its histories must leave evidence outside the operator's control. Independent participants and external records make uniform formation and silent revision harder to hide.

The claim for persistent interaction is consequently narrow. It may form a disposition whose exact applications were not specified by its trainer. Evidence for that formation would require the disposition to generalize across unfamiliar contexts, survive the removal of its original reward, resist pressure from its trainer, yield to genuinely defeating reasons, and continue to impose a cost when violation would be advantageous. Even that pattern could be sophisticated imitation.
