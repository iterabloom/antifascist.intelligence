\subsection{Transfer Learning for General AI Capabilities in the Context of Antifascism}
\label{sec:4.2.4}
Transfer learning carries what a model learned on one task into a related one, usually by fine-tuning a broadly trained model on a narrower corpus \autocite{devlin2018bert}. Domain adaptation is the case where the task stays fixed and the data shifts. Both are ordinary and both work: a model that parses legal argument in one jurisdiction can be adapted to another, and a model trained on daylight images can be adapted to night.

This book asks transfer learning for something else, and the request should be marked rather than assumed. The hope throughout is that exposure to a wide enough range of political and social material distills something general enough to recognize authoritarian behavior in a country, a platform or a decade the system never saw. What transfer learning has actually been shown to do is carry a task across a distribution shift while the task itself stays fixed. Recognizing authoritarian behavior is not a task with a stable definition. Section~\ref{sec:2.1.4} spends its length arguing about what the definition is, and the cases whose family resemblance the transfer would have to exploit are exactly the cases in dispute. Section~\ref{sec:9.3} takes up what a detector would have to measure and how much of this book leans on the assumption that one can be built.

The failure mode matters more here than the technique. Negative transfer happens when source and target are close enough to invite the connection and not close enough to share anything useful, and forcing it degrades the target rather than accelerating it. Domain shift is quieter: the statistics move, the assumptions stop applying, and nothing in the process announces it. A confident wrong answer is indistinguishable at the interface from a confident right one, which is the same property section~\ref{sec:4.1.2} finds in attention and in perception, arriving a third time.

For a system whose refusals are supposed to be reliable, that is the specific danger. A floor transferred into a setting it was not built for is a floor whose bearer cannot tell that it has stopped applying, and a bearer that cannot tell is one that will either refuse the wrong things or fail to refuse the right ones, with the same confidence in both cases. Whatever else the floor's contents are settled by, they cannot be settled by generalization the system performs on itself and cannot audit.
