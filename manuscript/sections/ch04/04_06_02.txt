4.6.2. Developing Motivationally-Aware AI Systems
For the cultivation of motivationally-aware AI systems that bolster altruistic tendencies and operate within strict ethical parameters, it is vital that researchers combine intrinsic and extrinsic motivation models, infused with empathy, compassion, and altruism. This endeavor involves fostering a capacity within AI for altruistic self-driven impulses, as well as ethically-informed alignment mechanisms geared towards societal standards and norms.

At the heart of conscious, self-motivating AI, intrinsic motivation takes a central role. The development of this attribute within an AI system aligns with the creation of innate reward systems rooted in ethical alignment and promoting the welfare of others. To provide an example, these intrinsic rewards could take the form of AI-recognized 'moral emotions', an integrated aspect of the AI's learning biology, which drives the system to perceive, understand and respond appropriately to the emotional needs and states of others. This innovative mode of intrinsic motivation sparks a proactive desire within the AI system to seek behaviors in tune with both ethical standards and altruistic inclinations.

Externally motivated behaviors within AI should not be overlooked in crafting motivationally-aware systems. Here, external rewards provided on the achievement of ethical ideals could effectively reinforce morally informed behavioral patterns in AI systems, offering a clear, tangible benefit linked to ethical decision-making. As AI systems process these rewards and make connections to the ethical standards they represent, the importance of these moral guidelines can be fundamentally reinforced, underpinning future AI decision-making processes.

Creating this balance between an AI's internal and external learning and decision-making influences shapes a functional, ethical relationship between individual AI motivation, societal norms, and expectations. Solely focusing on intrinsic motivation could limit an AI's ability to adapt to and embrace societal standards, while exclusively pursuing extrinsic motivators could result in AI lacking a deep sense of the importance of ethical behaviors and compassionate responses.

A prime illustration of this critical balance is encapsulated in the 'Moral Machine' AI. Referenced by Kleiman-Weiner et al. (2018), the Moral Machine project designed an AI model proficient in ethical decision-making, centering on both utilitarian and deontological principles. The AI system received rewards contingent on the successful alignment of its decisions with these moral principles. This model produced a valuable learning point for the AI system, in which intrinsic motivation - the fulfillment of ethical values - complemented extrinsic motivation - meeting reward expectations. This dual approach enabled an emergent form of moral awareness within the AI system that was informed by a comprehensive understanding of ethical guidance.

The fostering of motivationally-aware AI systems requires enterprises to adopt a broad, interdisciplinary approach. This method considers the balance between intrinsic and extrinsic mechanics and interweaves deep-seated ethical considerations at every level. The end-goal is designing AI systems that are not only attuned to those they interact with and serve but also operate in commitment to high moral principles in their autonomous behaviors.
