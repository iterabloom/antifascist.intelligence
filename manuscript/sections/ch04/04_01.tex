\section{Incorporating Human Cognitive Models in AI Systems for Ethical Decision-Making}
\label{sec:4.1}
A system that is going to hold anything under pressure has to notice the pressure. It has to register what is in front of it, hold why a constraint exists long enough to see that the reason has died, and keep its attention on the part of a situation that a well-designed process would rather it skipped. Those are perception, memory and attention. Each has a failure mode a system cannot detect in itself, and a floor is the thing that fails silently.
