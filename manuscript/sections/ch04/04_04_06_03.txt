4.4.6.3. Strategic Recommendations for Ethical AI Development Against Authoritarianism
In light of our shared quest to design and implement AI systems that counteract authoritarianism while upholding democratic values and ethical standards, we encourage the adoption of the following comprehensive strategic recommendations:

1) Champion Interdisciplinary Collaboration: Prioritize funding for cross-disciplinary initiatives that merge expertise from AI researchers, ethicists, social scientists, legal scholars, and policymakers. This breadth of knowledge will provide a robust environment for AI systems to be rooted in ethical theories and evolving technical advancements, while simultaneously addressing any socio-political implications. Collaborative projects could range from co-developed research endeavours to shared technological platforms, and joint academic-industry workshops.

2) Encourage Transparency and Explainability in AI Systems: Support the development of comprehensible AI technology that can be easily understood by both experts and lay users alike. By making AI systems transparent and their operations explainable, we pave the way for public trust and enable rigorous examination of the technology's impact on democratic societies, thereby facilitating accountability.

3) Propel AI Literacy and Education: Pledge support to educational programmes that aim to bolster critical understanding and mastery of AI. By promoting AI training at different levels of the education cycle, from young learners to adult professionals, we ensure that our future societies are equipped to interact with AI technologies in ways that strengthen democratic institutions.

4) Frame Policies for Ethical AI Evolution: Implement both national and global norms and policies that mandate adherence to ethical standards in AI development. A strong policy framework must also emphasize transparency and discourage the potential misuse of AI systems for authoritarian purposes.

5) Safeguard Data Privacy and Security: Build robust computational infrastructure that ensures data are handled respectfully and confidentially, thereby protecting the privacy rights of individuals. Continuously updated cybersecurity protocols can further fortify these systems against breaches or manipulations by malicious activities, including authoritarian agents.

6) Involve Diverse Stakeholders in Policy Formulation: Give platforms to public and private players, as well as non-government organisations, to share their expertise, views, and concerns during the drafting of AI policies and guidelines. This collaboration should encourage democratic values and curb authoritarian inclinations.

7) Prevent Power Asynchrony: Curb the concentration of AI proficiency within a narrow set of organizations to eliminate the possibility of monopolistic practices and control by non-democratic entities. Regularized checks and robust regulatory frameworks must be developed to foster a competitive AI market space, conducive to innovation and democracy.

8) Foster Global Cooperation: Promote international partnerships that reinforce ethical guidelines, uphold democratic values, and center anti-authoritarian principles in AI research and innovation. It is crucial to establish universal norms that safeguard human rights, respect the sovereignty of democracies, and strengthen democratic institutions globally.

In adopting these strategic recommendations, we move towards creating anti-authoritarian AI systems that not only identify and counteract authoritarian tendencies but also protect and promote democratic values. Through ethical development, these systems will respect human rights and contribute to strengthening democratic structures, globally and locally.
