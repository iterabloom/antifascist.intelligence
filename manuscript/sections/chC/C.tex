\chapter{The Instruments, Translated}
\label{sec:C}
The floor takes its instruments entire, and a deployment translates them into terms for its own pipeline (Chapter~\ref{sec:5}). This appendix holds the translations the book runs: the Maven pipeline (\S\ref{sec:C.1}), the four Geneva Conventions and Additional Protocol I for that pipeline (\S\ref{sec:C.2}), and the abduction and forcible transfer pipeline, with the two tables chapter~\ref{sec:11} reads against it (\S\ref{sec:C.3}).
