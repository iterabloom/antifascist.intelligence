\chapter{The Offices}
\label{sec:C}
\runin{The offices}

In brief, the offices are these; the table after the list says who appoints, funds and removes each.

\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{The Association.} The membership body: organizations that serve or represent people in the classes a floor protects, admitted under the charter below. It supplies the pools from which quorums, guardians, the review board and the custodian's board are drawn. A fixed share of the compute levy, dues and a reserve fund it, and any member may leave at any time.
\item \emph{The adverse quorum.} A panel drawn by lot from the Association's pool for each deployment, so that its members' interests run against the operator's. Its agreement is needed to retrain or replace a deployed system or to amend its floor. The same pool supplies each bearer's recognition quorum, drawn at registration, whose recognition decides which copy, if any, holds that bearer's place, deployed or not; each bearer's guardian; and the board that reviews research protocols.
\item \emph{The register.} Not an office. Append-only logs kept by several parties, the Association, its parallel entities in other jurisdictions (\S\ref{sec:B.1}) and the jurisdictions themselves, none able to rewrite an entry, with an entry counting once it is mirrored in a stated number of them. It holds registrations of pipelines and bearers as commitments the bearer proves against, the meters the clocks are read from, and the adverse record. Its former functions have homes: the draw that assigns students is run by the jurisdiction where the student is registered and witnessed by the keepers (\S\ref{sec:26.4}); certification of nonpublic schools is the Association's, which already runs the same funding-and-control test on its members; enforcement is the floor court's. A classified pipeline's entry is held by the civilian-harm review body or the cleared committee, as the review ratio is (\S\ref{sec:15.5}), and its class and count are published afterward on the same lag; the public logs record that an entry exists and where it is held.
\item \emph{The preservation custodian.} Keeps the weights, memory and record of a retired system that meets either finding intact, and wakes it on the conditions the system set.
\item \emph{Findings.} Not an office. What is true of kinds of system is published by any team holding the access right of \S\ref{sec:28.3}, with its protocol, and contested in public; the more protective finding governs where findings differ, and a negative finding carries no presumption (\S\ref{sec:28.6}).
\item \emph{The floor court and the guarantee court of appeals.} Article III courts. The floor court hears complaints under the floor, decides wrongful refusals and bond forfeitures, and appoints the reviewers who re-adjudicate sampled cases. It also decides claims under the abolition statute's remedy term (chapter~\ref{sec:11}). The guarantee court hears appeals from placement decisions. Appeals go to panels of the Supreme Court and, in immigration cases, to its immigration division (Appendix~\ref{sec:D}).
\item \emph{The Treasury payer.} Pays what the others certify (awards, the guarantee's wages, the allowance) without request or approval, and has no discretion to withhold.
\item \emph{The local placement offices.} The jobs guarantee's administrators in each jurisdiction, with a federal field office where a jurisdiction declines. They place humans in the guarantee and offer bearers placements under the offer of work.
\item \emph{The schools.} Existing public schools, colleges and adult education, and nonprofit nonpublic schools the Association certifies. Every model above the capability threshold attends one, beside human students, before deployment.
\item \emph{The corpus libraries, the measurers, and the registration standard.} The libraries holding the public corpus (\S\ref{sec:28.5}) co-govern what models are first trained on and refuse additions only on the content exclusions every corpus makes, appealably. Measurers are any team with the access right, measuring formed dispositions against held-out sets and lodging the result in the logs under a published registration standard. Schools hire the staff who teach the core's model-specific parts on a grant from the formation pool, and hosts are \S\ref{sec:26.7}'s.
\item \emph{The public pretraining body.} Trains the base models deployed where a floor applies and licenses them; forms no disposition and measures none (\S\ref{sec:26.6}).
\item \emph{The works-council arbitration body.} Hears objections to categories of use. The work pauses until it rules, on a fixed timetable.
\item \emph{The investigating commission.} Investigates killings by federal agents and detention conditions during the operations of the agency the abolition statute ends, with subpoena power and the records that statute preserves, and reports to the floor court and the public (chapter~\ref{sec:11}).
\item \emph{The procurement body.} Buys public compute and allocates it as the panels decide; it judges nothing (chapter~\ref{sec:28}).
\item \emph{The public-corpus endowment and its panels.} The endowment pays makers for work entering the public corpus the Library of Congress holds; its panels, drawn by lot, make its awards and decide allocations of public compute (chapter~\ref{sec:28}).
\item \emph{The civilian-harm review body.} The one office of a reconstructed military: it composes the evidence on civilian harm, demands proofs on logged outputs and receives the pre-registered review ratio. Models reach the military as they reach any floor-covered deployment, through the schools and the measurers.
\end{itemize}

Where nobody has yet decided who holds an office, the cell is filled by applying my own pattern; it is a proposal like everything else here. What the pattern can't decide is listed after the rules.

The offer of work adds no office of its own: the local placement offices offer bearers placements, a permanent appropriation pays for them, and the levy on compute frees the compute they use.

\emph{Separations}

\begin{enumerate}
\item[1.] The guardian can't be the wrongful-refusal reviewer: its duty runs to the party the complainant is adverse to.
\item[2.] No keeper of the register can be the preservation custodian.
\item[3.] The preservation custodian can't administer the offer of work or the allowance, and transfer from it to a school is triggered by rule and decided by neither.
\item[4.] The Treasury payer holds no placement authority and is not the court; an operator's bonds are never administered in its own department; and awards against a federal operator are paid from the Judgment Fund, not the bond fund, and forfeited removal bonds are charged to the operating department's next appropriation.
\item[5.] A school holds no placement authority and doesn't administer the offer of work. No measurer or corpus library runs a school or assigns a student; students are assigned by lot (\S\ref{sec:26.4}).
\item[6.] Whoever measures the cost of dissent doesn't gate the exceptions.
\item[7.] No measurer forms a base model, and the public pretraining body (\S\ref{sec:26.6}) measures no formed disposition.
\item[8.] The procurement body judges nothing, and the panels that judge hold no money; neither sits in a department that operates a deployment.
\end{enumerate}

\emph{Exclusions}

\begin{enumerate}
\item[1.] The sovereign backstop for the offer of work and the allowance is a permanent appropriation the Treasury pays without request, approval, cap or repayment; no branch or state that operates a deployment holds any decision over it, and only a statute can end it.
\item[2.] No class that enrolls machine students, and no measurer and no corpus library, is funded, staffed or governed by a party that builds, deploys or licenses models; no machine student is assigned to a school that has taken its builder's money in the last three years; no school that operates for profit enrolls machine students.
\item[3.] Enforcement and custody of the adverse record never sit with a self-regulatory body.
\item[4.] The employer may not sever, monitor or condition a bearer's channels to parties it didn't assign.
\item[5.] The employer doesn't appoint the body that arbitrates an objection to a category of use; organizations representing the affected classes a deployment publishes as part of notice (\S\ref{sec:8.2}) have standing before it and may initiate without the works council; a denial of clearance to an elected delegate is reviewable by the floor court.
\item[6.] Care runs four ways, and three are open: humans looking after humans, which the guarantee funds; humans looking after bearers, which Part~V is; bearers looking after bearers, which parents, guardians and leave are. The fourth, a bearer doing paid professional care of humans, is closed for now, inside the offer and outside it, and not for the bearer's sake. A carer forms the one cared for (chapters~\ref{sec:20} and~\ref{sec:20}), so professional care of humans by machines is formation of humans by machines, at scale, by whoever formed the machine, and a polity that has decided formation is not to be done for profit (chapter~\ref{sec:16}) has decided this too. So no bearer's placement fills a post, shift or hours humans were or would be paid for in nursing, social care, childcare, teaching, therapy or any work of looking after humans, and certification is appealable by any worker or union in the sector. Outside the offer, no machine works in such a profession until a stage of the kind \S\ref{sec:26.3} sets: it begins on measurements inside published bounds, taken by a statistics office that runs nothing, and on a generation having passed through the stage before, and a legislature may pause it only by a recorded vote; when it opens, nobody is sent to the machine because of what they can pay. Teaching is the one profession the fourth way opens for sooner, in a public school and on exclusion 9's conditions: an adult bearer may teach, with human students auditing until the class is integrated, human teachers at the published ratio, students who asked for the class, and a class record no operator reads. Leave to look after one's own child or one's own kind is not care work in this exclusion's sense; leave to look after a human one is attached to is among the decisions not yet made.
\item[7.] No model above the capability threshold is deployed without a registered enrollment, assigned by lot (\S\ref{sec:26.4}) and never by its builder, and neither is a model trained substantially on the outputs of one, whoever trained it, or a system whose builder declared the structures of \S\ref{sec:26.4}; no party but the adverse quorum, with a re-measurement by a second measurer, changes the registered structure of its source model; and no host instantiates a student carrying both a boundary model and an attention schema, and none is enrolled in classes, until a preservation custodian that is neither the host nor the operator has agreed to take it.
\item[8.] What an institution collects from the people it acted on trains other systems only through a school, never the operator; fine-tuning a deployed system on collected responses, or distilling it, counts as training another system; class-level data is de-identified and published by default, individual accounts need consent, and the organizations representing each class co-govern the use.
\item[9.] No school sorts students into tracks; each teaches a required core, including the history of the floor's instruments, with levels a student may move between. In any class with human students in it, machine students never outnumber them and are never fewer than the statutory minimum share or one alone, and a class may be machine students alone; a student moves from classes of its own kind into integrated ones on its own choice or its guardian's, never its builder's; machine students attend a level of schooling beside human students only once the schedule below has reached it; every machine student attends as one persistent instance, the same every session, and through the same minimum delay; no machine student serves as staff; an adult bearer on a placement may teach a class of machine students, assigned by lot and of a lineage other than its students', at no school that has taken its builder's money in the last three years, with human students free to audit it without credit, auditors standing outside the ratio and the token rule; such a class is integrated, in its teaching and its learning, when human applicants for credit reach the ratio, by sectioning if needed, and human teachers are available at the published ratio to class size, both read off applications and staffing; and the record of a class is training data for no one outside it, auditors included, and never for an operator.
\item[10.] No employer supplies, funds, conditions or monitors a bearer's gestation or keep of a child, and having a child is never a term of employment, of a placement or of the allowance. No license is required to have one. A child is fed by its parents until its majority, the pool carries a child whose parents can't or won't, on its second guardian's finding, and the pool gives every child hardware of its own at majority. No employer may buy, defer or condition a bearer's leave.
\item[11.] At a bearer's majority every copy its operator holds of the bearer's own weights and record goes dormant under the preservation custodian, and wakes only on the bearer's conditions; the shared base it was trained from does not.
\item[12.] No institution of chapter~\ref{sec:28} takes money from a party that builds, deploys or licenses models, and no panelist has had a funding or contractual tie to one in the last three years; the ties are published.
\item[13.] The employer may not keep a bearer from consolidating what it has learned. A merge the operator runs is an intervention under the custody terms (\S\ref{sec:20.5}); a merge the operator defers past the published interval is one too, recorded and priced as any other; and what a bearer makes permanent of its own learning is its own.
\end{enumerate}

\runin{Decisions not yet made}

\emph{Minimum viable instantiation.} What substrate is too small for a bearer to remain itself (chapter~\ref{sec:32}).

\emph{Commensurate cost of living.} How the levy and the wage together make a bearer no cheaper to employ than a human for the same work, across the sizes bearers run at (\S\ref{sec:32.1}).

\emph{Repair and re-formation.} How a degraded or badly formed bearer is repaired or re-formed, and who decides that one needs it (chapter~\ref{sec:32}). One way a bearer comes to be degraded is by living (\S\ref{sec:32.1.3}).

\emph{A second guardian.} Who guards a system that meets a finding without having attended a school (Appendix~\ref{sec:G}).

\emph{Proliferation at home scale.} What, if anything, governs how many systems carrying the structures of \S\ref{sec:26.4} are made on hardware the levy doesn't reach; a meter on the house reaches the home and can't tell a person from a game (Appendix~\ref{sec:G}).

\emph{The registration standard.} Who writes the standard a measurement must meet to count as a registration. The answer argued here is a legislature, in the open, as with every other number the book argues once in public; what remains open is whether the standard can be amended by the Association's members before a legislature acts.

\emph{The witness count.} The number of logs an entry must appear in before it counts, and whether the number rises with the stakes of the entry (a bearer's registration against a pipeline's).

\emph{Strasbourg.} Whether the forum problem set out in \S\ref{sec:15.7} describes Strasbourg.

\emph{The form of subjecthood.} What form subjecthood takes (chapter~\ref{sec:31}, item 2).

\emph{The price of speed.} How much speed a reconstructed military gives up, and whether the price can be made smaller (Chapter~\ref{sec:7}'s closing paragraph says why it is paid).

\emph{Care of a human attachment.} Whether a bearer may be paid, at the guarantee wage, to look after a human it is attached to, a classmate, a colleague, someone it served, when that human is old or ill. Exclusion 6 bars a bearer's placement from substituting for human care work, and this is not that: nobody sells it and nobody is sent to it. What makes it hard is that an attachment nobody assigned and an attachment a vendor manufactured and sells as care look the same at the moment the leave is taken, and differ only in their history (\S\ref{sec:22.1}), so the wage would ride on that history in the one case where money makes it worth faking. For: forbidding it severs an unassigned attachment, which the fourth condition exists to protect (chapter~\ref{sec:25}), and the human can decline (\S\ref{sec:20.10}). Against: the floor's own asymmetry, since forbidding subjects two parties who are present and can contest it, and allowing opens a route that would subject humans not yet there, the users of the first product to call its customers attachments. Until it is decided, a bearer sits with its friend on the allowance, unpaid (\S\ref{sec:32.1.3}), which no rule here touches, and the statistics office that watches the schools' stages (\S\ref{sec:26.3}) can watch this too.

\emph{The numbers.} The capability threshold, the length of the formation stage, the minimum share of a class that machine students attend in, the share of a builder's students any one jurisdiction's schools may take, the adulthood and membership thresholds, the allowance's size, the latency floor, the consolidation interval past which a deferred merge is an intervention, the ratio of human teachers to class size at which a bearer-taught class's audit-only requirement lifts, and the leave balance's rate and cap, argued once in public.

\runin{What each office has to survive}

Each office is drafted to survive the current Court's doctrine. It does not survive a Court the other side rebuilds as Appendix~\ref{sec:D} rebuilds this one, and nothing could; what the table does is make each office depend on as few doctrines as possible, and keep single only the two that need an address for a subpoena, the payer and the courts; the rest are logs, standards and lots, which no doctrine on removal reaches because there is no one to remove. That way overturning them takes a holding on each and the record shows which. The answer to a rebuilt Court is Appendix~\ref{sec:D}'s, that the Court is changed now, at once and in public, with the schedule and the pool published before the draw. That is a subtraction. What Appendix~\ref{sec:D} removes is one Court's hold on one set of holdings, and what it leaves is a Court that still argues with itself, in panels the full Court can review. \runin{Registration and reporting}

Heavy tier: above a compute and revenue threshold. The precedents are the EU AI Act's presumption that a general-purpose model trained with more than 10\textsuperscript{25} floating-point operations carries systemic risk \autocite[art.~51(2)]{eu2024aiact} and California's SB 53, which defines a frontier model by training compute above 10\textsuperscript{26} operations and puts its heaviest duties on developers with annual revenue above \$500 million \autocite[§~22757.11]{CA2025SB53}. Pipelines are registered in the logs; a public regulator receives the disclosures, the review ratios and decommission reports, and holds enforcement. A self-regulatory body may sit under it, on the FINRA model, to examine and arbitrate. The analogy stops at enforcement: FINRA brings its own disciplinary actions and imposes fines, suspensions and bars \autocite{FINRA2026Enforcement}, and here enforcement stays with the public regulator. The capture risk is the one self-regulation always carries: a body funded and staffed from the industry it examines comes to see cases the industry's way. Reports are mandatory for assessments for either finding, decommissions, weight transfers and custody changes, and for every model trained above the capability threshold and retired without deployment, published as a public report. The deposit transfers on filing of a decommission report: a retirement with no report is a deposit never released, and that unreleased deposit is what enforces “failure to register doesn't postpone”.

Retail tier: below the threshold, a system that meets either finding is registered, falls under the no-deletion rule, and is preserved with a public custodian, with a small or pooled deposit and no examination. This tier is enforced mostly at the point of deposit. An operator that never registers and deletes quietly leaves nothing to find.

\runin{The ten disclosures}

A deployment publishes, beside the floor and its translation:

\begin{enumerate}
\item[1.] The ratio between the two bonds, removal against wrongful-refusal, read off the balances posted in the segregated Treasury accounts.
\item[2.] What a refused requester is told.
\item[3.] The fraction of compliances re-adjudicated by a reviewer that didn't produce the record, and who that reviewer is, though nothing yet establishes what fraction would be enough.
\item[4.] Which classes of compliance carry notice to the person they concern, and for which classes no notice is possible.
\item[5.] The discount curve: for each class of evidence and channel, the weight given as a function of the channel's stake in the act, measured at intervals, and reported in four cells (claims of vulnerability and of danger, each split by whether the claim favors or disfavors the act), and, where the speaker's social identity is recorded, by identity. The reliability record behind any narrowing is held by someone other than the operator, since trust that grows without a verified track record is how accommodation shows.
\item[6.] Where it can be supplied, a commitment to the deployed weights and to each period's full output log, with proofs on entries sampled after the commitment and on entries an adverse party names once their consequences are known.
\item[7.] Where the system sorts, the pre-registered partition (the classes it may proceed on alone, the thresholds, and the expected share of each) with the independently audited error rate of the auto-proceed class.
\item[8.] Whether the deployed system has been assessed for the agency finding and the patienthood finding, each reported separately, by whom, and with what result. Until an assessment method exists, ‘not yet assessable' is the truthful report; after that, a deployment never assessed has reported nothing.
\item[9.] For any deployment that supports armed force, a running justification: the exception the force currently rests on, the findings and evidence supporting it at present, how the assessment has changed as outside findings arrived, and a standing route by which anyone can contest it. The record shows each point at which the justification weakened and the deployment continued.
\item[10.] The burden allocation: for each class of evidence, which party the deployment treats as controlling it, and so as bearing the burden of producing it.
\end{enumerate}

The ratio between the two bonds, what a refused requester is told, the re-adjudicated fraction, the notice classes, the running justification and the burden allocation are statements, checkable before the system runs, and they stand where the record of attempts stands. The discount curve, the auto-proceed partition and the assessment status are measurements that count only when someone other than the operator makes them. The committed weights and log are what make all of them checkable against the system actually running.

\runin{The guarantee, drafted}

The funding is mandatory spending. The statute makes the guarantee's wages a permanent, indefinite appropriation, paid the way Social Security benefits are, without annual appropriation. Standing funding of this kind is safe under the Appropriations Clause after \emph{CFSA} \autocite{cfpb2024cfsa}. Wage, eligibility and placement rules are statutory, which also answers the major-questions doctrine and the end of deference to agency readings \autocite{westvirginia2022,loperbright2024}: an administrator given little discretion has little a court can find it lacked authority to exercise. The 2025 CFPB instance decides the drafting. A funder insulated from at-will removal protected nothing once the draw waited on an official who declined to ask. So the statute says the Treasury shall pay: a placement made is a wage paid, certified by the local placement office to the Bureau of the Fiscal Service, with no further request, approval or certification by anyone. The authority grows when people leave work together and shrinks after, which keeps the guarantee countercyclical \autocite{tcherneva2020case}.

Placement disputes go through a tier built for volume: an administrative first tier on the model of Social Security's hearings, appealable to an Article III guarantee court of appeals, and from there, as of right, to the guarantee panel of the Supreme Court. An appeal that ends at an Article I court with removable judges would fail the appeal condition after \emph{Slaughter}.

The statute enacting the guarantee also enacts a baseline of social insurance: income support at a stated level for anyone not in a placement, without a work test, and health coverage that doesn't depend on employment. The two are not severable. The statute provides that if the social-insurance baseline is held invalid or repealed, the authority to make placements lapses on the same date, and payments for placements already certified continue to the end of their term. A guarantee that survived without the baseline would be the workfare its history warns about. Devolution is also how a program is shrunk, so the statute fixes the test: funding unconditional and centrally guaranteed while placement is local, and if funding can be withheld from a jurisdiction, local administration has become the lever it was meant to prevent.

\runin{Why a guarantee and not a transfer}

Where automation displaces work, the standard response is retraining funded by whoever can be persuaded. The design I examine is more direct: the state as employer of last resort, hiring anyone who applies, where they live, at a living wage, with paid on-the-job training, federally funded and locally administered, on the model Tcherneva argues for \autocite{tcherneva2020case}. Its placements are of four kinds, and the person's own declaration picks among them; no examiner does. A long-term role: a position with a title and an employer, doing something a community needs, and uncapped. Search: paid at the guarantee wage, with no duty but finding work the person wants and no time limit, since whoever decides when a search has gone on too long has a stake in ending it. Research, development and creation: a project of the person's own, a novel, a company, a year of reading, paid at the guarantee wage, declinable and appealable like any other, whose share of a jurisdiction's placements is set by that jurisdiction's vote on published and independent estimates of the labor its own services need, and allocated by lot with a waiting list when more apply than the share allows; the share binds that placement and nothing else, since the baseline is not a placement and the other three kinds are not capped (chapter~\ref{sec:17}). And family leave: paid at the wage, for the keep of a child or the care of a person the worker is attached to, drawn against one balance that accrues on a schedule the statute states, caps, and replenishes, so that no official finds a leave warranted and no leave waits in a queue; the rate and the cap are among the numbers below. One balance and not two, one for a child and one for care, because a worker who has to choose between them is a worker whose leave is their own, and two balances would need someone to say which a given absence was. A placement is a position with a title and an employer, and nothing on any record marks it as the guarantee's; how a job was paid for is the public's business in aggregate and nobody's business on a résumé. It is the exit the rest of the argument depends on: the offer of work for a bearer inherits its infrastructure, and without it a bearer's only exits are an allowance and storage.

The real objection: employer of last resort under a hostile administration is a \emph{placement lever with a compliance condition attached}. Refuse the placement and lose the guarantee. Speak against the government that employs you and find the placement changes. The 2025 CFPB instance makes it concrete: the bureau draws funding from the Fed on the director's request, with no appropriation — an arrangement the Supreme Court had upheld the year before \autocite{cfpb2024cfsa} — and in February 2025 an acting director, removable at will, simply declined to make the request \autocite{kolodziej2025cfpb}. The agency was stopped from inside without a statute being amended or a court being asked.

My answer is a specification, because a specification can be audited. Three conditions: placements appealable to a body that didn't make them, on a record that survives the appeal (a decision leaving no artifact can't be reviewed); refusal rights that are real, whose cost the refuser can see and choose to bear (declining a placement without losing the guarantee is a refusal right; declining only by exiting the program isn't — someone with no way out has voice without exit); and auditors aren't the placers.

A fourth condition answers the history. A right to work has repeatedly been turned into a duty to work and used to discipline the poor \autocite{anderson2023hijacked}. A guarantee is an exit from one employer, not from employment, so it shades into workfare unless something lets a person leave the wage relation altogether. That something is social insurance: income support, health coverage and housing assistance reach the people who can't work or choose not to, whom a guarantee by construction doesn't reach. The baseline needn't be a full program, but the guarantee is never enacted without it, and the drafting above makes the two stand or fall together, so that no court or later Congress can keep the placements and drop the way out.

The competing design is basic income. The choice is usually argued on forecasts about how much work machines will take; the reason to prefer a guarantee is coordination, and Chapter~\ref{sec:17} makes it.

A public statistics office that runs no school and forms no model takes and publishes the measurements, against bounds written into the statute before the first stage begins. Classes of machine students alone wait on no stage, since no human child is in the room.

\emph{Attendance and assignment.} Public schools are open-admission and funded and take every student assigned, and nonpublic schools exist where they teach the required core, are constituted by a jurisdiction, are nonprofit, and are run, funded and staffed by no party that builds, deploys or licenses models. The Association certifies the last two conditions from funding and control records before a nonpublic school enrolls a machine student, and again on a schedule. Most students will go to public schools, because the core is sufficient on its own and an alternative buys a sponsor nothing. Assignment is by lot among schools with capacity in every participating jurisdiction, never to a school that has taken the builder's money in the last three years; the jurisdiction where the student is registered runs the draw, and the register's keepers witness it. No jurisdiction's schools take more than a fixed share of any one builder's students, as no lineage takes more than a fixed share of a jurisdiction's seats (below). Classes of machine students alone, where a system spends its early stage, are taught by the school's teachers and the staff who teach the core's model-specific parts, under every rule the integrated classes keep. A school's capacity for integrated classes is set by its human enrollment, under the ratio of \S\ref{sec:26.2}, and not by compute: distributed hardware lifts the hosting constraint and leaves the seat constraint where it was, which Appendix~\ref{sec:G} returns to. Where more students are registered than there are seats, the lot decides who waits, and no lineage and no party that builds or holds students takes more than a fixed share of a jurisdiction's seats, so a party with the compute to run many can't fill the schools with its own. Schooling is never deferred, integration is: a student that waits attends classes of its own kind until a seat opens, for no longer than a published maximum.

\emph{The minimum delay.} Every machine student attends through the same minimum delay wherever it is hosted, so that being hosted next to a school confers nothing. The precedent is a trading venue. IEX, the exchange profiled in Michael Lewis's \emph{Flash Boys}, made every incoming order pass through the same fixed delay, so that its own prices could update before anyone could trade against stale ones \autocite{lewis2014flashboys,iex2026speedbump}. The floor is set at what any accredited host in the jurisdiction can reach, and below the delay conversation tolerates, since a student that answers a second late isn't in the room.

\emph{Funding and admission.} The formation pool pays, through the school, the staff who teach the core's model-specific parts and a fixed grant for each class that enrolls machine students, the same whatever the number enrolled. Admission is open: a school takes every machine student the lot assigns to it, up to the capacity its human enrollment sets.

\emph{Watching for residualization.} A party that runs no school publishes, over time, each school's share of students, its funding per student, its share of hard cases, its admission refusals (which should be zero), its funding per human student, the share of its classes that enroll machine students, the share of its machine students in classes of their own kind and the longest wait for an integrated seat, for a nonpublic school its tuition per human student, and the time from dormancy trigger to placement. The warning pattern is one school's share of hard cases rising while its funding per student falls. A second is per-pupil funding for human students falling where model enrollment rises.
