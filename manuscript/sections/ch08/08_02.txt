8.2. Signatures of Altruistic and Antifascist Superintelligence
What follows is a set of milestones for recognizing whether the project this book describes is actually working, and a method for revising them when it turns out they weren't the right milestones. Section 8.2.1 sets out what those milestones look like across the research areas the book has covered; section 8.2.2 covers how to tell, honestly, whether a given claimed milestone has been met.
