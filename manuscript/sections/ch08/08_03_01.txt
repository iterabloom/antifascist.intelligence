8.3.1. Developing Shared Norms and Principles for Ethical AI and Anti-Authoritarian Sentience
Establishing global governance structures is essential to ensure that AI is developed and deployed in a manner that supports anti-authoritarianism and respects democratic principles. The creation of shared norms and principles that incorporate ethical considerations, human rights norms, and international legal principles can enhance transparency, public trust, and promote the common good.

International organizations, such as the United Nations, have a key role to play in funding collaboration between nations, offering support for the establishment of appropriate regulatory frameworks for AI development, and creating guidelines that address ethical, legal, and safety dimensions. By creating an environment in which different stakeholders – including governments, academia, industry, civil society and citizens – can share insights, experiences, and best practices on ethical AI development, these institutions can help shape standardized principles that adhere to democratic values and resist authoritarian tendencies.

A common set of principles for ethical AI and anti-authoritarian sentience should be cultivated, encompassing core values such as transparency, accountability, fairness, privacy, and human rights. These principles should also encourage open and inclusive dialogue among stakeholders, promote continual reassessment and refinement of AI systems, and incorporate diverse perspectives to avoid biases and ensure that AI benefits all populations. For example, AI systems should be developed with an inherent respect for human dignity, ensuring that human values and liberties are safeguarded rather than threatened by increasing machine autonomy.

Once shared norms and principles are established, international collaborative initiatives can be launched to put these values into practice, including AI research and development programs, ethical AI roadmaps, and public-private partnerships that focus on fostering altruistic and anti-authoritarian AI systems. Through such initiatives, nations can work together to create standardized methodologies for AI evaluation and validation, develop guidelines for public oversight, and design frameworks for international cooperation.

In order to maintain accountability and ensure that AI systems are adhering to democratic values, it is crucial to establish robust monitoring and assessment mechanisms. These mechanisms should gather input from diverse stakeholders, undertake periodic evaluation of AI systems, and adapt policies or regulatory measures when required. Regular reports documenting the progress, challenges, and implications of AI developments should be shared across nations to collectively learn from successes and failures.

In developing shared principles for ethical AI and anti-authoritarian sentience, it is necessary to recognize and incorporate the cultural, social and political differences between countries. While maintaining global standards, AI development should be responsive to local needs and promote inclusiveness. Simultaneously, international cooperation should focus on addressing global challenges and opportunities presented by AI, encouraging the development of AI systems that contribute positively to the collective good.

This coordinated approach can help to ensure that AI technologies respect democratic values and contribute positively to societies around the world.
