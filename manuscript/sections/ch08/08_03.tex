\section{The Political Economy of AI and Power}
\label{sec:8.3}
The most ardent proponents of concentrated power tend to be those who already have it. With concentrated power comes access to capital. Concentrating power is the default outcome of AI development, because absent any pushback, capital does an excellent job of recruiting the inputs needed for a capable AI system: compute, data, and talent. Resistance to market power is among the antifascist design goals named at the start of this book; this section works out one form it could take.
