8.3. Addressing the Unintended Consequences
Everything this book has argued for can be built correctly and still go wrong in ways nobody designed for. Section 8.3.1 covers the practice of surfacing a risk before it causes harm. Section 8.3.2 covers what to do once a specific harm — to workers, to patients, to students, to a democratic process — has actually been identified, building on the technical fixes chapter 5 already covers. Section 8.3.3 covers a narrower and more adversarial case than either: not an accident a design process failed to anticipate, but an authoritarian actor deliberately trying to compromise, repurpose, or disable a system built against exactly that.
