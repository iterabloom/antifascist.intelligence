8.3.2. Establishing International Oversight Mechanisms for AI Development and Deployment
International oversight mechanisms should:

(1) Encourage collaboration, transparency, and accountability among stakeholders: International oversight mechanisms should fund cooperation and open communication among AI researchers, developers, policymakers, and other stakeholders. By sharing information and collaborating on AI development efforts, stakeholders can address shared ethical and anti-authoritarian concerns more effectively. Additionally, these mechanisms should encourage organizations to be transparent about their AI development processes, facilitating public scrutiny, and ensuring accountability for their actions.

(2) Develop and enforce global ethical guidelines and standards for AI development: International organizations should collaboratively develop and enforce a set of global ethical guidelines and standards for AI development. These guidelines should reflect core principles of ethics, human rights, and democracy, incorporating lessons learned from interdisciplinary research on altruism, anti-authoritarianism, and AI ethics.

(3) Monitor AI development to identify and address potential risks: The oversight mechanisms should proactively monitor ongoing AI research and development efforts to identify potential risks associated with the proliferation of AI technologies. They should establish early-warning systems and coordinate preventive measures for mitigating these risks before they lead to unintended negative consequences or the erosion of democratic values.

(4) Facilitate international cooperation on AI safety and security: Establishing international oversight mechanisms can also foster collaboration on AI safety and security issues. By working together, nations can bolster the resilience of AI systems against potential misuse, cyberattacks, or other threats that may arise in the context of an increasingly complex and interconnected global landscape.

(5) Address the global distribution of AI benefits: International oversight mechanisms should strive to ensure that AI technologies are developed and deployed equitably, preventing undue concentration of power or resources. This may involve addressing the digital divide, promoting accessibility of AI technologies to developing nations, and supporting AI-enabled initiatives that benefit the global community and foster democratic values.

(6) Establish monitoring frameworks for AI legislation, regulation, and ethical guidelines: The international oversight mechanisms should also monitor the implementation and enforcement of AI-related legislation, regulation, and ethical guidelines. This includes assessing the effectiveness of existing legal frameworks, identifying areas for improvement, and promoting best practices and innovations in regulatory approaches that promote altruism and counter authoritarianism.

International oversight mechanisms for AI development and deployment can help ensure that AI technologies are developed and used ethically, adhering to anti-authoritarian principles, and promoting democratic values.
