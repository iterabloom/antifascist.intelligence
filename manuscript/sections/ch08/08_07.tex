\section{Outcomes under the three adversaries}
\label{sec:8.7}
The Minab table holds for a removal with the nouns changed, with two differences.

The first is in the vendor rows. The removal terms held by one vendor prevent nothing, since the lead goes to another contractor. Held by every vendor (Chapter~\ref{sec:9}), they prevent the removals the terms name until the agency runs a system without them, at the price of rebuilding what was built on the floored one.

The second is the notice row, and it is conditional. At Minab, notice and standing could only remedy afterward. Here they can act before the removal, because the person is there to be told, but only where the law makes the operator wait for the answer. Without a stay that binds by operation of law, and counsel to use it, the 2025 record is the outcome. A man with a lawyer, a pending claim and a hearing date was flown out on the evening a federal judge ordered the planes returned. Under an operator willing to carry out the statute, notice prevents. Under a captured operator, it raises the cost of outrunning it and leaves a record of each time the operator did. Under total capture it leaves only the record, which is why the sixth term moves the record out of the operator's reach before the agency ends.
