8.1. AI as a Strategic National Asset
As nations globally commit to the pursuit of technological superiority, the pivotal role of AI as a strategic national asset emerges. This dominance is sought not only for economic advancement but also in the context of national security, social services delivery, and fostering public policy goals. Considering the anti-authoritarian underpinnings of our research, recognizing AI's potential for enhancing democratic systems while also guarding against its misuse for authoritarian ends is necessary.
