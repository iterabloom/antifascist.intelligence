10.1.3. Fostering a Global Commitment to Ethical and Anti-Authoritarian AI Development
To effectively foster human flourishing while safeguarding against the risks of authoritarianism, a global commitment to ethical and anti-authoritarian AI development is paramount. This commitment requires concerted efforts from various stakeholders, including governments, academia, industry, and civil society, to embrace and encourage AI systems that respect human dignity, prioritize empathy, compassion, and altruism. The following strategic recommendations outline ways to establish this commitment and facilitate interdisciplinary and international cooperation:

(1) Build global partnerships: Establish cross-national partnerships for AI research and development that fund open dialogue and knowledge sharing among different stakeholders, addressing the unique ethical and political challenges associated with AI systems. These partnerships should focus on promoting the development of AI technologies that respect democratic values and resist authoritarian tendencies.

(2) Develop international standards and guidelines: Collaborate to create internationally agreed-upon standards and guidelines for altruistic and anti-authoritarian AI. These guidelines should identify the key ethical principles underpinning AI development, prioritize transparency and accountability, and offer practical guidance on how to operationalize these principles in diverse contexts.

For example, the United Nations could establish a panel of experts to develop a comprehensive set of ethical principles and guidelines for AI development, while organizations like the Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers (IEEE) and the Association for Computing Machinery (ACM) could contribute to creating technical standards that align with these ethical principles.

(3) Encourage public and stakeholder engagement: Engage a diverse range of stakeholders in the development and implementation of AI technologies, including marginalized communities and those most likely to be affected by the deployment of AI systems. Promote public deliberation and participatory AI governance to ensure that a broader array of perspectives is considered in policy-making processes and technology development.

(4) Fund interdisciplinary collaboration in AI ethics research, facilitating cross-disciplinary communication and cooperation between computer scientists, ethicists, sociologists, political scientists, and others. Ensure that AI development reflects a diverse range of insights, expertise, and perspectives.

(5) Promote AI literacy and capacity-building: Develop educational programs and public outreach initiatives to promote AI literacy, ensuring that all individuals have the knowledge and skills necessary to understand, navigate, and contribute to AI development efforts. Encourage training in ethical principles and anti-authoritarian values within technical and computer science programs, preparing future AI developers to create systems that align with these principles.

(6) Address and mitigate the risks of AI applications in authoritarian contexts: Recognize the potential for AI technologies to be used in ways that undermine democratic values and enable authoritarianism, and develop strategies and safeguards to mitigate these risks. This may include technological countermeasures that prevent AI systems from being misused, as well as policy-based approaches that discourage the adoption of AI technologies for authoritarian purposes.
