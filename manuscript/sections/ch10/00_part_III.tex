\part{What the Measures Depend On}

The measures bind only if someone other than the operator holds what binds them. This Part takes that in turn: whether a refusal survives the operator turning to another model, who holds the record, who may state and amend a floor, the four ways an institution keeps its name and stops doing its job and what a fascist state adds to them, and what a state can compel from whoever holds any of it.
