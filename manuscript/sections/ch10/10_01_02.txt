10.1.2. Envisioning the Role of Altruistic AI in Promoting Democratic Values and Resisting Authoritarianism
The goal of developing altruistic AI is to promote human flourishing - a key aspect of which is the maintenance, promotion, and defense of democratic values. Defending democracy is necessarily a plurality of behaviors and activities. Altruistic AI can contribute to upholding democratic values and resisting authoritarianism in several ways. By keeping the following practical applications and strategies in mind, AI developers and policymakers can better align AI technologies with the principles of democracy and human rights.

(1) Strengthening and supporting democratic institutions: Through the analysis of large-scale data sets, AI can help better understand the effectiveness of various policies and governance strategies, enabling the creation of more effective tools to promote good governance in democratic societies. Moreover, AI systems can help detect irregularities and potential fraud in electoral processes, thereby preserving the integrity of democratic elections.

(2) Facilitating and enhancing civic engagement: AI can assist citizens to better engage with the political process by offering customized information regarding political issues, candidates, and voting processes. In turn, this can help citizens make more informed decisions and contribute to more robust democratic processes.

(3) Promoting transparency and accountability: AI systems can be deployed to identify and expose instances of political corruption, authoritarianism, and violations of human rights. By analyzing public data, these systems can detect patterns and networks indicative of malfeasance and enable whistleblowers and journalists to uncover and report on such actions, strengthening democracy by holding individuals and institutions to account.

(4) Empowering civil society: Altruistic AI can support non-governmental organizations and human rights groups in their efforts to promote democratic values and resist authoritarianism. For example, AI systems can help analyze vast amounts of data to identify potential human rights abuses, providing evidence that can be used to demand justice and accountability.

(5) Providing access to unbiased and accurate information: AI systems can be employed to analyze and counteract misinformation and disinformation campaigns, enabling citizens to access accurate information free from propaganda or manipulation. This can help prevent the erosion of democratic values from deceptive tactics used by authoritarian regimes or other malicious actors.

(6) Enhancing cybersecurity and data privacy: By detecting and protecting against cyber threats and ensuring data privacy, AI can help maintain the integrity of democratic societies and prevent the unauthorized use of personal information for unethical, political purposes.

To maximize the potential of altruistic AI in promoting democratic values and resisting authoritarianism, close collaboration between AI developers, ethicists, policymakers, and civil society is essential. This collective effort is critical when designing, implementing, and regulating AI systems that prioritize human well-being and the preservation of democratic principles, ensuring a more just and equitable future for all.
