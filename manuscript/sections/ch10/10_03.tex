\section{Addressing the Unintended Consequences}
\label{sec:10.3}
Everything this book has argued for can be built correctly and still go wrong in ways nobody designed for. Section~\ref{sec:10.3.1} covers the practice of surfacing a risk before it causes harm. Section~\ref{sec:10.3.2} covers what to do once a specific harm — to workers, to patients, to students, to a democratic process — has actually been identified, building on the technical fixes chapter~\ref{sec:6} already covers. Section~\ref{sec:10.3.3} covers a narrower and more adversarial case than either: not an accident a design process failed to anticipate, but an authoritarian actor deliberately trying to compromise, repurpose, or disable a system built against exactly that.
