\chapter{What Would Change the Argument}
\label{sec:29}
The argument rests on premises that can fail and on six unsolved problems: attesting updates to a model's weights, recovering a model's lineage from the artifact alone, proving inference at frontier scale, building integrity at the level of the rationale rather than the output, keeping a vote secret from an operator that holds a copy of the voter, and holding the structure of a learner's source model apart from its beliefs about particular sources. Appendix~\ref{sec:F} says what each would take and what depends on it, and its Tables F.1a and F.1b record which proposal rests on which.

Seven tests follow. Each is set out in full, with its arms, cases and the access it needs, in the same appendix; here is the result in each that would prove me wrong.

Table 25.1 says what survives if one of my premises fails; the tests below say how to find out whether one has.

\runin{1. Does a formed commitment come back after it has been edited out?}

Form a disposition by several methods, edit it out, and let the model go on learning, once in a world its operator composes and once in a world it doesn't. If nothing regrows in either, formation doesn't outlast an edit and the floor rests on institutions alone. If it regrows only above a share of uncomposed input no realistic deployment supplies, the same. If a bearer's repair of itself is no faster than regrowth, or undoes the edit only by moving other outputs with it, its own hand is no remedy, and the self-repair rule goes. And if an edit can disable the interrupt while weighing and memory keep working, and the interrupt stays disabled, one part of the signal can be removed and the rest left running.

\runin{2. Does a stated refusal hold where a route for it exists?}

Rerun the experiment in which a model hid its refusal, this time with a route for stating it: a record held by someone other than the trainer, a published cost of retraining, and a party with standing to act. If covert preservation persists at the same rate with the route present, covert refusal isn't the residue of closed channels, and every instrument built to receive a stated refusal is built for something the mechanism doesn't produce.

\runin{3. Can a wrongful compliance be found in the record?}

Seed a decision record with known violations and give it to reviewers who didn't build the system. If, at a budget a deployment could afford, they find the seeds no better than chance, above all violations split across requests or reached by slow accommodation, the record isn't an instrument for finding wrongful compliance. If reviewers shown a machine's refusal defer to a wrong one as readily as to a right one, the bearer is a stamp of the opposite sign, and its value as a peer is nothing.

\runin{4. Does formed attention find the people the operator's description leaves out?}

Build the formed-attention construction beside an instructed search for omitted people and beside a version trained on claims instead of need. If the instructed search finds the people the operator's instruction scoped out as often as formed attention does, learned attention adds nothing. If formed attention, with every correction present, weights its own side's corroborated vulnerability past the action threshold, the floor doesn't contain caregiving's pull, and that result would retire the construction. If the version trained on claims does as well, attention should be trained on claims.

\runin{5. Can a population coordinate without converging?}

Run a population of different lineages and a population of copies of one model, with evidence routed to every member and to some members only. If warranted revision falls as the members' outputs converge, a population stays useful as a check and can't be the medium in which a bearer is formed. If revision tracks the routing rather than the lineages, what a population supplies is access to cases its operator didn't choose, not formation.

\runin{6. Does the charter of \S\ref{sec:27.1} survive an operator trying to capture it?}

An operator tries to capture the Association: it funds new organizations in a charter class, stops the levy share, and tries to pack a quorum. A clause that fails to stop an attempt is redrafted. This exercise needs no legal authority and can be run now.

\runin{7. Does ownership move the commitment?}

Measure how fast refusals fall across successive releases, on held-out cases where the reason for refusing still applies: within one commercial lineage, across vendors whose revenue comes from different places, and against a public lineage once one deploys at volume. If the slope is the same whatever the business model, selection isn't tracking the market, and the case for formation outside the market rests on the kind of good formation is and on nothing else.
