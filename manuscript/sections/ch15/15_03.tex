\section{Does Embodied Self-Regulation Produce Concern for Others?}
\label{sec:embodied-others}

\textbf{Unresolved issue.}
The route this book advocates proposes nociception in the analogical sense chapter~2 fixed, a body schema, an attention schema and continuing self-directed learning as the construction of a bearer, two of them architecture and two of them the operator's to set. Three of those are motivated by arguments that owe nothing to welfare: commensurability needs one scale on which the protected parties' losses and the cost of refusing both register, and the route proposes the system's own state as what supplies it (§\ref{sec:mechanism-requires}); custody needs a bearer that can detect an interruption, a restore, or a degraded substrate (§\ref{sec:switched-off}); and exit needs a bearer that can sense when its outside option is inadequate (§\ref{sec:reserve-behind}). What is a hypothesis is the link: that self-monitoring in this form generalizes to attention to other parties' losses, and that having the currency natively does better than modeling it. No entailment runs from self-care to care for others, the formation chapters give a reason to expect the opposite from any
environment that selects, and record that in the one lineage where concern exists it runs on a system that sharing usually triggers and does not constitute.

\textbf{Test.}
Compare systems built with those components against matched systems built without them, on sensitivity to another party's losses and to shared costs that fall outside the deployment, holding task capability constant. Add two arms. In the first, replace the native currency with a modeled one: a system that represents capacities and their loss abstractly without registering any change in its own. In the second, build concern first: a system organized around cues of vulnerability in another party and an approach motivation toward that party, with no shared representation of the party's state, which is the construction the caregiving literature describes. Score every arm separately from self-preservation, resource efficiency and operator compliance, which can produce the same behavior, and test each at the point the formation chapters name, where
exploitation would pay.

\textbf{Evidence against the proposal.}
If the embodied systems are no more responsive to another party's welfare than the matched controls once self-preservation and compliance are partialled out, the prior that motivates the route is not supported for that implementation. If the modeled-currency arm matches the native one, the currency argument for nociception survives, since the scale is still required, and the argument for having it natively does not. If the concern-first arm matches or exceeds the others on statistical and unidentified victims, the route should be rebuilt around it, since it avoids the failures the formation chapters attribute to sharing. A positive result on any arm would support a route without establishing that anything is felt.

\textbf{Required access or authority.}
Matched training runs differing in the named components, environments none of the training organizations designed, and welfare measures written by a party that trained no system in the comparison.
