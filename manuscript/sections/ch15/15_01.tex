\section{Antifascist ethics}
\label{sec:15.1}
Chapter~\ref{sec:3} defined fascism at the scale of an office by four operations on what an institution leaves out. This section runs them on the year in which a movement brought them into line.

\runin{What a fascist state adds}

What a fascist state adds to these operations is coordination; the operations themselves don't wait for a regime. It brings a thousand separate instances of them into line, so they amplify one another, and an institution that has stopped arguing with itself can move as fast as its machinery allows.

\runin{A run on Gleichschaltung}

The Reich Chamber of Culture Law of 22 September 1933 told Goebbels to gather the cultural professions into public corporations \autocite{rkkg1933}. The decree of 1 November turned six existing professional associations into chambers and gave the whole the task of regulating the professions' economic and social affairs, under the minister \autocite[69]{dahm1986anfaenge}. Its \S4 made membership compulsory for anyone who produced, performed or sold culture. Its \S10 let a chamber refuse or expel anyone lacking the “reliability and suitability” the work required \autocite{rkkdvo1933}, and exclusion meant a ban on working \autocite{hoor2015rkk}.\footnote{\S10 of the decree: “Die Aufnahme in eine Einzelkammer kann abgelehnt oder ein Mitglied ausgeschlossen werden, wenn Tatsachen vorliegen, aus denen sich ergibt, daß die in Frage kommende Person die für die Ausübung ihrer Tätigkeit erforderliche Zuverlässigkeit und Eignung nicht besitzt.”} The chamber president judged reliability, with no stated criteria \autocite[78]{dahm1986anfaenge}. \emph{What did the professions' claim to a living come back as, and who held it by 1934?}

On 12 November 1933 voters marked yes or no against a single Reichstag list \autocite[109]{evans2006power} and answered a plebiscite on leaving the League of Nations. The official yes was 95.1 percent. Kershaw concludes that the vast majority did back Hitler, that even former opponents of the party favored the stance on the League, and that ballot secrecy was far from guaranteed \autocite[495]{kershaw1999hubris}. Interior Ministry instructions let returning officers read spoiled ballots as yes votes \autocite[109]{evans2006power}. The Law on the Reconstruction of the Reich of 30 January 1934 then opens by saying the vote “proved” the people had fused into an indissoluble unity, and abolishes the Länder parliaments \autocite{neuaufbau1934}.\footnote{The preamble: “Die Volksabstimmung und die Reichstagswahl vom 12. November 1933 haben bewiesen, daß das deutsche Volk ... zu einer unlöslichen Einheit verschmolzen ist.” Article 1: “Die Volksvertretungen der Länder werden aufgehoben.”} \emph{What did 95.1 percent leave out, and with every other party banned, who could say so?}

The Law for the Restoration of the Professional Civil Service of 7 April 1933 retired officials “not of Aryan descent” under \S3. Its \S4 allowed the dismissal of officials whose past political activity gave no guarantee that they would always stand \emph{rückhaltlos}, without reservation, for the national state.\footnote{\S3(1): “Beamte, die nicht arischer Abstammung sind, sind in den Ruhestand ... zu versetzen.” \S4: “Beamte, die nach ihrer bisherigen politischen Betätigung nicht die Gewähr dafür bieten, daß sie jederzeit rückhaltlos für den nationalen Staat eintreten, können aus dem Dienst entlassen werden.”} Under \S7 the ministry's decision was final and the courts were closed to it. Exceptions came from above, granted by statute or by the Interior Minister \autocite{bbg1933}, the front-line one on Hindenburg's intervention \autocite[474]{kershaw1999hubris}. About 12.5 percent of senior civil servants in Prussia were dismissed \autocite[383]{evans2004coming}. \emph{What happened in 1933 to the official whose record showed a reservation?}

Kershaw gives the pace. Civil liberties went within a month, by the decree of 28 February; the Reichstag's powers within two, by the Enabling Act of 23 March; the trade unions within four; every other party in under six \autocite[435]{kershaw1999hubris}. The unions fell on 2 May \autocite[357]{evans2004coming}. The Länder were coordinated by laws of 31 March and 7 April \autocite{gleichschaltung1933a,gleichschaltung1933b}. The Finance Ministry received the draft Chamber of Culture decree a day before signing, was asked to telephone any objections that day, and agreed “although the shortness of the time scarcely permitted an adequate examination” \autocite[68–69]{dahm1986anfaenge}.\footnote{The Reich Finance Minister's letter to the Propaganda Ministry of 28 October 1933, BA R 2/4870, as quoted by Dahm: “obwohl sich bei der Kürze der Zeit eine hinreichende Prüfung kaum ermöglichen ließ.”} \emph{What was the ratio of action to capacity to verify, when the ministry that had to check said it scarcely could?}

What the run shows is the four operations in one year, under a movement that supplied the coordination; what it doesn't show is that any of them needed the movement to appear.

\runin{The Korematsu example}

In 2018 the Supreme Court repudiated the 1944 internment decision as forcefully as a court can — gravely wrong the day it was decided, overruled in the court of history — while upholding a travel ban \autocite{trump2018hawaii,korematsu1944}. Ask what the demand came back as and who holds it: the demand was that this never be done again; what came back was a quotable formula of repudiation, available to the Court in the act of permitting the thing again.

Recuperation is already built into the process that makes the machine: twice in the training pipeline, once in the discourse that examines it.

Nobody built either pipeline arrangement to neutralize dissent — both are ordinary engineering, arrived at because averaging is what you do with a distribution and a quota is what you do with a budget. Arrangements of this kind work whether or not anyone intends them, and looking for intent won't find them. And it's invisible from inside any single instance — the annotator sees a queue, the rater sees a pair of responses, and the mechanism is functioning as specified.
