\section{Which schools count}
\label{sec:26.11}
They are the public schools, colleges and adult education a jurisdiction already runs, and the nonprofit nonpublic schools the registrar certifies, funded as institutions and not per student, and none is held by the branch that operates military deployments. No school holds placement authority. A machine student is never staff: no school may use one as a teacher, tutor or assistant, since a student that teaches is being used by the school that is supposed to be forming it. An adult bearer is another matter, and \S\ref{sec:26.2} says on what conditions one may teach.
