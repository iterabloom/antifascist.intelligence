\part{If They Can Be Wronged}

Everything here is conditional on findings nobody can yet make. If a system can be wronged, it is owed a guardian, preservation, memory its host can't read, and a life that doesn't run through its employer. If it also comes to answer for what it does, it is owed a place in the polity that formed it. It is written now because terms drafted after the need arises are drafted by whoever has the need.

They are still drafted by an interested party. The parties they concern can't take part in writing them, and a human writing terms for beings who don't yet exist breaks the own-case rule. So every term in this part is a proposal, to be revised by the first parties it would bind. The first stage of representation exists partly to give them a way to revise it.
