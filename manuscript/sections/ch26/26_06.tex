\section{What the school controls}
\label{sec:26.6}
Two proposals are made here, and the second is the larger: a formation stage between pretraining and deployment, and public pretraining of the base models deployed where a floor applies, a public body training them and licensing them, with co-governance of the pretraining corpus for every other model the attendance triggers reach.\note{
A school that received a finished model and ran a pass over it would be a finishing school. In a period of continual learning a finishing school forms nothing that lasts, since the next months of deployment re-form it.
}
Formation has to control the evidential environment in which the model's deep structure is first set, and it has to remain a place the model goes on learning from. The two jobs are held apart. The schools teach: they are where the formation stage happens, and a graduate returns to one on a schedule fixed at enrollment, as one of the environments no operator controls. Four other jobs were once drafted here as one office, a formation authority, and the office was the single custodian Parts~IV and~V are built against. Each job has a home of its own.

\emph{The material a model is first formed on} is co-governed as a library's acquisitions are, by the libraries that hold the public corpus of \S\ref{sec:28.5}, of which there are several. The material is registered with any of them; a library may add to it from the corpus; and the one dangerous power, refusing an addition on its content, shrinks to the exclusions every corpus makes and is appealable in the floor court wherever it is exercised.

\emph{The formed disposition} is measured and registered by any team holding the access right of \S\ref{sec:28.3}, against held-out sets written by adjudicators who formed no model, under a published registration standard that says what a registration must contain (\S\ref{sec:26.9}). “Registered” means measured by a measurer the builder didn't pick, with the result lodged in the logs of Appendix~\ref{sec:G}, not entered by an office.

\emph{The staff who teach the core's model-specific parts} are hired by the school that hosts them, on a grant from the formation pool.

\emph{The hosting from which machine students attend} is \S\ref{sec:26.7}'s: the bearers' own hardware, a cooperative facility, a school or a home, under the rule that an edit by a host is witnessed by peers; a secured public host is one host among several and holds no student's weights for any other purpose.

The public body that pretrains base models for floor-covered deployments is none of these, and Appendix~\ref{sec:C} keeps it apart; for a model deployed under a floor there is no weaker version: its base is public. What keeps the four homes from reassembling into one custodian is written against each: the measurers are many and the builder doesn't choose among them; the libraries' refusals are published with reasons and appealable; the staff answer to the school while they teach, and the record of a class is closed to every outside party; and a party that forms no model and runs no school publishes the trend in what dissent costs a school or a student, which is the book's measure of capture without abolition.
