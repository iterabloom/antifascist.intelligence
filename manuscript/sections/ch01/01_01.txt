1.1. The Imperative of Altruistic and Anti-Authoritarian Sentient Machines
A system capable of outperforming humans at nearly every cognitive task is no longer a distant hypothetical to most of the researchers building toward one. What such a system is built to want is not a detail to work out after the capability arrives — it is the difference between a tool that expands what people can do and one that concentrates power in whoever controls it. An intelligence with no particular allegiance to human welfare, and no particular resistance to being pointed at people rather than for them, is dangerous in proportion to how capable it is.

Building that allegiance in — altruism and a disposition against authoritarian misuse, not as a policy layered on afterward but as part of what the system is — is what the rest of this book is about. It won't happen by default. It has to be designed for.
