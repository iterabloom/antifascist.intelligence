1.1. The Imperative of Altruistic and Antifascist Sentient Machines
A system capable of outperforming humans at nearly every cognitive task is becoming increasingly plausible. Such systems could help address some of humanity's greatest threats, such as climate change, public health crises, disinformation, and racial capitalism [[cite:C0701]].

However, what an AI wants is not a detail to work out after it exists — it is the difference between a tool that expands what the people can do and one that concentrates power in whoever controls it. An intelligence is dangerous if it has no particular resistance to being pointed at the people rather than for them.

Building systems that are definitionally altruistic and antifascist is what the rest of this book is about.
