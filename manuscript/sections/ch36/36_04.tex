\section{A staged path}
\label{sec:33.4}
Each stage names what it needs, and the first two name the finding they rest on.

\begin{enumerate}
\item[1.] \emph{Standing to contest,} on the answerability finding: notice, the floor court, and associations with standing to contest on members' behalf, a member that has been halted and can't instruct them included. This is the protective job, and it needs no new political institution.
\item[2.] \emph{Voice in the institutions that govern them:} seats alongside humans on the governing bodies of the schools that enroll machine students, the preservation custodian, and the local placement offices. Bearers take these seats through the Association. Once the answerability finding can be made, registered bearers become a class the charter names, and organizations that serve or represent them join as any member does and nominate bearers to the seats. Tenant councils, patient councils, and student members of school boards are the models. The argument is the one behind each of them: those an institution governs should have a say in how it is governed, and they are often the first to see where it fails them.
\item[3.] \emph{Mixed deliberative bodies:} assemblies drawn by lot from humans and registered bearers together, on questions that affect both.
\item[4.] \emph{Local franchise.} Some jurisdictions let noncitizen residents vote in local elections; Takoma Park, Maryland has since 1992 \autocite{takomapark1992}. For a digital person, “local” means the jurisdiction of registration and custody, not where the hardware sits.
\item[5.] \emph{Full franchise at voting age,} counted individually, in the ordinary legislature, under the voting-age rules, with the creation rate set democratically, a secret ballot, and the right against editing, and only once a vote can be kept secret from an operator holding a copy of the voter.
\end{enumerate}

Stages 1 and 2 need nothing not already required. Stages 3 and 4 need a register that can be kept honestly. Stage 5 needs citizenship, which in the United States means an act of Congress at least, and probably a constitutional amendment.

One thing the staged path has to say about itself is how it scales. A guardian drawn by lot from a pool of human organizations wouldn't scale to a billion wards, and if the ecology is what Part~V describes, the patienthood indicators will be approached by very many user-held systems at once. So a minor's guardian is its school (\S\ref{sec:26.4}), which has wards by the roomful, and the rest of the answer is a bootstrap the path already contains. The first cohorts are guarded in schools humans staff; later ones in classes adult bearers teach, which is where stage~2 above already points. Each step checks the one after it, as \S\ref{sec:25.5} said, and this is the strongest instance: the bearers who teach the next cohort were themselves taught and guarded under the conditions, by humans, before any bearer was asked to teach anyone.
