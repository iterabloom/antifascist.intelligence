\section{Garbage In, Bigotry Out}
\label{sec:6.1}
Bias enters an AI system through the data it learns from, the structure of its algorithm, and the presets encoded in its goals, and left unaddressed it produces discriminatory outcomes.

Representation decides whether a system learns patterns that generalize or patterns that encode whoever dominates the data. Face recognition trained on data that underrepresents women and people of color disproportionately misidentifies them \autocite{buolamwini2018gender}, and over-representation cuts the other way, treating one group's experience as the default and generalizing it, wrongly, to everyone else.

Bias enters again after deployment, as a system adapts to new data, which is what regular audits of credit-scoring and hiring systems are for, and what section~\ref{sec:6.1.2}'s instruments are supposed to make contestable.
