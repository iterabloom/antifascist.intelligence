\section{What the term binds}
\label{sec:6.9}
The term binds the machine, not the war. A state whose claim no disinterested body has yet corroborated can still fight, with its own systems, and answer for it afterward. What the term withholds is a general-purpose machine already in the building, ready for whoever claims defense next. The exceptions are not a concession to that state. They are what lets a machine take part where the claim is good, and each is drawn as narrowly as its evidence allows, because each is also the route a war of choice would take.
