6.3. International Cooperation and Norm Development
No single country can bind AI development to altruistic and anti-authoritarian values on its own. Section 6.3.1 covers how research institutions build the working relationships cross-border AI research depends on. Section 6.3.2 turns to standards built by the technical community itself, which is a different layer from the treaty-and-declaration landscape chapter 7 covers in depth. Section 6.3.3 closes with what international AI collaboration can do for problems that are not primarily about AI ethics at all — climate, health, poverty — and what it cannot.
