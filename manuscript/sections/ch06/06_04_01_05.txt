6.4.1.5. Singapore
In Singapore, the significant developments in the regulatory landscape revolve around the Model AI Governance Framework, a comprehensive set of guidelines that promote the responsible and ethical use of AI. This framework focuses on four key principles: explainability, transparency, and fairness in AI decision-making; maintaining human oversight of AI systems; user data and privacy protection; and ensuring the robustness and security of AI systems.

Additionally, Singapore has established the Advisory Council on the Ethical Use of AI and Data, consisting of members from various sectors. The council advises the government on ethical AI principles and recommends regulations and guidelines for AI use. With these initiatives, Singapore positions itself at the forefront of AI ethics and governance.

Complementing regulatory efforts, Singapore has launched initiatives to support AI research and development. The National AI Strategy aims to develop AI technology in key sectors, such as healthcare, education, and finance, enhancing citizen well-being and economic growth. This strategy highlights the importance of utilizing AI to address societal challenges and ensure human-centered development.

Singapore recognizes the need for interdisciplinary collaboration in AI development. The AI Ethics and Governance Body of Knowledge initiative seeks to establish a common language and understanding of AI ethics among professionals from diverse fields like law, engineering, and social sciences. This effort encourages cross-disciplinary idea exchange and facilitates collaborative research on ethical AI development.

To develop AI expertise and nurture talent, Singapore has invested in AI education and training programs. The AI Apprenticeship Programme (AIAP) offers hands-on training for aspiring AI professionals, while the AI for Everyone (AI4E) initiative educates the broader public on AI fundamentals and applications.

In summary, Singapore's regulatory framework and initiatives focus on ethical AI development, emphasizing human-centered principles and interdisciplinary collaboration.
