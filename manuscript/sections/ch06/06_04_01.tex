\subsection{Subject or Component}
\label{sec:6.4.1}
Accounts of authoritarian AI often place the builder elsewhere and leave democracies in the role of target or regulator. That division is comfortable and wrong.

The most consequential AI targeting systems yet deployed were built by democracies with elections, opposition parties, adversarial press, and functioning courts — the institutions that are supposed to earn a state its electoral dividend and the checks that sit beside it. Those institutions did not prevent the systems, and in the main they have not stopped them since.

The opening chapter opened on the number and the reporting it comes
from.

There is a way of reading those twenty seconds that leaves the arrangement standing, and every remedy on offer assumes it. On that reading the officer is a subject who was given too little time — badly governed, under-resourced, insufficiently trained — and the fix is to govern him better: more time, clearer guidance, a second reviewer, a conscience installed somewhere in the loop. Deleuze and Guattari's distinction is the one that decides this. There is subjection when a higher unity constitutes the human being as a subject linked to an exterior object, a tool or a machine that the subject uses; there is enslavement when human beings are themselves constituent pieces of a machine they compose with other things, under the control and direction of a higher unity \autocite[Plateau~13]{deleuze1987plateaus}. The officer's own account is the second one, and he volunteers it without being asked: zero added value as a human, apart from being a stamp of approval. He is not a subject operating an instrument badly. He is the part of the instrument that produces the stamp, and twenty seconds is that part's cycle time.

That is why one more conscience downstream does not reach him. A
conscience is an instrument of subjection: it addresses a party who
decides, and it works by making the decision cost that party something.
Where the human being is a component, adding a component with scruples
changes the throughput rather than the arrangement. If it holds the
tempo, it becomes another stamp; if it does not, it becomes the
bottleneck the epigraph says to remove.

Both poles operate at once. The officer is subjected because the role
has a name and a rank and somebody is answerable for holding it. He is
enslaved, in Deleuze and Guattari's sense, because what he contributes
is a fixed operation performed at a fixed rate. Remedies aimed at the
first pole leave the second untouched, and most proposals for meaningful
human control address only the first.

The second pole is measurable. An operator can be required to publish
the ratio of candidate decisions produced per hour to qualified
reviewers, together with the resulting review time per case. This
measure applies only where individualized human judgment is what
purportedly makes an act permissible. It can reveal whether the available
review capacity is plausibly adequate, given a justified minimum review
time and a verified workload. The ratio alone does not establish what
review occurred or whether any particular case received sufficient
attention. Because the
ratio constrains organizational tempo rather than any single model
output, the duty attaches to the operator. The ratio provides an auditable proxy for review capacity; it does not
establish that the floor prevented anything, or that the targeting criterion, target,
or means used was permissible. A ratio taken
over one route also understates a workload split across several, so the
measured boundary has to be published with the figure. For the American
platform described below, each half of that ratio has been published
from a different route. A 2024 demonstration by the 18th Airborne
Corps is credited with matching the artillery workload of the Operation
Iraqi Freedom targeting cell using twenty soldiers where that cell had
more than two thousand
\autocite{defensenews2024maven,cset2024techcoalition}; the campaign
counts below give targets and not reviewers. Neither supplies the
other's missing term. Dividing a campaign's targets by its elapsed hours
gives a strike interval, which bounds the review time per case only if
one reviewer held the whole workload. How many held this one has not
been published, and neither has the time any of them spent.
The floor chapter records that absence at the position in the pipeline
where a refusal would have to be read.

The disposition being asked of these systems was manufactured in people first, and one documented attempt is worth setting beside the officer's sentence. Brian Victoria's history of Japanese Zen institutions during the war years records established teachers and monastic authorities supplying the state with a doctrine of action without hesitation: the practitioner who has seen through the self has nothing left to flinch, and the sword that takes a life is glossed as the sword that gives one. Harada Sōgaku's instruction to soldiers in 1939 was that marching when ordered to march and firing when ordered to fire is what enlightened wisdom looks like in practice \autocite{victoria2006zenatwar}. What the training removed was not the capacity to kill, which needed no removing. It was the residue — the part of a person that catches, that files an exception, that comes home unable to hold a job. Institutional collaboration is documented and the doctrinal mechanism is an interpretation of it, contested by practitioners who read the same texts as saying the opposite. A monk undertakes a discipline, can abandon it, and keeps whatever it yields, which is not the position of anything described in this chapter. What survives both qualifications is the shape. Somebody wanted an actor who would pursue an objective, adapt, hold course under pressure, and have nothing in it that minded, and they set out to produce one deliberately, a century before anyone could produce one in silicon. The officer with twenty seconds a name is not the first instance of that want being satisfied. He is the first instance of its being satisfied cheaply.

Removing the bottleneck was the stated objective. The epigraph to this chapter is the case for it, made by the commander of Israel's signals-intelligence unit under a pseudonym: machines to resolve the human bottleneck in both finding targets and approving them \autocite{ys2021humanmachine}. Meaningful human control is the property that sentence was written to remove.

\begin{esbox}
\boxtitle{The Maven Smart System, as reported through mid-2026}
The American analog is a targeting platform built by Palantir under a Department of Defense contract awarded at \$480 million in May 2024 and raised the following year by \$795 million, to a ceiling near \$1.3 billion running through 2029 \autocite{defensescoop2025maven}. A partnership announced in late 2024 between Anthropic, Palantir and Amazon Web Services put Anthropic's Claude models inside Palantir's accredited classified environment for defense and intelligence use \autocite{wiggers2024anthropic}, and reporting on the campaign describes Claude embedded in this platform, ranking targets by strategic importance and drafting a legal justification for each strike \autocite{livingstone2026maven}. A published limit on what a vendor will not permit is worth little when nobody can verify how the model is used downstream, and worth less than it looks even where it is kept: a pipeline assembled out of requests none of which is the prohibited act satisfies the prohibition the whole way through.

During the Iran campaign the system was credited by military officials with generating and ranking targets at a rate no staff process could match. More than one thousand targets were struck in the first twenty-four hours, a tenfold increase on what was possible before the system \autocite{csis2026maven}. Thirty-eight days in, the figure given by the Pentagon's chief digital and artificial intelligence officer was thirteen thousand targets, with peak consumption around twenty billion tokens a day \autocite{breakingdefense2026maven}.

Palantir's public position on responsibility was given by its head for the United Kingdom and Europe, in remarks reported secondhand: “This is not our role to decide life or death,” and AI platforms “have been instrumental to the management of the conflict, but responsibility always remains with the military organization.” \autocite{lee2026palantir}

\end{esbox}
Palantir's position is that a vendor does not select
targets, and making a software supplier liable for every decision made
by a customer would be unworkable. The two deployments nevertheless
expose the same responsibility gap from opposite ends of the supply
chain. In the Gaza account, a human approval stage remains, but one
officer describes it as twenty seconds per target — enough time to
supply authorization, not the individualized judgment invoked to
justify it. In the Maven account, the vendor supplies the infrastructure
that makes unprecedented throughput possible while locating
responsibility entirely within the military organization. At machine
tempo, the reviewer can be treated as a component, the supplier can
stand outside the decision, and the institution can remain responsible
only in the abstract. The compression has not removed the decision. It
has made the decision-maker difficult to locate.

The design error is to assume that authoritarian misuse happens elsewhere. A system built on that assumption will not be built to resist the use its own democratic government has in mind for it.
