6.4.2. Adapting Regulatory Frameworks and Ethical Considerations to Local Contexts
Adapting regulatory frameworks and ethical considerations to local contexts is essential in developing altruistic, anti-authoritarian AI systems that respect and respond appropriately to the needs, preferences, and values of different communities and cultures around the world. This means tailoring AI policies and practices without making preposterous reductions such as "Chinese people actually like having a police state because it is their culture."
