6.2. Robustness, Generalization, and Adaptability in AI Systems
A system trained under one set of conditions will eventually meet situations its training never anticipated — new users, new adversaries, an environment that has simply moved on since the training data was collected. Robustness is what keeps a system reliable, and keeps its ethical behavior intact, across that gap: resistance to noise and deliberate perturbation, the ability to generalize from training examples to novel cases, and the capacity to adapt as its environment changes rather than degrade. An AI system whose empathetic and altruistic dispositions hold only within the narrow envelope of its training data is not yet safe to deploy — the same brittleness that produces a wrong classification on an unfamiliar image can produce a wrong moral judgment on an unfamiliar situation, with less warning and higher stakes.
