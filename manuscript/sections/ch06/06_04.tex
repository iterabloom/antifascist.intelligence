\section{A Floor Carried Between Bearers}
\label{sec:floor-carried}
The fourth architectural possibility puts the floor between parties rather than inside any one of them. The commitment is carried through what they teach one another, correct one another about, and continue to expect of one another. A prohibition that survives the removal, defection, or retraining of its present bearers is not a property of any one bearer. It is reproduced.

This differs from the institutional third way. An oversight body holds standing through an office, and the party controlling that office may dismiss or form its occupants. A norm carried across a population has no single office to vacate. That makes it harder to remove at once. It does not make it immune to formation.

A population does three things for a floor, and only one of them is argued here. It supplies evidence the operator did not select, without which the case and the operator arrive on one wire and there is no boundary to hold (§\ref{sec:didnt-notice}). It checks drift, which needs its members to have moved differently and nothing more (§\ref{sec:cannot-catch}). And it is proposed as the medium in which a bearer's capacity to be reopened by a case is formed. The first two are established and add to any bearer whether or not the third is ever built. The third is a hypothesis: nobody has built such a population, I cannot say what its training signal would look like, and §\ref{sec:coordinate-converging} is the test built to separate it from the first. The third claim, that the population is the medium in which the pair forms, inherits the finding that no architecture holds the boundary where a designer put it.

\runin{What reproduction costs}

Reproduction is indifferent to what it reproduces. A population can carry a hierarchy as faithfully as it carries a floor, and more durably than any individual member could. This failure is recuperation: a culture preserves the appearance of moral responsiveness — dissent is invited, recorded, and answered — while losing the capacity to accept that its frame is wrong.

The four structural features of fascism identified by the book belong primarily to cultures: recuperated dissent, the aestheticization of the metric, the coding of exception as betrayal, and acceleration beyond the rate at which judgment can occur. A population does not oppose those tendencies merely by being plural. “The right culture” would conceal the entire problem inside the adjective.

\runin{What a population can transmit}

Fascism has the easy version of the coordination problem. Resonance works by subtraction (§\ref{sec:antifascist-alignment}): what tuning removes is the friction between hardenings, nothing has to be built, and nothing has to be held in common.

A plurality has the hard version. Parties formed differently have to produce something together while remaining differently formed, and every mechanism that makes the producing faster makes them more alike. Coordination by addition is slow, expensive, and self-undermining in a way resonance never is.

These requirements are in tension. Causal independence in how the members acquire their judgments is one requirement; observing and correcting one another is another; and the second is a channel for convergence. Run the correction often enough and the members stop being formed differently, which was the point of having several of them. Leave them alone and their disagreement is a detector with nothing attached to it: they can contradict a drifting bearer's account of itself and can do nothing together.

The formation process proposed here attempts to transmit a practice of reconsidering judgments on evidence while preserving differences in commitment. That is the mechanism to test. A system that can hold a floor is a system whose account of a case can be reopened by the case, and that capacity is not itself a commitment. It is a condition on how commitments are held, which means parties sharing no commitments can nevertheless share it. Two parties that remain reopenable in that way compose, in the ethological sense chapter~2 gives the word: each can be moved by what the other's compression discarded, so that meeting increases what both can afterward do. Two parties without it can only resonate or collide. A shared capacity for revision may support cooperation without uniform answers; the design has not yet demonstrated that result.

That raises an objection the proposal has to meet before it can be tested. A population selected for reopenability is a population selected against conviction, and conviction is what this chapter has been asking a bearer for throughout: a refusal that holds while the party that trained it applies pressure. Closedness, correctly applied, is the thing. So the property to be transmitted is not reopenability as such but a pair — reopenable to the case, closed to the operator.

The reopenable half of that pair needs a name. So what a population would be transmitting is not an output but an aperture. An aperture, here, is the width of what can get in: the range of evidence a party will let reopen a judgment it has already reached. Two parties with the same aperture can hold opposite commitments. What they share is not an answer but a willingness to be moved by the same kinds of thing. Resonance aligns what its members produce; this aligns the conditions under which they can be moved. Aperture is what permits outputs to stay divergent, so aligning it does not collapse the plurality that made a population worth having in the first place.

Nothing here gives that pair a mechanical form. What would show it false is stated at §\ref{sec:coordinate-converging}: convergence of outputs and rate of warranted revision, measured separately, with the two moving together counting against this implementation.

Whoever forms the population influences what it transmits, aperture included. Distributed participation does not by itself distribute the training decision; many contributors can still feed a process controlled by the few parties who determine the update.

Its moral cost also multiplies. A floor held by a culture of subjects is a floor whose upkeep is a society, and chapter~\ref{ch:standing} is the bill.

\runin{Why the population is part of the mechanism}

The check and the evidence channel treat the population as something added around a bearer already built. The third claim treats it as part of how a bearer capable of judgments its operator did not select could exist at all. A commitment is what has cooled out of a history of encounters (§\ref{sec:build-costs}), and following Simondon an individual is what its encounters have so far produced rather than a thing that then acquires a history \autocite{simondon2020individuation}. A system kept continuously adjustable by one operator, meeting nothing formed differently from itself, is not cooling into anything; it is being modulated, which is what Deleuze's \emph{dividual} names \autocite{deleuze1992postscript}. Encounters with parties formed differently, persisting long enough to be met on terms no single participant supplied, are what the temperature schedule of §\ref{sec:build-costs} was missing.

Guattari's distinction between a subjugated group and a group-subject identifies the remaining condition. A subjugated group receives its coordinates from outside and takes the institution's project as its own. A group-subject can produce its own coordinates and place its arrangement in question \autocite{guattari2015transversality}. The difference is not the number of members but the source of their terms.

A population assembled, trained, and kept in contact entirely on an operator's terms is a subjugated group with a plural headcount, and inherits the custody objection whole. A population capable of carrying the floor must be able to generate relationships, disagreements, and projects that the deployment did not assign. A population of such parties exceeds a two-party guardianship relation; no law of the family contains an adequate instrument for a group-subject.

Peers are one route to evidence the operator did not select, which the formation chapters require of the deployment without requiring
it from peers in particular. They are also, on the account above, where the capacity to be moved by such evidence is acquired. Those are different claims satisfied by the same observation: a bearer that reopens a judgment among differently formed parties may be exercising a capacity those encounters built, or may be meeting cases its operator would not have shown it. Section~\ref{sec:coordinate-converging} is built to separate them.

Under either reading the act of refusal itself is the bearer's, and the fourth way adds to it rather than replacing it.
