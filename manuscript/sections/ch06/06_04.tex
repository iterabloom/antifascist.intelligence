\section{A clean fail: Iran, 2026}
\label{sec:6.4}
The 2026 campaign against Iran fails the Charter as courts read it on the administration's own account. The strikes began on 28 February. On 10 March the administration told the Security Council it was acting in self-defense under Article 51 against Iran's “attacks and ongoing threats,” as the latest stage of an armed conflict dating from June 2025 and in the collective self-defense of Israel. It named no armed attack under way, and asked that the imminence of the threat be judged against the regime's conduct since 1979 \autocite{usun2026s161}. Article 51 as courts read it doesn't reach anticipatory self-defense, so it fails before any departure applies, and the refusal is the term's from the first strike. That is the question Chapter~\ref{sec:2} says the vendor dispute never reached.
