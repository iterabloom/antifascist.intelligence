7.1.2. AI and the Concentration of Power in the Technology Industry
The acceleration of the commodification of artificial intelligence technologies has fostered a stratification within the technological landscape, a landscape where a small cadre of ‘Big Tech’ corporations -- namely Google, Apple, Facebook, Amazon, and Microsoft (GAFAM) -- wield significant influence. The outsized power that these firms hold poses significant economic and ethical implications for the trajectory of AI development.

Companies like GAFAM are advantaged by their vast financial resources, enabling them to fund extensive research and development initiatives, draw top AI talent, and maintain command of sprawling networks of data, the fuel upon which effective machine learning systems depend. Consequently, this creates a vast chasm of disparity between these multi-national conglomerates and smaller firms or newcomers, who struggle to gain traction due to a comparative dearth of data or resources.

This power dynamic intensifies when large corporations engage in vertical and horizontal expansion by acquiring competitors and startups, as displayed by Facebook’s absorption of Instagram and WhatsApp. Not only does this activity solidify their supremacy in the market, but it also grants them unparalleled access to diverse, comprehensive datasets from billions of users worldwide. This subsequent consolidation bolsters their control over AI evolution and use in sectors such as social media, digital advertising, and e-commerce, and solidifies the perception of an inescapable technological monopoly. 

The consolidation of power within select technology corporations presents a fertile ground for unwarranted surveillance, exploitation, and manipulation; a particularly concerning issue when AI technologies fall into the hands of authoritarian governments. This concern is epitomized by the symbiotic relationship between the Chinese government and informatics giants such as Alibaba and Tencent. By harnessing the machine learning capabilities of these corporations, the state wields AI-enabled tools of surveillance and censorship, engendering an authoritarian model of governance that poses a near existential risk to free thought, individual privacy, and democratic traditions.

Furthermore, the monopolization of AI technology detrimentally impacts competition and impedes innovation. These monopolies act as gatekeepers, barring access to essential datasets and AI infrastructure, which are critical to developing modern, competitive AI systems. Startups and small businesses attempting novel approaches to AI development are thereby disproportionately disadvantaged, stunting the flourishing of diverse, creative AI solutions. This homogenization of thought collectively inhibits the potential for groundbreaking developments in ethical AI advancement and further undermines the democratisation of AI, presenting a significant roadblock to realising the vision of altruistic and anti-authoritarian AI tools.

In response to this increasingly oligarchical AI environment, several institutional countermeasures warrant consideration, such as enforcing stringent antitrust regulations to curtail the monopolization of AI, mandating data-sharing initiatives, and advocating for robust transparency standards. The creation of funding opportunities for projects focused on ethical AI research can stimulate diverse and inclusive thought, while initiatives promoting cross-sector collaboration can ensure a broad range of stakeholders share responsibility for the trajectory of AI development.

Ultimately, the concentrated power within the technology industry poses a significant challenge to fostering altruistic and anti-authoritarian AI. To circumvent these issues, proactive and intentional actions from various stakeholders, including policymakers, scholars, industry leaders, and the public are necessary. Cooperation across sectors and countries is essential in driving collective progress towards aligning AI technologies with human values, fostering a competitive innovation landscape, and ensuring the democratic values we hold dear are respected and upheld in an increasingly digital future.
