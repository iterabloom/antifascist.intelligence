7.4. Accountability, Legal Responsibility, and Antifascism in Sentient Machine Experimentation
Chapter 2 did the definitional and procedural work this section builds on: §2.4.1 argued that "is it conscious" and "can it suffer" are different questions with different evidence, §2.4.4 worked out what informed consent and an oversight board would need to mean for a system whose capacity to consent is itself unresolved, and §2.4.5 laid out the options for a sentient system's legal status. Each of those sections ended by handing a specific, unfinished question to this chapter: what makes a review board's ruling binding rather than advisory, what a court actually does with a system whose legal status is unsettled, and who answers for harm a sentient system causes or suffers. This section takes up those three questions in turn, then closes on what keeps the whole apparatus more than a policy document nobody enforces.
