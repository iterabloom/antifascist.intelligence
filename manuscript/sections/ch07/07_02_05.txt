7.2.5. Encouraging the Development of Altruistic, Anti-Authoritarian, and Ethical AI Systems
It remains paramount that we encourage altruistic, anti-authoritarian, and ethical principles to guide the construction of AI systems.
To effectively counteract authoritarian uses of AI technologies, governments, organizations, and AI developers should actively establish and implement comprehensive policies that fund an environment of transparency, accountability, and human rights protection. These policies must specifically focus on inhibiting potential misuse of AI capabilities by malicious actors, including authoritarian governments, to curtail human rights and perpetuate oppressive practices.

(1) Regulation on AI-enabled surveillance systems: Governments should restrict the sale, distribution, and usage of AI-enabled surveillance technologies to authoritarian regimes and actors known to violate human rights. By doing so, they can prevent these advanced tools from being leveraged in mass surveillance, censorship, and other repressive activities. Further, international cooperation should be sought after to better control the global market for such technology exports.

Example: In 2019, the European Union agreed to restrict the export of cyber-surveillance technologies to countries with poor human rights records, recognizing the potential misuse of these technologies in perpetuating human rights abuses.

(2) Adopting a human rights-centric approach: AI developers and companies should be encouraged to adopt a human rights-centric approach in the design and development of their systems. This approach would ensure that AI systems are not easily misused by authoritarian governments or malicious actors, while fostering trust in AI technology and maintaining transparency.

Example: The Toronto Declaration, presented at the 2018 RightsCon conference, provides guidelines for protecting rights to equality and non-discrimination in machine learning systems. By adhering to similar guidelines, AI developers can create systems that are resistant to authoritarian exploitation. 

(3) Developing technical safeguards and countermeasures: AI researchers and developers should actively work on creating technical safeguards and countermeasures that can prevent or mitigate the misuse of AI systems by authoritarian actors. This may include incorporating privacy-preserving features that render data collection and monitoring more difficult or developing AI systems capable of detecting obstructive or manipulative behavior.

Example: Differential privacy, a technique for sharing information about a dataset without revealing the individual data points, is increasingly being deployed by tech companies to protect user privacy in AI systems. Similarly, AI technologies like federated learning, which allow models to learn from decentralized data sources, can help maintain privacy while ensuring the system remains effective.

(4) Public and private collaboration: Encourage collaboration and partnerships between governments, NGOs, and AI developers for the exchange of information and best practices in preventing the misuse of AI in authoritarian contexts.

Example: Partnerships, such as the AI Global Surveillance (AIGS) Project, which tracks and monitors the worldwide spread of AI surveillance technologies, enable governments, organizations, and developers to access global data on potential abuses and cooperatively act to prevent authoritarian uses of AI.

(5) International cooperation and norm development: Nations should cooperate in developing shared norms, guidelines, and regulations for AI technologies that promote democratic values and discourage authoritarian uses. Such international cooperation is vital for addressing the global challenges and opportunities that ethical AI development presents.

Example: The G7 Global Partnership on Artificial Intelligence is a multilateral initiative that aims to guide the responsible development and use of AI, promoting the importance of human rights, democratic values, and transparency. By participating in and supporting such initiatives, nations can collaboratively develop better policies to counteract authoritarian uses of AI.

As AI technologies continue to evolve, it is essential for governments, organizations, and developers to adopt a proactive approach in establishing comprehensive policies and regulatory frameworks that protect human rights and prevent authoritarian misuse. By doing so, AI can be harnessed to contribute positively to democratic values and global well-being.
Developing AI systems that explicitly support democratic values and human rights requires prioritizing transparency, accountability, and diversity while actively addressing biases and potential misuse. In this section, we provide examples of AI applications designed to protect and promote democracy and human rights, while highlighting potential challenges and recommendations.

Example 1: AI-Driven Online Content Moderation

AI systems can be employed in content moderation to protect freedom of speech while actively fighting hate speech, disinformation, and extremist content. Balancing this will encourage open discussion and promote democratic values while limiting the spread of incitements to violence.

Challenges: Ensuring that AI-driven content moderation algorithms do not unfairly target marginalized or underrepresented communities, and effectively distinguishing between genuine concerns and hate speech.

Recommendations: It is crucial to involve diverse perspectives in developing and refining these algorithms, as well as regular audits and assessments to monitor for unintended consequences and potential biases.

Example 2: AI-Assisted Election Monitoring System

Artificial intelligence can help ensure transparency, fairness, and integrity in electoral processes by monitoring and reporting election-related incidents in real-time, identifying patterns and trends indicative of fraud, and informing appropriate corrective actions.

Challenges: Ensuring that AI-driven election monitoring systems are impartial, secure, and not susceptible to being manipulated by external actors or biased intents.

Recommendations: Developing robust, transparent algorithms that prioritize data privacy and security. Establishing a multi-stakeholder oversight committee, including representatives from various political parties and civil society organizations, which can review and evaluate the performance of AI-driven election monitoring systems.

Example 3: AI-Enabled Citizen Participation in Government Decision-Making

AI systems can facilitate citizen participation in government decision-making processes by automating the analysis of large volumes of public input, identifying common themes and concerns, and providing tailored, evidence-based recommendations to policymakers.

Challenges: Ensuring that AI systems capture and consider the diverse perspectives of citizens, avoiding the amplification of current biases, and maintaining transparency in how AI systems process and prioritize citizens' inputs.

Recommendations: Developing open-source AI algorithms that prioritize representativeness, diversity, and inclusiveness. Providing clear explanations about the processing of citizens' inputs, and allowing public scrutiny and adjustment of AI system parameters.

Designing and implementing AI systems that actively support democratic values and human rights is a crucial goal in the development of ethical AI. By addressing potential challenges and following a set of best-practice recommendations, the AI community can create systems that strengthen democracy, promote inclusivity, and foster a more egalitarian society.
To promote the development of anti-authoritarian AI systems and ensure their positive impact on human well-being, democracy, and human rights, it is critical to initiate global cooperation through the formation of a unified set of goals, principles, and strategies. We propose the following key steps to foster international collaboration in ethical AI development:

(1) Establish shared ethical guidelines and international standards for AI development: Develop a set of unified principles that provide a foundation for anti-authoritarian AI systems. These guidelines should prioritize transparency, fairness, accountability, and human rights protection. 

Example: In 2020, UNESCO adopted a draft recommendation on the ethics of AI, aiming to create a global ethical framework for AI governance. This initiative encourages collaboration between countries and sets a strong foundation for the international development of ethical AI that supports altruism and anti-authoritarianism.

(2) Develop collaborative research initiatives: Encourage the formation of research agreements, joint laboratories, and international conferences focusing on ethical and anti-authoritarian AI development. By promoting cooperation over competition, these collaborative efforts can fund innovation and hold nation-states accountable for upholding ethical AI practices.

(3) Share knowledge and best practices: Encourage the exchange of information, research outcomes, and successful strategies among democratic nations. Sharing expertise ensures a unified and effective effort against the misuse of AI to undermine democratic values.

(4) Implement AI-specific treaties and international agreements: Formulate policies that formalize international collaborations, such as shared ethical frameworks, technology standards, and AI-specific treaties. By doing so, a coalition grounded in shared democratic values and dedication to transparency can serve as an influential counterweight to the spread of AI-enabled authoritarian practices.

(5) Create global platforms for stakeholder engagement: Establish forums where stakeholders from different sectors, such as academia, industry, governments, and civil society, can collaborate on the development, deployment, and governance of anti-authoritarian AI systems. These platforms facilitate dialogue, encourage diverse perspectives, and build cooperative networks dedicated to responsible AI development.

(6) Encourage capacity building and shared expertise: Support global initiatives that promote AI literacy, ethical AI education, and technical skills development, ensuring that countries have access to the necessary resources and expertise for developing ethical and anti-authoritarian AI systems.

(7) Prioritize AI's role in promoting democratic values and human rights: Support the development of AI-driven tools that reinforce democratic values, such as technologies to detect and prevent electoral fraud, or collaborative research on algorithms to minimize the spread of disinformation.

Fostering global cooperation in the development of anti-authoritarian AI systems necessitates a comprehensive approach that unifies countries under shared ethical guidelines, funds collaborative research, and funds the exchange of best practices.
