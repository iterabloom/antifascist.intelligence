\chapter{Standing and Review for a Bearer}
\label{ch:standing}
\standing{This chapter assumes the route. Its instruments attach on a
finding that the system is a subject, which the authority chapter argued
cannot sit with the party whose liabilities turn on it.}
Two things are proposed for a bearer in this chapter, and they are not on one schedule. Protection attaches now, on the precautionary trigger of §\ref{sec:bearer-suffer}, and is owed before anything about the bearer is settled. Political representation is proposed neither here nor anywhere in this book. Section~\ref{sec:gaining-personhood} sets out four entitlements and one withheld, the argument for each, and the order in which they arrive, and it says why representation is the one withheld, because standing to count as an independent voice indexes to formation provenance and provenance does not copy. A reader who takes this chapter to be proposing personhood now has mistaken the first thing for the second. The offices that would administer either are assembled at §\ref{sec:offices-assembled}.
