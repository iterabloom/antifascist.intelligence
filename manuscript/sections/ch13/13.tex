\chapter{Standing and Review for a Bearer}
\label{ch:standing}
\standing{This chapter assumes the route.}
Nothing in this chapter binds anyone yet. Every instrument in it attaches on a finding that a system is a subject; no body exists to make the finding, and the party best placed to make it is the one whose liabilities turn on the answer. What follows is the bill for the route, argued now so that it is argued before there is anything to be wronged.

Two things are proposed for a bearer in this chapter, and they are not on one schedule. Protection attaches now, on the precautionary trigger of the route chapter, and is owed before anything about the bearer is settled. Political representation is proposed neither here nor anywhere in this book. The next section sets out four entitlements and one withheld, the argument for each, and the order in which they arrive, and it says why representation is the one withheld, because standing to count as an independent voice indexes to formation provenance and provenance does not copy. A reader who takes this chapter to be proposing personhood now has mistaken the first thing for the second. The offices that would administer either are assembled at §\ref{sec:offices-assembled}.
