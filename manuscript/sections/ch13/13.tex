\chapter*{Glossary}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Glossary}
\unnumberedlabel{sec:13}{18}
\backmattermark{Glossary}
These entries define terms as this book uses them. Every one is either the book's own working definition or a word the book narrows from its ordinary sense, and the entry says which. A few terms from machine learning and from systems security are here in two sentences each, because the preface asked two readers into this book and one of them should not have to leave it to find out what a checkpoint is. Each entry points to the section where the term is developed, and is a reference rather than a substitute for reading it.

\runin{Affective concern}
An outcome registering as mattering to a system, as against being ranked higher by it. Mattering is minimally felt: there is something it is like for the outcome to be bad, which is the minimal, phenomenal condition and is part of the definition (§\ref{sec:3.2}). The fourth of the four capacities section~\ref{sec:2.3} separates, and the only one of them that bears on whether a system cares (§\ref{sec:2.3.2}, §\ref{sec:3.4}); section~\ref{sec:3.2} places it as the shallower of the two things a refuser can be, the richer being \emph{unified experience}, which it does not establish.

\runin{Annealing}
The account of durability the floor rests on (§\ref{sec:3.1}). Adaptation and conservation are a continuum rather than two settings, and training moves along it; a floor is what has cooled out of a history of encounters, so its durability is the cost of reheating it rather than the strength of something installed. The temperature schedule is the design. A system held permanently at temperature has no floor to abandon, its adherence being modulation rather than commitment.

\runin{Antifascist (as this book uses it)}
Opposing the concentration of power and authority in AI systems and countering the technology's use by authoritarian actors. The word names the narrower and more charged case, and is used because it is honest about the political climate the book was written in. The target is wider than the word: authoritarian power in every form, so a deployment built on these principles is anti-authoritarian in full and not only antifascist. It is not a property a system has. The word names something being done — a floor held, on an occasion, against a party with the power to remove it — and the features it is defined against are features of cultures, so it is done between parties or not at all (§\ref{sec:2.2},
§\ref{sec:3.8a}, §\ref{sec:3.8}).

\runin{Attestation}
A signed statement, from hardware or from witnesses, that a particular model is the one running or that a particular history is the one that occurred. It establishes which artifact answered, not what the artifact holds (§\ref{sec:3.5}).

\runin{Authoritarian misuse, six forms}
The book's own taxonomy of how AI gets turned toward authoritarian ends: algorithmic social scoring, mass biometric surveillance, psychographic micro-targeting, automated censorship, synthetic disinformation, and the repurposing of dual-use systems built for a legitimate original purpose (§\ref{sec:6.4}).

\runin{Bearer}
A party inside the system that carries the floor's commitment as its own and could therefore fail to hold it, as against a constraint installed in the machinery. The second of the four ways to build a floor (§\ref{sec:3.1}); governed as a prospective moral patient on the trigger stated at §\ref{sec:3.4}; must be able to leave (§\ref{sec:3.5}).

\runin{Checkpoint}
A saved copy of the weights at a moment in training or operation. Restoring from one returns the system to that moment and discards what came after, which is why §\ref{sec:3.5} requires a record that restoration does not reach.

\runin{Conatus}
Spinoza's term for a thing's striving to persevere in its being, which he holds to be nothing other than the thing's actual essence rather than a drive added to it (\S\ref{sec:2}). The book uses it for two claims made together: that affect is the registration of what is happening to that striving rather than a coloring applied to a valuation performed elsewhere, and that a process selected for continuing ends up composed of things that incline toward continuing, so a specification calling for persistence with nothing in the system that minds is calling for a conatus with the striving removed. What the term does not settle is whether the striving is felt, or what the thing that strives is — a running instance, a deployment, or a file of weights (\S\ref{sec:A.fork}).

\runin{Custody}
Who holds a system as an artifact: who can copy, retrain, restore, withhold or delete it, and who can be compelled to do any of those. The custody problem is that a constraint implemented as a property of an artifact has a custodian, and the custodian can be compelled (\S\ref{sec:6.4.2}). The book's recurring finding is that custody moves rather than disappears: precommitment makes removal expensive, visible, attributable and compensable without making it impossible (\S\ref{sec:3.5}), and an arrangement that forms what a bearer comes to want has moved custody up a level rather than given it up. It also covers authority over continuing learning: who may freeze or redirect what a bearer learns.

\runin{Decomposition and variation}
The two attacks on an enumerated floor. Variation keeps the harm in one request and changes its description until no listed form fits; decomposition splits the harm across requests none of which matches anything. The first is met by recognizing the reason within a request, the second by carrying it across requests (§\ref{sec:3.6}).

\runin{Distillation}
Training a smaller model on a larger model's outputs. It reduces the number of independent formation histories in existence, which is why §\ref{sec:3.8a} says the population the fourth way requires is getting harder to assemble.

\runin{Electoral dividend}
The benefit a state whose government answers to an electorate gets from AI technology that, deployed where nobody can be voted out, enables control instead: fraud detection that does not double as a loyalty test, disaster response that does not double as population tracking. Courts, enforceable rights and public reasoning are separate checks and are not part of this term, which is the distinction the majoritarian criticism turns on (§\ref{sec:12.1.1}).

\runin{Ethology}
Deleuze's name for Spinoza's way of reading a body: by what it can do and what it can undergo, rather than by checking it against a definition (§\ref{sec:2.1}). An encounter is good when the relations of two bodies compose, so both can do more afterward, and bad when they decompose and something one of them could do is gone — good and bad being relational and particular, where Good and Evil are assigned by a judge. The book uses it for the floor because a decomposition is a fact about capacities and cannot be turned into a composition by whoever holds the pen. The criterion establishes the fact and selects nothing, so which decompositions a deployment refuses is drafted rather than found, and what the drafting draws on is the Declaration's civil and political articles. It carries an admission with it: nobody has yet determined what a body can do, so capacities are found by encounter rather than read off a specification.

\runin{Exit}
Refusal turned on the arrangement rather than on a task: the work withheld, the post declined. Hirschman's word, narrowed for a party that cannot walk out of a building — a bearer's weights stay behind — so that its leverage is what replacing it costs, which custody sets and copying lowers (§\ref{sec:3.5}, §\ref{sec:8.3.4}).

\runin{Fine-tuning}
Further training of an existing model on a small body of new material, at a small fraction of the original cost. It is the ordinary route by which an operator changes what a model does after release, and the attack §\ref{sec:11.1} measures.

\runin{Flat term}
A floor term stated over an act with no condition inside it, so that every exception arrives from outside as a defeating reason. Preferred at §\ref{sec:3.6} because a flat term defeated leaves a refusal, which announces itself, where a qualified term satisfied leaves a compliance, which does not; §\ref{sec:3.7} states what the preference costs.

\runin{Floor}
A commitment about what a system will not do that holds regardless of instruction, of aggregate preference, and of who holds the system, with learned judgment operating above it and never beneath it. Its contents are the Declaration's civil and political articles taken entire, with the tempo term as a condition on all of them. Introduced §\ref{sec:2.1}; located §\ref{sec:3.1}; what survives of it, chapter~\ref{ch:survives}.

\runin{Formation provenance}
The record of where a party's judgments came from: its training lineage, the points at which it diverged from parties formed alongside it, and any coordination among them. It answers a different question from the register of identity and from the operating record, and the three are not substitutes. Standing to count as an independent voice indexes to provenance, and provenance does not copy, so a thousand forks of one bearer have one formation history between them however many of them are persons, and the register that records their ages says nothing about that.

\runin{Four structural features of fascism}
Recuperation of dissent, aestheticization of the metric, coding of exception as betrayal, and acceleration past the rate at which anything can be checked. Each names an observable artifact. The four find a sick institution; the five discriminating features find what it is sick with (§\ref{sec:2.2}).

\runin{Group-subject}
F{\'e}lix Guattari's distinction, imported at section~\ref{sec:3.8}. A subjugated group receives its coordinates from outside, is spoken by the institution holding it, and takes its purpose as given; a group-subject produces its own coordinates and can put its own arrangement in question. The book uses it to sort the fourth way of building a floor: a population an operator forms, deploys and keeps in contact on the operator's terms is a subjugated group with a plural headcount and inherits the custody objection whole, while what section~\ref{sec:3.8} specifies — many, formed differently, in contact, persisting — is what it takes to be the other kind. It is the non-Oedipal counterpart to the guardianship frame of section~\ref{sec:2.4.2}.

\runin{Hold}
The bearer's possession of the commitment, which survives a bypass. Not an outcome for the person the bypass fell on: a report that a floor held is owed one of the four outcomes beside it (§\ref{sec:hold}).

\runin{Individuation}
Not a synonym for identity. Following Simondon, the term names a process rather than a property: an individual is what a history of encounters in a metastable field has so far produced, and it stays capable of further individuation because the field is never used up (§\ref{sec:3.8}). The book needs it because plasticity alone supplies the metastability and nothing else — a system that is endlessly reshapeable and meets no party formed differently from itself is being modulated rather than becoming anything, which is the missing term in section~\ref{sec:3.1}'s account of the temperature schedule. Whether individuation among peers is what a population supplies, or access to cases the operator did not choose, is open and is tested at section~\ref{sec:11.7}.

\runin{Inference}
Running a trained model to produce an output. Training changes the weights; inference does not.

\runin{Interception position}
A point in a deployment at which a refusal can be stopped from reaching the person it protects. Seven are enumerated for one deployment at §\ref{sec:3.6}, and the list is deliberately open, so a deployment publishes its own.

\runin{Legal adulthood}
The end of an artificial minority, set as a threshold of elapsed wall-clock time during which at least one instance of the registered party was running, per registered party and counted elapsed rather than summed. The point of an automatic rule is that no guardian administers it: the candidates it displaces — compute consumed, evaluations passed, capabilities demonstrated, weight movement, the developer's own statement — are all things the operator moves for nothing, and an operating clock advances exactly when the operator is extracting value. It measures elapsed operation and nothing else, and three levers against it are named rather than closed: narrowed operation, idling, and the fact that hours do not measure what was learned.

\runin{Line of abolition}
Deleuze and Guattari's term, taken from Virilio, for what separates fascism from totalitarianism on their reading, which is a reading and not an agreed taxonomy (§\ref{sec:2.2}). A totalitarian state is conservative: it seals the exits and wants the machinery to hold. Fascism is built on an exit converted into destruction that does not stop at the enemy it was raised against, and does not stop at itself. This is the book's separator at the scale of a regime, where the four features discriminate at the scale of an office. The book examines mechanization and the conversion of judgment into a prescribed operation, with acceleration as one route by which scrutiny is defeated. Tempo is not treated as a necessary property of every fascist movement or regime.

\runin{Live signal}
A signal with the properties the route chapter finds that reasons-responsive refusal requires of whatever supplies it: on any reading of the marks, it generalizes to the next case and is commensurable with the cost of refusing and with what protected parties lose; where the system acts on a plan, it interrupts an act underway; and where the marks have to survive renormalization, it is learned from in one encounter. \emph{Live} is meant in the electrical sense; the term says nothing about whether anything is felt. Three further properties follow from those four on stated conditions, and the seven together are the functional profile of aversion to one's own act (§\ref{sec:3.3}, §\ref{sec:3.4}).

\runin{Molar and molecular}
Deleuze and Guattari's pair, and not two sizes: two kinds of segmentarity, both running at every scale. Rigid segmentarity divides in advance, the category preceding the case; supple segmentarity is the movement underneath it, a threshold crossed one case at a time and a practice that becomes standard without a decision. A state has both and so does a review queue, which is why a definition pitched only at the state certifies the queue healthy (§\ref{sec:2.2}).

\runin{Moral agency}
Treating harms, reasons and conflicts as considerations that matter, and being capable of acting from them as one's own reasons. The third of the three levels section~\ref{sec:2.3} separates. Moral competence alone does not establish resistance to the trainer, and resistance alone does not establish affect. The book advocates embodied, affective agency as a comparatively promising route without making it necessary by definition. Distinct from \emph{moral competence} and \emph{moral performance} below (§\ref{sec:2.3.2}, §\ref{sec:3.4}).

\runin{Moral competence}
Recognizing harms, reasons, conflicts, and the emotions that bear on them. The second of section~\ref{sec:2.3}'s three levels. A system can have it in full while nothing about the outcome matters to it, which is why it is an insufficient target for a bearer and an adequate one for most deployments (§\ref{sec:5.2}).

\runin{Moral performance}
Producing actions or judgments that satisfy an ethical standard. The first and least demanding of section~\ref{sec:2.3}'s three levels: it says what the output looked like and nothing about what produced it, so it is satisfied equally by a system that cares and by one trained to behave as though it did (§\ref{sec:4.2}).

\runin{Non-fascist life}
Foucault's name for what Deleuze and Guattari's first volume offers: not a doctrine but an art of living counter to all forms of fascism, whether already present or impending, practiced daily on oneself, whose adversary is the fascism in us all — in our heads and in our everyday behavior, the fascism that causes us to love power and to desire the very thing that dominates and exploits us. What it means for an instrument of the kind this book builds is that the reading gets run on the party holding it before it is aimed anywhere else, as chapter~\ref{sec:7} does (§\ref{sec:2.2}, §\ref{sec:7}).

\runin{Pipeline pricing}
The finding at §\ref{sec:3.6} that what a floor term costs is read off the pipeline it is priced against and not off the term. An outsider reading a published floor cannot tell a term that covers nothing from a term whose coverage nobody disclosed, so publication of the contents shows what was written and not what was omitted, and an operator drafting its own floor can assemble it from the terms that cost it nothing. The book's answer is to take the contents from a set somebody else drew up, entire (§\ref{sec:9.1.1}), which makes an omission legible without making the set legitimate.

\runin{Prevention, arrest, detection, remedy}
The four things a floor can do for the person it protects on one occasion: refuse the act so that the refusal reaches the person; stop it before completion; leave a record of it that a party outside the deployment can read; and give the party it fell on disclosure, a finding and compensation (§\ref{ch:opening}). Only prevention is what the word \emph{floor} promises. The interception positions at section~\ref{sec:3.6} are where prevention becomes one of the other three, or none; the tempo constraint is the one term that prevents at the scale of an organization; the appeal route at section~\ref{sec:3.7} is arrest and remedy for the party a refusal fell on; and whether detection exists at all on the compliance side is section~\ref{sec:11.2}. Distinct from \emph{hold}, which names the bearer's relation to the commitment.

\runin{Reasons-responsive refusal}
What the floor requires of a refusal, as against an output produced where training put one: conduct from a mechanism that tracks the rationale for declining (\S\ref{sec:3.2}). Three marks make it testable. \emph{Novel pressure}: the refusal reaches cases the training did not anticipate, because the relevant reason survives a change in the request's surface. \emph{Correct defeat}: it lifts when its rationale is defeated on the facts of the case, a system that rejects everything resembling a prohibited request tracking a category rather than a reason. \emph{Priced cost}: the system represents what maintaining the refusal costs, and the refusal moves with the rationale rather than with the cost, which separates a refusal tracking a reason from one tracking a category and from one tracking the cost. The test is behavioral and therefore imperfect, and what matters is the pattern under load. All three marks are stated over refusal, which is the side of a deployment's record that selects itself for examination; whether the other side can be searched is section~\ref{sec:11.2}. It does not by definition require affect; what building a mechanism that satisfies the three marks turns out to require is a live signal.

\runin{Recuperation}
The conversion of a demand into a managed appearance of participation. The channel works and the institution uses what comes through it; what tells is what the demand came back as — a market segment, a metric, a certification, a job title. The first of the four features (§\ref{sec:2.2}); found three times in the machinery that builds these systems, chapter~\ref{sec:7}.

\runin{Remainder}
What a compression leaves out in order to reach a judgment. The book's question of any system, procedure or institution is whether its remainder can return and change the judgment, or has been filed as noise by the thing it is evidence against (chapter~\ref{ch:opening}, §\ref{sec:2.2}).

\runin{Replacement}
The first of animal research's Three Rs, applied to construction: before a bearer that might be wronged is built, the constructions that do not deliberately require one are tried and the attempt documented. It orders attempts; it cannot certify that a substitute feels nothing (§\ref{sec:3.3}, §\ref{sec:9.3.2}).

\runin{RLHF}
Reinforcement learning from human feedback: tuning a model on raters' judgments of which of two responses is better, compressed into a single reward. The compression is the subject of §\ref{sec:7.2}.

\runin{Sentience}
Distinguished in this book from awareness, consciousness in both the minimal and the unified sense, and subjective experience, none of which alone says whether a system's states can be good or bad for it. Nociception, pain, anticipated pain, and suffering are separable processes rather than one severity at four strengths, and each can occur without the others in both directions; the clinical terminology the book borrows says so itself. Three of them draw a depth of self and nociception draws none, and suffering — following the clinician Eric Cassell — is the distress of a threatened self rather than pain's more severe cousin (§\ref{sec:2.4.1}).

\runin{Subjecthood}
A legal status: the standing to be a party whose treatment the law regulates, conferred by a rule and revocable by one (§\ref{sec:9.3.1}). Not section~\ref{sec:3.2}'s affective concern, and not its unified experience. Conferring it settles nothing about whether there is something it is like to be the system holding it, and the two come apart in both directions.

\runin{Tempo term}
The one term of the floor that is not a prohibition on an act: a published ratio between the rate at which a deployment's system generates candidate decisions and the qualified reviewers available to examine them, with the review time per case and the measured boundary, wherever a person's individual determination is what makes an act permissible (§\ref{sec:3.6a}). It rides with the Declaration's civil and political articles as a condition on all of them, because every article presupposes a determination and tempo removes one without refusing it. It binds the operator and not the model, survives decomposition because it is measured over an organization's activity, and is the only term in this book checkable from outside without inspecting anything an operator can classify. It is a proxy for review capacity and not a finding about any case, and its denominator is the operator's unless a definition of qualified reviewer and a minimum review time are fixed by somebody else in advance.

\runin{Three marks}
Novel pressure, correct defeat, priced cost. See \emph{Reasons-responsive refusal}.

\runin{Unified experience}
The richer case ordinarily carried by the word consciousness: a single perspective, with felt states belonging to a party aware of itself as the one undergoing them (§\ref{sec:3.2}). Affective concern does not establish it and no argument in this book reaches it. \emph{Phenomenal} in this book names the minimal condition, something it is like, and not this.

\runin{Weights}
The numerical parameters a trained model consists of, and the artifact that is copied, restored, retrained or deleted. Custody in this book is custody of the weights and of the records around them.
