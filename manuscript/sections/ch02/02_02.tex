\section{Empathy, Social Intelligence, and Theory of Mind}
\label{sec:2.2}
Empathy, social intelligence and theory of mind are the capacities usually named when an AI system is asked to work with people rather than on them. This section complicates the first of the three. Empathy, felt or simulated, is not the load-bearing capacity this chapter's argument needs, and compassion, a separable system, does more of the work. What survives the complication still matters: a system has to read an emotional state accurately before anything can be built on top of that reading, and being honest about which of these capacities is carrying the weight is what makes its dealings with people trustworthy instead of merely fluent.
