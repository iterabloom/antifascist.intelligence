\section{Empathy, Social Intelligence, and Theory of Mind}
\label{sec:2.2}
Empathy, social intelligence and theory of mind are the capacities usually named when an AI system is asked to work with people rather than on them: reading an emotional state, placing it inside the norms and relationships it occurs in, and modeling a person's beliefs and intentions well enough to predict what they will do with them.

This section complicates the first of the three. Empathy, felt or simulated, is not the load-bearing capacity this chapter's argument needs, and compassion, a separable system, does more of the work. What survives the complication still matters: an AI system has to read an emotional state accurately before anything can be built on top of that reading, however the “on top of” ends up working. Doing that honestly, including honesty about which of these capacities is carrying the weight, is what makes a system's dealings with people more trustworthy instead of merely more fluent.
