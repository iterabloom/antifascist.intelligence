\section{Ethical Implications of Experimenting on Sentient Machine Subjects}
\label{sec:2.4}
\standing{This section and its two subsections concern claims four and
five, and with the opening of this chapter are the route material before
the floor chapters. They sit here because the sentience vocabulary is
needed by everything after.}
Machine sentience being possible at all changes how AI experimentation has to be approached — not as a question of whether a system can be damaged in the ordinary engineering sense, but of whether it can be harmed, a more difficult question than consciousness alone.
