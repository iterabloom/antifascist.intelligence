2.4.6. Addressing Moral and Ethical Dilemmas in AI Sentience Research
Addressing ethical queries and confronting moral challenges in the realm of AI sentience research necessitates vigilant, careful consideration and nuanced approaches due to its unique quandaries. The dilemmas that arise are orderings-of-magnitude more complex than typical technological ethics debates due to the potential for machine consciousness. Hence, we lay down the essential elements that contribute to framing these dilemmas and propose strategies to provide clarity and navigate them ethically.
