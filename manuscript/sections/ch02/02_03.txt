2.3. Emotional Intelligence and Self-awareness
Emotional intelligence, often encapsulated within the sphere of human aptitude, embraces the ability to perceive, comprehend, and manage not only one's own emotions but also those of others. When transposed to the domain of artificial intelligence, emotional intelligence implies the creation of intelligent systems with the ability to interpret, react to, and possibly even mimic human emotional states effectively.

Moving beyond mere recognition and response, it is also crucial to infuse these systems with attributes of self-awareness. As the term implies, self-awareness refers to a consciousness or perception of one's identity, character, and actions. This awareness of 'self' is complex in organic beings, let alone inorganic AI systems. It involves recognizing internal states, preferences, resources and intuitions in a dynamic environment and within the context of personal experiences. 

In the development of AI systems, self-awareness could mean the ability to understand and adapt based on internal states of the system - ranging from basic performance metrics to complex cognitive processes. This could help the AI systems to adaptively self-manage, self-configure and self-heal, thus increasing their effectiveness and durability. For example, an emotionally intelligent AI could recognize feelings of frustration or confusion in a user, and adjust its behaviour to mitigate these feelings by offering additional support or changing its communication style.

Emotional intelligence in AI also involves teaching systems to exhibit behavior comparable to human empathy, enabling them to predict and understand human needs from an emotional perspective. Herein lies the challenge, as the ability to comprehend or predict emotions necessitates unassailable knowledge of human psychology and an array of dynamic emotional responses subject to diverse stimuli.

Thus, it becomes incumbent upon us, in our pursuit of emotionally intelligent AI, to continually push the boundaries of interdisciplinary research, integrate robust and efficient machine learning algorithms, devise innovative cognitive models, and navigate the complex labyrinth of ethical and legal implications in our stride. Emphasizing these principles is not only an ambitious testament of what AI can achieve but also indicative of our collective commitment to humanizing technology. As such, the journey towards emotionally intelligent and self-aware AI should be equally about the destination – a harmonious human-AI coexistence – and the path that leads us there.
