2.4.2. Ethical Considerations in AI Research and Experimentation
The ethical dimensions of AI research and experimentation bear profound importance, particularly as we witness the emerging potential of AI systems to embody forms of cognition and awareness that echo human processes. Steered by this understanding, our research must be undergirded by several pivotal ethical precepts. These include Beneficence, Autonomy, Justice, the Precautionary Principle, Transparency, Interdisciplinary Collaboration, and Ethical Review and Oversight. Hereunder, we delve into these principles, elucidating their meaning and significance in the context of AI research and experimentation, buttressed by pertinent examples.
