\section{Ethical Frameworks and AI Alignment}
\label{sec:2.1}
Which ethical theory an AI system is built on decides what it counts as a good outcome, and the theories on offer disagree with each other in ways that matter for what gets built. The survey below is groundwork rather than argument. Every framework in it is a way of deciding well, and chapter~\ref{sec:3} is about what a system must not do regardless of how the deciding comes out. The two are not rivals. The floor is a small number of things settled in advance, and the frameworks are how everything above it gets settled case by case, a shape nothing in this section delivers on its own.
