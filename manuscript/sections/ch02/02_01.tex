\section{Ethical Frameworks and AI Alignment}
\label{sec:2.1}
Which ethical theory an AI system is built on decides what it counts as a good outcome, and the theories on offer disagree with each other in ways that matter for what gets built. Every framework surveyed below is a way of deciding well, and chapter~\ref{sec:3} is about what a system must not do regardless of how the deciding comes out. The two are not rivals. What this section recommends is a hybrid approach — a small number of things settled in advance, with everything above them settled case by case — and it is a shape nothing here delivers on its own.
