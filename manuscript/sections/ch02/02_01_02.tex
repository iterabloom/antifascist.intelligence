\subsection{Translating Ethical Principles into Machine Learning Objectives}
\label{sec:2.1.2}
Turning ethical principles into machine learning objectives means continually revising an AI system's goals, values, and decision-making in light of what those principles require — distilling a broad ethical construct into a well-defined, measurable target that a training process, an evaluation criterion, or a learning algorithm can use directly. Fairness, for instance, becomes a metric that monitors bias in the system's outputs, paired with an optimization strategy that reduces it.

Demographic parity and equality of opportunity are two such metrics. Demographic parity requires that every demographic group receive advantageous outcomes at the same rate; equality of opportunity requires equal odds of a favorable outcome for equally qualified applicants, regardless of demographic group.

An AI medical diagnostic system makes the process concrete: beneficence, promoting well-being, becomes minimizing false positives; non-maleficence, preventing harm, becomes minimizing false negatives. Fairness becomes maintaining balanced diagnostic accuracy across patient demographics. Privacy becomes a specific technical commitment — differential privacy or a comparable technique to keep sensitive patient data from being exposed to misuse.

What defeats the translation is not vagueness in the principles, which can be stipulated away, but that the stipulation is where the ethics now lives. Someone chose minimizing false negatives as what non-maleficence means here, and chose the demographic groups across which accuracy is balanced, and chose what to do when balancing them costs accuracy overall. Each choice is defensible and each is a moral decision made by a person and then compiled into something that looks like a specification. A system optimizing the result has no access to the decision and no way to reopen it, which is why section~\ref{sec:3.1}'s first branch — the floor as a property of the artifact — cannot notice when the reason for a constraint has died. Whatever was decided at translation time is what the system will still be doing years later, stated in a metric that has stopped meaning what it meant.

Reinforcement learning is where many of these translated objectives get enforced during training; section~\ref{sec:4.2.1} works through what can go wrong when a reward signal is supposed to encode a value like this and doesn't quite manage it.
