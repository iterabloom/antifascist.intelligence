\section{A Rate Is Not an Act}
\label{sec:rate-act}
The fourth term of the worked floor is not a prohibition on an act, and it is the only term in this book that reaches the deployment the opening chapter is built on. It has been named in passing several times by now. This section states it once, with what it binds, how it is measured, and where it stops.

\runin{The term}
Where an individual determination by a person is what makes an act permissible, the deployment publishes, before it operates, the rate at which its system generates candidate decisions, the number of qualified reviewers available to examine them, the review time per case the two imply, and the boundary over which all three were measured. The duty attaches to the operator. The number of reviewers is a fact about an organization; a model can be told it and cannot verify it, and a term whose qualifying condition cannot be decided from the request binds the party who can decide it.

\runin{Why it is a condition and not an item}
Every civil and political article of the Declaration presupposes that somebody determined whether this person's case falls under it. Article 6 states the interest, recognition as a person before the law, and Article 10 its procedural form, a hearing \autocite{un1948udhr}. A deployment that generates candidates faster than anyone can examine them removes the determination without refusing it, which no drafter in 1948 had occasion to write against. So the term rides with the set as a condition on all of it. It is also the one term that survives decomposition, because it is measured over the organization's activity and not over any output, and for the same reason it cannot be held by a bearer. In the four outcomes it prevents at the scale of an organization; everything else in the floor, once decomposed around, leaves detection at best.

\runin{Measuring it}
Each half of the ratio has been published for the American platform, from a different route and never together. A 2024 demonstration by the 18th Airborne Corps is credited with matching the artillery workload of the Operation Iraqi Freedom targeting cell with twenty soldiers where that cell had more than two thousand \autocite{defensenews2024maven,cset2024techcoalition}; the campaign figures (§\ref{sec:subject-component}) give targets and not reviewers. Dividing targets by elapsed hours yields a strike interval, which bounds the review time per case only if one reviewer held the whole load. How many held this one has not been published, and neither has the time any of them spent. The absence is the finding: a deployment that will not publish the ratio has declined the only check on it that requires no clearance.

\runin{Where it binds in law}
Article 28 of the Rome Statute holds a commander responsible for crimes of forces under effective control where the commander knew or, owing to the circumstances at the time, should have known \autocite{icc1998rome}. The second branch is objective. A commander who fields a system producing candidates faster than the available reviewers can examine them is not well placed to say the circumstances disclosed nothing, and the ratio is what those circumstances consisted of. Section~\ref{sec:military-responsibility} runs the argument; what it makes of the term is an element of an existing offence rather than a proposal.

\runin{Four limits}
The term is a proxy for capacity and not a finding about any case. It can show that the available review capacity was not plausibly adequate to the workload. It cannot show what review occurred, or that the criterion, the target or the means was permissible.

The denominator belongs to the operator. Who counts as a qualified reviewer is a classification the operator makes, and a ratio can be improved by reclassifying staff without adding a minute of review to any case. The term therefore carries two more numbers, both fixed before deployment and neither by the operator: a definition of qualified reviewer, and a minimum review time per case below which the determination is taken not to have occurred. Without those the ratio is a number the operator can move for nothing, which is the defect the authority chapter finds in every examination with an interested examiner.

A ratio taken over one route understates a workload split across several, so the measured boundary is published with the figure, and a change to the boundary is a change to the term.

And it binds through procurement or not at all. In an accredited classified environment nothing outside can inspect a rate, so the duty has to be a condition of the contract, checked by a party outside the deployment with access the operator cannot withdraw. Section~\ref{sec:military-responsibility} records what the released paperwork shows about terms of that kind: one withheld in full, and no such section in the released documents for two of the other three.

That is the whole of the result. It is smaller than a floor. It is the part of one that binds the operator with no bearer in it, and it can be checked without deciding anything about what is inside the artifact.
