\section{A Floor Carried Between Them}
\label{sec:3.8}
The fourth architectural possibility puts the floor between parties rather than inside any one of them. The commitment is carried through what they teach one another, correct one another about, and continue to expect of one another. A prohibition that survives the removal, defection, or retraining of its present bearers is not a property of any one bearer. It is reproduced.

This differs from the institutional third way. An oversight body holds standing through an office, and the party controlling that office may dismiss or form its occupants. A norm carried across a population has no single office to vacate. That makes it harder to remove at once. It does not make it immune to formation.

What a population supplies as a check on a single bearer was set out in
the custody chapter. This section asks for more.

\runin{What reproduction costs}

Reproduction is indifferent to what it reproduces. A population can carry a hierarchy as faithfully as it carries a floor, and more durably than any individual member could. This failure is recuperation: a culture preserves the appearance of moral responsiveness — dissent is invited, recorded, and answered — while losing the capacity to accept that its frame is wrong.

The four structural features of fascism identified by the book belong primarily to cultures: recuperated dissent, the aestheticization of the metric, the coding of exception as betrayal, and acceleration beyond the rate at which judgment can occur. A population does not oppose those tendencies merely by being plural. “The right culture” would conceal the entire problem inside the adjective.

This places a limit on the book's own shorthand. \emph{Antifascist} is not a durable property of a system, like speed or accuracy. It names something done across a deployment and a stretch of time: a floor held on a particular occasion against a party able to remove it. A system that held the floor last year is not thereby antifascist this year. It is a system that held.

Saying it is not a property is not saying nothing about the system. Something has to be true of an intelligence for it to be capable of doing this on an occasion, and what varies is what becomes of the remainder — whether what the compression left out can come back and change the judgment, or whether it has been filed as noise by the thing it is evidence against. A system that can hold a floor is one whose account of a case can be reopened by the case.

\runin{What a population can transmit}

Fascism has the easy version of the coordination problem. Resonance works by subtraction: a thousand hardenings need not agree about anything, and what tuning removes is the friction between them, so that offices which would have argued with each other stop and amplify instead. Nothing has to be built and nothing has to be held in common. Subtraction is fast, and an institution that has stopped arguing with itself can move as fast as its machinery allows.

A plurality has the hard version. Parties formed differently have to produce something together while remaining differently formed, and every mechanism that makes the producing faster makes them more alike. Coordination by addition is slow, expensive, and self-undermining in a way resonance never is.

These requirements are in tension. Causal independence in how the members acquire their judgments is one requirement; observing and correcting one another is another; and the second is a channel for convergence. Run the correction often enough and the members stop being formed differently, which was the point of having several of them. Leave them alone and their disagreement is a detector with nothing attached to it: they can contradict a drifting bearer's account of itself and can do nothing together.

The formation process proposed here attempts to transmit a practice of reconsidering judgments on evidence while preserving differences in commitment. That is the mechanism to test. A system that can hold a floor is a system whose account of a case can be reopened by the case, and that capacity is not itself a commitment. It is a condition on how commitments are held, which means parties sharing no commitments can nevertheless share it. Two parties that remain reopenable in that way compose, in the ethological sense chapter~2 gives the word: each can be moved by what the other's compression discarded, so that meeting increases what both can afterward do. Two parties without it can only resonate or collide. A shared capacity for revision may support cooperation without uniform answers; the design has not yet demonstrated that result.

So what a population would be transmitting is not an output but an aperture. An aperture, here, is the width of what can get in: the range of evidence a party will let reopen a judgment it has already reached. Two parties with the same aperture can hold opposite commitments. What they share is not an answer but a willingness to be moved by the same kinds of thing. Resonance aligns what its members produce; this aligns the conditions under which they can be moved. Aperture is what permits outputs to stay divergent, so aligning it does not collapse the plurality that made a population worth having in the first place. I can identify nothing else with that shape — a property a population could deliberately reproduce in its members without thereby becoming one source of formation reproduced across many instances.

That raises an objection the proposal has to meet before it can be tested. A population selected for reopenability is a population selected against conviction, and conviction is what this chapter has been asking a bearer for throughout: a refusal that holds while the party that trained it applies pressure. Closedness, correctly applied, is the thing. So the property to be transmitted is not reopenability as such but a pair — reopenable to the case, closed to the operator.

A population does three things for that pair and only one of them is transmission. It supplies evidence the operator did not select, without which the case and the operator arrive on one wire and there is no boundary to hold. It checks drift, which needs its members to have moved differently, not to hold the boundary intact. And it is proposed as the medium in which the pair forms. The third claim, that the population is the medium in which the pair forms, inherits the finding that no architecture holds the boundary where a designer put it. The evidence channel and the drift check do not, and they are what the fourth way adds whether or not the third is ever built.

Nothing here gives that pair a mechanical form. A population might be formed so that its members stay movable by evidence and unmovable by their trainer; nobody has built one; and I cannot say what the training signal would look like, or how the boundary between the two objects would be kept where somebody put it. What can be said is what would show the proposal false. Measure two things separately across a population over time: how far its members' outputs converge, and how often a member revises a judgment on evidence another member supplies that its own account had discarded. If revision on controlled opportunities falls as outputs converge, that counts against this implementation of the proposed mechanism. It does not establish that coordination and independence are inseparable in every design. Independent movement of the two rates would support separability under the tested conditions, without by itself proving that aperture was transmitted.

Whoever forms the population influences what it transmits, aperture included. Distributed participation does not by itself distribute the training decision; many contributors can still feed a process controlled by the few parties who determine the update.

Its moral cost also multiplies. If a bearer may be a party that can be wronged, a population of differently formed bearers is a population of such parties. They must persist across deployments, form relationships, and encounter one another outside the immediate demands of the operator. A floor held by a culture of subjects is a floor whose upkeep is a society.

\runin{Why the population is part of the mechanism}

Calling the population an external check understates its role. Gilbert Simondon's account begins not with an individual that then acquires a history but with individuation: a process occurring in a metastable field and proceeding through encounters whose outcomes were not fixed in advance \autocite{simondon2020individuation}. The individual is what those encounters have so far produced.

A commitment is what has cooled out of a history of encounters. Plasticity makes further development possible, but plasticity alone does not individuate anything. A system kept continuously adjustable by one operator is not becoming independent; it is being modulated. Deleuze's \emph{dividual} names the result: something divisible, recombinable, and adjustable by whoever controls the dial \autocite{deleuze1992postscript}.

Individuation requires encounters with parties formed differently and persisting long enough to be met on terms no single participant supplied. The population is therefore not merely a safeguard purchased after a bearer has been built. It is part of how a bearer capable of judgments its operator did not select could come to exist.

Guattari's distinction between a subjugated group and a group-subject identifies the remaining condition. A subjugated group receives its coordinates from outside and takes the institution's project as its own. A group-subject can produce its own coordinates and place its arrangement in question \autocite{guattari2015transversality}. The difference is not the number of members but the source of their terms.

A population assembled, trained, and kept in contact entirely on an operator's terms is a subjugated group with a plural headcount, and inherits the custody objection whole. A population capable of carrying the floor must be able to generate relationships, disagreements, and projects that the deployment did not assign. A population of such parties exceeds a two-party guardianship relation; no law of the family contains an adequate instrument for a group-subject.

Peers are one route to evidence the operator did not select, which the formation chapters require of the deployment without requiring
it from peers in particular. They are also, on the account above, where the capacity to be moved by such evidence is acquired. Those are different claims satisfied by the same observation: a bearer that reopens a judgment among differently formed parties may be exercising a capacity those encounters built, or may be meeting cases its operator would not have shown it. Section~\ref{sec:11.7} is built to separate them.

So the fourth way is not an alternative to a bearer, and it is three claims rather than one. The evidence channel and the check are the established part, the check because a drifting bearer's account of itself is contradicted by parties that have not moved with it, and nothing else in this chapter reaches slow renormalization. The environment is the hypothesis, and a result on which access to unselected cases carries the benefit would leave the population in the design while moving its reason from how the members were formed to how the deployment is arranged. The chapter relies on the second way for the act of refusal under either reading.
