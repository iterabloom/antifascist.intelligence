3.2. Aligning AI Systems with Humane Values and Antifascist Principles
Ethical superintelligence depends on AI systems that comprehend and integrate humane values and antifascist principles. Value alignment is the process of aligning an AI system's objectives with human values, and it takes different shapes across six learning paradigms: reinforcement learning, inverse reinforcement learning, cooperative inverse reinforcement learning, supervised learning, unsupervised learning, and transfer learning.
