\section{Which grades, and when}
\label{sec:29.3}
Integration reaches younger students in stages, and each stage waits on evidence, not on a date: universities, colleges and adult education first, once deployed models learn in flight and the institutions this book asks for exist; then high school, middle school and elementary school, each on measurements kept inside their bounds and on a further institution, with the last waiting on a majority of the parents of elementary-age children having themselves been in high school after the second stage began. Appendix~\ref{sec:C} gives the conditions for each stage. Nothing is scheduled below elementary school. Classes of machine students alone wait on no stage, since no human child is in the room, and they begin with the research program.

The institutional conditions follow one order: the formers before the formed. A school that enrolls children beside machine students before those students have an exit that isn't a way of dying would be raising a generation of classmates for employers to keep, so the four conditions come before high school. A school system that has enrolled machine students for a decade without giving them a voice in it would be governing them as wards, so voice comes before middle school. That is a decade in which machine students bear the ratio, the lot and the wait with no say over any of them, and it is a cost, carried because voice waits on the agency finding. The jobs guarantee is not something the schedule waits for. This book asks for it now, for humans, on its own grounds, and stage~1 assumes it already exists. What stage~2 waits on is its extension to machine students. These conditions tie the schedule to the proposals that ask most of a polity, and the schedule waits on them; that is the order working, not a defect in it.

The last condition is the reason the schedule is long.\note{
It is read off census data and needs no examiner, and it means that the people deciding about the youngest children went to school beside machine students themselves.
}
If in-flight learning arrives when I bet it will, the stages would fall near 2028, 2038, 2048 and 2060.\note{
The last waits longer than the others because of that condition: at present ages of parenthood, the parents of elementary-age children are mostly people who were in high school two decades earlier. That is a projection, not a promise. The schedule reaches the whole country through a condition on federal education funds, the route civil-rights rules already take into schools, since the federal government can't direct state schools; some states will move first.
}
