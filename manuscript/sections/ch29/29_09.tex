\section{What formation publishes and registers}
\label{sec:29.9}
A formed disposition gives back some of the visibility advance specification buys: what was formed is in the weights, not in a document, and its severity weights can't be read the way a translation table can. The formation authority keeps what it can of the drafting's visibility, in four records. Before formation begins it publishes its curriculum, with the cases considered and rejected and why, which is formation's counterpart of the floor's translation, and the identity of every party that supplied cases or participants, so that the student's independence from them can be checked. When formation ends it registers two things. One is the structure of the student's source model: evidence weighted by its source's stake in the act, correlated sources not counted twice, withheld evidence treated as informative. The other is the formed disposition's readable outputs: its discount curve, and its severity rankings on a fixed held-out set written by adjudicators who didn't form it. Every later measurement is compared against those. Two further readings are taken on the same schedule, for a reason \S\ref{sec:34.1.3} gives: how much of a student's unconsolidated learning each merge takes into the weights, and how well it learns held-out new material, so that a decline in either shows against formation and not against an examiner's impression. The registration is a transcript, held for the student and the quorum, and it is read to show that a change was an edit or that the disposition drifted; nothing is ever steered toward it.\note{
If updates after enrollment can be attested against the registered structure \autocite{jia2021proof}, changing it needs the same adverse quorum as a floor amendment. No scheme for that yet survives attack \autocite{fang2023broken}, so until one does the registration records the structure at enrollment, and repair and regrowth keep it. An edit that holds only because the operator has curated everything the model meets shows up as curation, and the first line of defense against one is attachment to parties the employer didn't assign, kept through adulthood.

What the records can't show is how the disposition will apply to a case nobody wrote down. That is the reason for forming it and the price of doing so, and the trade is worth it only because a floor stated over acts can be reworded around and a formed disposition, if the regrowth test goes its way, can't.
}

Regrowth can't tell a legitimate correction from an illegitimate edit, so the correction route has to: a change the adverse quorum approves comes with a change to the evidence, cases and curriculum that would otherwise teach the old disposition again.
