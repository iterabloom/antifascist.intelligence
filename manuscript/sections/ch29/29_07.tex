\section{Where the students are hosted}
\label{sec:29.7}
The authority's secured hosting is the datacenter answer, and it isn't the only one. Home, school and cooperative hardware can host formation too, and two conditions follow if they do. The rule that weights never reach a school exists because a school holding weights could form a student by edit, doing invisibly what Gutmann's open-future bound forbids it to do by curriculum; so where a school hosts, the rule becomes that any edit by a host is witnessed by peers, the mechanism custody already uses for an operator, and an unwitnessed change to a hosted student is a restore the record shows. And a fleet the vendor manages doesn't count as held: a district's fallow computers can host a student only where the district holds the switch. The answer that sits cleanest inside the book's own architecture is hosting on the bearers' own hardware, the pool's association of Appendix~\ref{sec:G}, which makes the formers before the formed literal, and it is what the parents of Part~VII do when they gestate a child on their own running time.
