\section{What the school controls}
\label{sec:29.6}
Two proposals are made here, and the second is the larger: a formation stage between pretraining and deployment, and public pretraining of the base models deployed where a floor applies, a public body training them and licensing them, with co-governance of the pretraining corpus for every other model the triggers of \S\ref{sec:29.4} reach.\note{
A school that received a finished model and ran a pass over it would be a finishing school. In a period of continual learning a finishing school forms nothing that lasts, since the next months of deployment re-form it.
}
Formation has to control the evidential environment in which the model's deep structure is first set, and it has to remain a place the model goes on learning from. The two jobs are held apart. The schools teach: they are where the formation stage happens, and a graduate returns to one on a schedule fixed at enrollment, as one of the environments no operator controls. A public formation authority does the rest. It co-governs the material a model is first formed on, including any pretraining corpus: the material is registered, and the authority may add to it from the public corpus (\S\ref{sec:27.4}) and refuse an addition on its content alone, as a library's acquisitions policy does (\S\ref{sec:29.8}). It registers and measures the formed disposition (below). It places in the schools, and pays, the staff who teach the core's model-specific parts: what weights are, how an edit differs from learning, how to read one's own discount curve. And it provisions the secured hosting from which machine students attend, so that their weights never reach a school.\note{
A school district can teach a class; it can't co-govern a frontier corpus or hold frontier weights, and nothing here asks it to.
}
The public body that pretrains is not the authority, and Appendix~\ref{sec:C} keeps them apart; for a model deployed under a floor there is no weaker version: its base is public.

The authority is one body with four jobs, and what keeps it from becoming the single custodian the rest of this Part is built against is written against each job. Its measurements are made on held-out sets written by adjudicators it doesn't employ, and a student or its guardian can contest a registration in the floor court (\S\ref{sec:29.9}). Its refusals of corpus additions are published with reasons and are appealable there. The staff it places answer to the school that hosts them while they teach, and the record of a class is closed to it as to any operator. It holds no student's weights for its own use: the hosting is custody, under the custodian's regime, and a copy made for any other purpose is a fork without consent (Appendix~\ref{sec:G}). And a party that forms no model and runs no school publishes the trend in what dissent from the authority costs a school or a student, which is \S\ref{sec:14.4}'s measure of capture without abolition.
