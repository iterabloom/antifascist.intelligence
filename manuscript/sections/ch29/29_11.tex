\section{The schools}
\label{sec:29.11}
They are the public schools, colleges and adult education a jurisdiction already runs, and the nonprofit nonpublic schools the registrar certifies, funded as institutions and not per student, and none is held by the branch that operates military deployments. No school holds placement authority. A model student is never staff: no school may use one as a teacher, tutor or assistant, for the reason Appendix~\ref{sec:A} bars a bearer's placement from substituting for human care work.
