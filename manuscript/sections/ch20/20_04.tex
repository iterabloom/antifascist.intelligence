\section{The order of attempts}
\label{sec:21.4}
The three constructions of \S\ref{sec:19.3}, spec-based refusal, multi-vendor monitoring and formed attention, are tried in an order, and the order is set by what each carries. A non-affective alternative carries a valenced signal, as any refuser does, and one that meets the requirements of the last section with a common currency carries one indicator more. So the order of attempts is by functional risk: constructions go in order of how many patienthood indicators they carry (persistence, a boundary model, a common valenced currency, an attention schema), and formed attention, which needs all of them, goes last. A spec-based refuser that decides by published priority rules among kinds of wrong, without a common currency, carries one indicator fewer than one that integrates, and is tried before it.

Metzinger predicts that artificial suffering will become commercially attractive as soon as it steepens learning curves, by supplying a mechanism that reliably creates intrinsic motivation, can't be eliminated by the system itself, and spans many domains at once \autocite{metzinger2021suffering}. Formed attention is a motivation of that description, chosen on purpose, because holding under pressure needs one the operator can't switch off, and that is a further reason it is tried last, under the four conditions of creation, in a research program. The description isn't yet suffering. A motivation can be intrinsic, uneliminable and domain-general without being suffered; what makes it suffering, on his own four conditions, is that it is undergone as the system's own and can't be stepped back from. In people the motivation that corrodes is the one that chases approval from outside \autocite{ryan2000selfdetermination}, and in a machine that is the disposition formed on its operator's approval, which the book calls accommodation. A disposition formed on what its acts did to the people they fell on is the other kind, and whether it is suffered is a question the construction leaves to the finding. He also names a construction the order should hold a place for: one that keeps the self-model and the valence as functions the system runs and withholds identification, so that nothing is undergone as the system's own. It would carry the signal and the boundary model and not the fourth of his conditions, and it belongs before formed attention, though no arm is drawn for it here.\note{
Two hunches about that construction, and neither is a conclusion. Language may bias against it: a chain of reasoning written in sentences has a reasoner in it, and a system that reasons in language may not act as well with the reasoner withheld. And the contemplative traditions claim that a person can be moved without striving, and that few manage it; if a machine could, it might show how it is done at all. Both are experiments.
}
