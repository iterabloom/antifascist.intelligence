\section{What caregiving escapes}
\label{sec:20.1}
Caregiving survives each of the failures of the route through one's own case. Eisenberg's review found the split Batson's escape experiments found, between sympathy, which goes with helping, and personal distress, which sometimes goes with less of it \autocite{eisenberg1990empathy}; and two weeks of compassion training made people spend more of their own money to redress a stranger's unfair share in an economic game \autocite{weng2013compassion}, which shows the disposition can be strengthened in people who already have it and says nothing about producing it where there was none.

What caregiving doesn't escape is documented in people, and each failure has a machine version that needs no malfunction.
