## Why this title

**It has to cover the section and not only its ending.** The body is the science:
Ekman's Fore study, the in-group advantage, the 2019 APS-commissioned review
finding that emotional state cannot be reliably inferred from facial movement,
and the EU AI Act ban that rests on the science failing rather than on misuse.

and lets the section answer what is being measured instead**, which is the
chapter's own register — §11.8 is *The Responsibility Gap, Which No Experiment
Closes*, and §11.2 is *What Is Owed to a Bearer, and Who Could Check*.

**It drops the question mark.** Two of chapter~11's nine titles were questions;
this section stopped being a question when its closing paragraph said the
question was the wrong one. §11.5 keeps the form.
