## The assessment, and where it was wrong

The transcript agrees with the book wherever the book has a position. Its first two turns are
§5.2.1:3 to :7 line for line, the three-way split, the Singer and Klimecki study, the burnout
reading and Bloom's spotlight, and its con-artist point is §2.2.1:14. Its third turn converges on
§3.2:13's definition of mattering by another route and adds the distinction the book blurs, caring
as a standing disposition against feeling as an episode, with Helm's account of caring as a pattern
of emotions over time. Its fourth turn, the author's own objection that deliberation is felt too,
reaches a distinction the book needs, epistemic feelings that carry thought against evaluative
feelings that make outcomes matter. Its weakest paragraph asks a single ventromedial case to show
that knowing what matters without affect yields paralysis, which the book declines at §2.2.3:3 and
§5.3.1:11, and it was not taken.

**One recommendation was wrong and the author caught it.** The assessment filed the transcript's
sentence on autistic people under D-137 as if the rule barred it. D-137's participation test is
about findings that research produced; Kennett ran no study and had no participants, and the P58
record cuts her on the other ground, a philosophy paper built from the clinical literature and two
autobiographies used for a conclusion their authors reject. The assessment then paraphrased the
transcript's *muted* as *without*, which the author called a flattening, and it was: it defined the
group by an absence, the move the D-137 review faulted, and it overstated the transcript. The book
was checked for Kennett and ignores her: no mention in any section, no entry in the bibliography,
the entry retired at P58; the two autism sentences left in the manuscript, at §11.8 and §12.1, report
a product screening out autistic candidates, a documented harm and not a claim about the population.

targets. The alexithymia co-occurrence holds in their words. Milton frames the difference as
mutual. None uses *compassion* as a separate term. So the sentence changed direction under the
check, and the transcript's *muted* does not survive it.

## The six edits, and the ruling on each

All six on *please apply all of this*.

- **§5.2.1** gains a paragraph after the Singer and Klimecki study: *Autistic adults asked about
  their own empathy give the separation from the other side. The reading of others' cues is
  effortful and uneven, the feeling is more often excessive than absent, and what they reject is
  the claim that they lack it; where the reading fails between autistic and non-autistic people it
  fails in both directions.* Cited to Kimber and Milton.
- **§5.2.1:3** says the word is asked to cover three distinct things, *and one census of the word's
  uses finds eight*, cited to Batson.
- **§2.2.3:22**, after the second-order volitions sentence: *The shape is all that is borrowed:
  Frankfurt's own account of caring puts it on the affective side, an investment that leaves the
  one who cares vulnerable to the object's losses and susceptible to its gains*, cited to 1982.
- **§3.4:31**, after the sentence on holding a reason under sustained pressure: *Holding in that
  sense has an account: on Bennett Helm's, to care about something is to have a rationally
  structured pattern of felt evaluations focused on it and projectible over time, and the emotions
  are what the caring consists of*, cited to Helm 2001.
- **§3.2:13**, inside the definition of affective concern: *The feeling meant is the kind by which
  an outcome is bad for the system, and it is a different kind from the one by which an inference
  feels right: William James counted a feeling of if and a feeling of but among the felt parts of
  thought, every reasoner has those, and they are about the argument and about nobody.* The next
  sentence now says a system for which nothing *of the first kind* is felt has rankings.
- **§11.7 retitled** *Open Questions in Theory-of-Mind Research*. Its body is theory of mind
  throughout and the old title used *empathy* in the umbrella sense §5.2.1:3 declines. The heading,
  `ORDER.tsv`, `outline.tsv` and `ledger.tsv` carry the new title, the TOC is regenerated, and no
  other section points at it.
