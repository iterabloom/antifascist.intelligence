# P164 — the fascism detector cut

Author instruction: delete the classifier proposal; keep the four features and
the five discriminators strictly as an analytic method used on institutions that
can be named, and above all on the book's own pipeline. **The stated reason is
the pass's whole argument**: the detector was the only fundable object in the
book, and the book's own instrument predicts what happens to fundable objects.

## What was removed

**§11.3, *What an Antifascist Detector Would Actually Have to Detect*, is gone**:
1,787 words. Chapter~11 renumbered 11.4–11.9 to 11.3–11.8;
`renumber-map_2026-09-10.tsv` is the translation.

**Its honest content moved to §2.1.2 as a limit rather than a research gap.**
Two of the five discriminators are facts about what an institution rewarded, no
organization publishes them, and the definition therefore tells you that for two
of the five you will not find it unless you are already inside. Stated as the
condition of using the definition, not as a gap a funder could close.

*Stochastic Parrots* opportunity-cost argument already lands on this book.
