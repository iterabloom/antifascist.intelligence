# P169 — annealing credited to the optimization lineage

Author instruction, answering a question P167 left open: the annealing metaphor
was meant in the numerical-optimization sense, and — as the author added — that
is itself a reference to metallurgy.

**`kirkpatrick1983annealing`**, *Optimization by Simulated Annealing*, `Science`
220(4598), 671–680, 13 May 1983, verified against the journal record.

**The lineage went into the prose and not only the note**, because it changes
what the book is claiming. §3.1 now says the word is on its third stop rather
than in a fresh metaphor — *the metal, then the search, then the training run* —
and states the fact the argument needs: *cooled too fast, the search freezes into
the first tolerable arrangement it reached.*
