## Deleuze, on modulation

*Postscript on the Societies of Control*, `October` 59 (Winter 1992), 3–7. **The
original is earlier and French**: "Postscriptum sur les sociétés de contrôle,"
*L'Autre Journal* 1, May 1990. The translator of the `October` printing could not
be confirmed and the entry says so rather than guessing; Martin Joughin is
verified for the *Negotiations* reprint.

The passage was pulled verbatim rather than paraphrased from memory: *enclosures
are molds, distinct castings, but controls are a modulation, like a
self-deforming cast that will continuously change from one moment to the other.*
**A second sentence turned out to be the better find** — *perpetual training tends
to replace the school* — which is continual learning named as the control form in
1990, and §3.1 now uses it.

## Virilio, on the suicide state, and one correction

**The obvious citation was wrong.** *Speed and Politics* would have been the
guess; its table of contents was checked first and does not contain the chapter.
"L'État suicidaire" is in the earlier *Essai sur l'insécurité du territoire*
(Paris: Stock, 1976; reissued Galilée 1993), which is what Deleuze and Guattari
take up. The entry records the distinction so the substitution is not made again.
§2.1.2 now says the reading *is Paul Virilio's before it is theirs.*
