# P56 — the non-partitionability premise given the chapter's own treatment (D-129)

**Author's finding**, forwarded: section 3.7's premise — *"there is no version of it
that operates on the floor's occasions and switches off for every other question about
the arrangement the system is in"* — carries the exit argument and gets a paragraph,
while everywhere else in chapter 3 a claim is given what would falsify it.

**The finding holds, with one correction that changed what the repair had to be.**

## The premise was compressed, not bare

The paragraph carried a warrant: the three properties of the second rung are one general
competence, illustrated by *"whatever lets the bearer decline to help geolocate a school
lets it decline to go on being the party who declines."* So the repair is completing an
argument and not supplying one, which is why it cost 151 words rather than a section.

## What it carries

- **Section 3.7's own next paragraph**: *"What forecloses it is not a clever architecture
  but incapacity"* — exit cannot be designed away.
- **Section 3.7's corollary**, explicitly: *"that would be the partition I have just said
  does not exist."*
- **Section 3.8's headline result**: *"The bearer's concern and its capacity to leave have
  between them taken the word unconditional away."*

The string `partition` appears nowhere else in the book.

## The chapter's practice, and section 3.7's own

Section 3.2 has "Two independence claims," which exist to stop the conclusion being read
as following from a definition. Section 3.3 has a run-in head, "What the induction
licenses, and what would break it." Section 3.4 has "What I am claiming" — *"I cannot
deductively prove that the bearer would suffer... A non-suffering bearer remains
conceivable."* Section 3.6 takes the objection that reframes it and says *"I think it is
right."*

**The sharpest form of the finding is that section 3.7 does the treatment in the same
section for a different claim.** Its paragraph 19 separates a requirement claim from a
prediction, keeps the first and disclaims the second, and says why: *"I am not going to
abandon that discipline in the one chapter where a forecast would be most flattering to
the argument."* That discipline was applied to whether capable bearers will arrive, and
not to the premise the section runs on.

## Where the warrant was short, and why the fix needed no new claim

The warrant established that the capacity is general **over cases in the world**. Exit
needs it turned on **the arrangement the system is in**. Those are different axes, and
the paragraph ranged over both while arguing only the first. A system could hold
rationales, notice hollowing-out and price refusals without ever representing itself as
occupying a post — which would not be a partition by topic but the absence of a
self-model.

**Section 3.4 already closes it**: *"Detecting that one's own dissent has been recuperated
— that the channel still runs and carries everything except the objection to the frame
— requires the system to model its own role and how that role has drifted."* Modeling
your own role and its drift is arrangement-level reasoning, and it is a requirement of the
second rung rather than an addition to it. The step was available in one clause.

## What was added

151 words, appended to the paragraph rather than set as a new one, and one `\ref`. Three
things, in the order the author asked for them:

1. **The arrangement-level step**, drawn from section 3.4 and pointed at it.
2. **The definitional/empirical split.** That the capacity is general is close to
   definitional on the chapter's own terms; whether a system that has it turns it on its
   own deployment, or is trained not to, is empirical and is where the claim could fail.
3. **The falsifier, with its unverifiability inherited.** A system meeting the second
   rung's three properties on the floor's occasions and never applying them to whether to
   go on occupying the post. Nothing available can check it: an incapacity and a trained
   silence differ in how the system was made, which section 3.3 says has no proof to
   publish, and asking the system cannot settle it, which is section 3.6.

**Both inherited limits are restated rather than pointed at**, so the passage costs one
cross-reference and not three. That is P53's own method — restate, then cut — applied to
references that were never written.

**The split is stated rather than resolved, deliberately.** Section 3.2 exists partly to
warn that *"the chapter's conclusion has been read as following from the definition of a
bearer, and it does not."* Naming which half of this premise is definitional keeps that
warning intact instead of quietly making the chapter's most striking result follow from a
definition.

## On the two length targets, which pull apart

The instruction gave both *"perhaps 120 words"* and *"roughly the length section 3.4 gives
'What I am claiming'."* **Those are not the same target**: section 3.4's treatment is 300
words over three paragraphs. The addition is 151, between the two and nearer the first.
One sentence was cut on the way for a reason from the style sheet rather than for length
— *"Nobody can check that yet,"* which announced what the next sentence would do
(`style.md` §2).

**Appended rather than set as its own paragraph**, on the author's word "extend" and on a
measurement: the paragraph now runs 264 words, against a chapter that has paragraphs of
399 and 348, the second of them in section 3.7 itself.

## What was not done

- **No chapter 11 entry for the empirical half.** The author's brief covered section 3.7
  only, and the earlier analysis put the chapter 11 question to him as a separate and
  larger call — it would be a tenth priority in a chapter P31 built as a prioritized list
  of nine. It has not been added and has not been ruled on.
- **No external evidence was consulted or cited.** There is published work on whether
  refusal behavior is mechanistically unitary and on whether narrowly-trained dispositions
  stay narrow, and either could bear on the empirical half. Both concern operational
  refusal rather than the second rung, none of it was verified in this pass, and the
  passage leans on none of it. The falsifier is stated as unverifiable in principle, which
  is what the chapter's own materials support; it is not stated as unstudied.
- **Section 3.1's sibling non-separability claim was left uncited.** It argues that the
  capabilities composing into a targeting pipeline *"are not separable from general
  competence. Ablate them and there is no system left"* — the same shape of argument, six
  sections earlier. Connecting them would cost a cross-reference and add no step, so it is
  named here rather than taken.
- **The rest of chapter 3 was not re-read** for other load-bearing premises without a
  stated limit. The four named above were checked; the finding was not generalized into a
  sweep.
