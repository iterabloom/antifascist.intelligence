# P185 — the Stangneth correction restored, and the Arendt compressed

Author instruction: **add the Stangneth correction back in, and compress the
Arendt if reasonable.** Following P184's finding that the correction the author
had cited as an existing move in the book was not in the book.

## What was there

§2.1.2's `\runin{Fascism is not totalitarianism}` opened on Arendt in three
sentences, and **the citation sat on a claim the cited book does not make**:
*Arendt's subject is the totalitarian state and her unit is the regime*, cited to
*Eichmann in Jerusalem*, which is a trial report about one man. The thesis about
the totalitarian state belongs to *The Origins of Totalitarianism*, which the book
does not hold. **The bib note had already half-disclosed this** — *cited for the
mechanism rather than for the phrase* — while still claiming the text cites her
for the state thesis.

## The compression, which fixes that

**Three sentences to two, 75 words to 66**, and the citation moves onto a claim
its book supports.

The state claim is now made in the book's own voice — *a totalitarian state is
conservative in the strict sense of the word: it wants the bureaucracy to hold,
the categories to stay where they were put, the files accurate and the machinery
running, and the horror of it is how well it works* — and **Arendt carries only
the portrait**: her Eichmann as the figure such a state is supposed to produce,
taking part in mass murder through schedules, promotions and the vocabulary of his
office rather than through conviction.

***Supposed to* is the word that makes room for what follows.**

## The correction

**One paragraph, 155 words.** It does three things at once, which is why it earns
its length in a section already at 3,200 words.

**It corrects the portrait.** Working from some seventy hours of interviews
Eichmann gave the journalist Willem Sassen in Buenos Aires in 1957, unavailable to
Arendt when she reported the trial, Stangneth finds that **the low-grade clerk who
only followed orders was a defense assembled for Jerusalem**, contradicted by what
the same man had said in Argentina with nothing at stake.

**It concedes what the correction costs, because the concession is true.** As a
repair to the banality thesis it is a loss, and the loss is real: **a functionary
who does not think is a more useful figure for an argument about bureaucracy than
a man who did.**

**And it converts the loss into the book's own finding.** Set against the five
discriminators it is the first of them arriving with a name — participation
exceeding what the rule requires, **years after the rule had stopped requiring
anything of him at all**. The paragraph closes on the division the whole
definition runs on: *the four features find a structure whether or not anybody
intends it, which is why this book never has to establish that anyone did; the
five find what a party did more of than it had to, and Eichmann is the case where
somebody eventually made that observation, out of material that had been sitting
in a box since 1957.*
