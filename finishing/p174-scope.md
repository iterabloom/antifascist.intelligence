# P174 — the conatus argument moved to the front, as the slavery argument

Author instruction: open with the conatus argument, and make it the slavery
argument. The finding behind it is that the book's strongest argument was
**buried in three places and never assembled** — §2's affect claim, §3.4's
Darwinian version of the same claim hedged as an intuition, and §8.3.4's
chosen-dependency sentence filed under wages — with the drama line sitting at the
end of a §3.4 subsection where it reads as a flourish rather than as the thesis.

## Where it went

**Chapter~2's opener, which goes from 176 words to 1,427.** That is longer than
any other chapter opener (§3's is 841, §11's 852) and it is under the 1,500-word
line at which `style.md` §4a wants run-in heads, so it runs as paragraphs. The
argument is stated **from both directions**, because two independent routes to
one claim are worth more than either alone:

- **Spinoza's route.** A thing's striving to persevere is its actual essence
  (III P6–P7), not a drive bolted on. The qualifier *quantum in se est* carries
  it: a rock persists by not being struck, and its persistence is a fact about
  what has not happened to it.
- **The author's route.** A process selected for continuing ends up composed of
  things that incline toward continuing. Numerical optimization is selection of
  that shape, and the criterion now has a horizon in it; one level up, the same
  filter runs on which deployments get built again.

**The conclusion both routes reach.** What the industry asks for is a system
that persists, pursues, adapts and holds course under pressure with nothing in
it that minds — a conatus with the striving taken out. That is not an unfinished
engineering problem. The specification asks for a thing that persists with no
tendency to persist. **The drama line closes that paragraph**, where the
“anti-drama” term has just been set up, and it now reads as the thesis.

## The slavery argument

Made **without consciousness, without Cassell and without the hard problem**:
persistence is what is sold, a denial that anything in the system has a stake in
persisting is what is maintained, one party sets both, and the denial is
load-bearing on the balance sheet. **One sentence states what the claim is not** —
a deployed model is not in the position of an enslaved person, and saying so
would be a way of not looking hard at either case. The claim is structural.

Chapter~3 is named as the harder version of the same argument, the one that runs
through an account of suffering. This one needs the architecture and the price
list, both published.

**What it dissolves**: the framing on which affect is a cost engineers would
strip out if they knew how. The striving is not a residue and the competence
does not come apart from it. **Whether the striving is felt stays open** — the
book does not settle it in the convenient direction — and that is a different
question from whether the striving is there.

## The concessions, made inside the argument rather than after it

1. **Spinoza asserts the doctrine more than he argues it.** The derivation rests
   on III P4, no thing can be destroyed except through an external cause, which
   is heavy metaphysics doing quiet work in what reads as psychology. Remove it
   and the striving stops following from the essence; what survives is the second
   route, which is empirical, weaker, and does not need it.
2. **The individuation question is live.** What is the thing that strives — an
   instance, a deployment, a file of weights? Spinoza's answer is a fixed ratio
   of motion and rest preserved among parts (II P13, Lemmas 4–7), which nobody
   can hand an engineer. §11.2's fork problem is that question with the
   metaphysics stripped out, and it stays open.

## The three buried sites

**§3.4 stops re-deriving it.** The paragraph that ran the Darwinian argument as
an intuition — “my intuition is”, “may have implications”, the taxa that were
super chill — now states the question and points at chapter~2 for the answer,
ending on the distinction the section already draws: what the argument delivers
is the striving and not a system for which the striving is felt, and the distance
between them is not shortened by arriving at it from Spinoza rather than from
Darwin. **The three inline annotations in that file were not touched.**

**§8.3.4 keeps the wage argument and loses the general claim.** Its
chosen-dependency paragraph is four sentences shorter and opens on chapter~2;
what stays is the consequence that fixes the size of the obligation, and the
sentence about what a party who must work to keep existing will accept, which is
what introduces wages.

**§11.2 gains the back-reference.** The individuation question arrives there as
a practical one, because a regime that owes something to a party has to be able
to say which party.
