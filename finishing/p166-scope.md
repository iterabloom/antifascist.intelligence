# P166 — fascism separated from totalitarianism, and tempo put in the definition

Author instruction: Arendt's unit is the totalitarian State — conservative,
wants the bureaucracy to hold. Fascism's distinguishing mark is the line of
abolition: acceleration toward destruction including its own. The fourth feature
(decoupling) is the symptom; the tempo is the thing. This makes §5.1.3
structural rather than incidental.

## The separation

**New run-in in §2.1.2, *Fascism is not totalitarianism*,** between the
discriminators and resonance. Arendt's state *wants the bureaucracy to hold, the
categories to stay where they were put, the files accurate and the machinery
running; its ideal is an apparatus that does not stop, and the horror of it is
how well it works.* Fascism runs the other way, on a line of abolition whose
destruction *does not stop at the enemy it was raised against.* Which is why
tempo is definitional: *a totalitarian state can be slow and remain entirely
itself, and a fascist one cannot.*

**Arendt is restored** from `unused_bibliography.bib` for this, which is the role
that wanted her. The self-demolition order is described rather than named and
cited, because standing rule 1 was still in force at this pass; the Virilio
attribution behind it arrived at P168.

## Tempo in the definition

**Feature 4 is now *acceleration past the rate at which anything can be checked,
of which decoupling is the symptom*.** The mechanism is named: checking costs
time, and an institution whose rate of action outruns the capacity to check it
*has decoupled by construction rather than by mistake. The other three features
protect the gap once it is open; speed is what opens it.* The fourth observable
artifact changes with it, from whether the model is checked against anything
outside itself to whether the rate has outrun the capacity to check it.

## §5.1.3 made structural

The ratio was an auditable safeguard indicator and is now a measurement of the
definition. **The thirteen thousand targets in thirty-eight days at §6.4.1 are
not an aside about a thin review step; they are the definition being satisfied**
— and satisfied in public, the figure given out by the office whose achievement
it was. **The figure was already in the book and cited** to
`breakingdefense2026maven`; it was checked there rather than taken from the
instruction, so no new citation was needed.

One addition beyond the instruction, flagged as such: a rate published as a
triumph is the second feature arriving alongside the fourth.
