# P176 — §5.1.3 rescoped as a veracity instrument

Author instruction: rewrite the indicator as a **veracity instrument, not a
lethality instrument**. It bites only where a deployment claims individualized
human judgment makes the act permissible; it removes an alibi rather than
preventing a harm; its demonstrated mode of operation is publication, not
enforcement. Then name what it does not reach, add Iraq, and run the book's own
second feature on the proposal.

## The rescope

**Three claims now carry the section, and each is stated flat.**

1. **It tests the truth of a claim, not the safety of an act.** Where no
   individualized-judgment claim is made it measures nothing. Where the claim is
   made and the ratio is bad, what has been established is that the claim is
   false, **and that is the whole of what has been established.**
2. **An institution whose ratio fails has lost an alibi rather than been
   stopped**, and every act remains available by any route not running through
   the claim.
3. **Publication is the only mode of operation demonstrated.** The Indiana
   paragraph carries this now: nothing about that system was secret, and what was
   missing was a party positioned to compare the rates and to say so where it
   would be read. **The ratio binds nobody. A version that bound somebody would
   be a regulation, and this is not one.**

The tempo argument survives and is demoted to what it is: **an argument, not a
mechanism.** An institution that accepts it and slows down has been persuaded,
and the number did not do it.

## What it does not reach — the new run-in

Five items, in the order the author gave them.

**Area effects**, where the question of who was individually selected does not
arise. **Ordnance choice**, so a deployment that clears the ratio and then strikes
an approved target with a weapon that takes the building has passed. **The
criterion itself** — a classifier understood inside the unit to be wrong about one
time in ten has a defect review time does not touch, because the reviewer checks
whether the machine applied the criterion and not whether the criterion should be
applied to anybody. **The campaign that drops the individualized-judgment claim
rather than satisfying it**, which steps outside the instrument at a political
price that varies with who is watching and is sometimes nothing.

**And the fifth, which is the one that should worry a reader who accepted
§3.6.** A constraint on a rate survives the decomposition objection there because
a rate is a fact about the assembly. This ratio is that constraint's *auditable
proxy*, and the proxy is measured at the review step — so a pipeline whose model
does enrichment, collation and ranking upstream of any decision a human is
nominally required to approve **yields no ratio to compute while the assembly goes
on working**. The instrument is weaker than the floor item it stands in for, and
**the difference between them is exactly the first interception position.** That
is a gap between item and instrument, not a reversal of §3.6.

## Iraq

**It runs the opposite way from the case the section has been using**, which is
why it belongs. Human Rights Watch's review of the 2003 war: fifty strikes aimed
at named members of the Iraqi leadership killed **none of the intended targets**
and killed dozens of civilians, while cluster munitions — overwhelmingly the
ground-launched ones fired into populated areas — killed or wounded **more than a
thousand**. The target list failed at its own task; the mass lethality came from a
weapon requiring no list, no candidate decision and no reviewer, so there is
nothing in it for the indicator to divide.

**From which the harder possibility follows: a rate constraint may displace
rather than reduce.** A force that cannot approve targets individually fast
enough retains means that do not require approving them individually, and on that
path the ratio improves while the toll does not. The section says so in its own
voice rather than leaving it to a critic.

## The second feature, run on the proposal

**Aestheticize this metric and a deployment clears it and acquires a certificate
of meaningful human control** — a number, defensible, checkable, and about the
wrong thing, since what it certifies is that reviewers had time and not that
anything was reviewed or that the criterion was worth applying.

**Zhuang and Hadfield-Menell are cited against the proposal, not for it.**
Optimizing a proxy defined over a subset of the attributes a principal values
drives overall utility arbitrarily low under resource constraint, which is a
result about proxies of this exact shape. **This indicator is a proxy defined over
a strict subset**, and the entry for `zhuang2020consequences` already described
itself as the formal form of the aestheticized metric — it had been cited only
once, in §2.1.2's own feature list, and never against anything the book proposes.

The defense the section keeps is narrow and stated as narrow: it is a proxy for
the truth of a claim rather than for a good, and being wrong about whether an
institution's account of itself holds up is a smaller error than being wrong about
what a life is worth. It survives on that and on one practice — **the ratio has to
be reported next to what it excludes**, and an institution publishing the number
without the exclusions has produced the second feature rather than a check on the
fourth.
