The author asked how to revise the manuscript in light of the reader-friendliness review in `finishing/reviews/author-discussion_2026-10-05.txt` and the six exchanges after it. A plan was drafted and approved. It is `finishing/revision-plan-for-p221_2026-10-05.md`.

## The author's rulings

Four answers, given before the plan was approved:

1. **D-007 is overridden within this plan's scope.** The plan adds passages, an ending and a new framing, which `style.md` §9 would otherwise bar. Outside the plan's scope D-007 stands.
2. **From the discussion, three ideas go into the text, and one stays out.**
   - In: the reframing of "needs no new kind of machine" (the measures can be adopted before a bearer exists and won't survive a captured operator without one); correctability in §16.2; Arendt, together with the line on compression and monoculture.
   - Out: "scale power down to what accountability can regulate" as a stated thesis.
3. **Part VI's protocols move to an appendix.** Chapter 28 keeps the open problems and which results would prove the book wrong.
4. **Two pairs of terms are renamed:** adoptable/departed term, and first/second tier. "Bearer" stays, and its first appearance gets a scene. The new wording goes to the author before the sweep.
