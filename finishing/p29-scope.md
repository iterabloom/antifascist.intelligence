## Item 1: chapter 2, cut by 20.2 percent

Read the whole chapter against what chapter 3 actually takes from it. Chapter 3
uses section 2.1's hybrid gesture and 2.1.1's setting-aside of deontology, 2.1.4's
structural signature and its first feature, 2.3's performance/competence/agency
vocabulary and its four-capacity table, 2.3.3's checkable self-model, 2.3.4's
affect evidence entire, 2.4.1's ladder and Cassell import and persistence
criterion, 2.4.4's guardianship finding, and 2.4.6's Three Rs. Everything it uses
is intact.

What came out, by class:

- **Prescriptive survey the rest of the book delivers on.** Section 2.1.4's
  seven-strategy list, 640 words of design recommendations that chapters 6, 8 and
  10 actually work out, compressed to one paragraph naming them and pointing at
  where they are priced. The definition, the two-scale argument, the five
  molecular discriminators and D-080's paragraph are untouched.
- **Procedural survey chapter 8 owns.** Section 2.4.2's six principles reduced to
  the three that do work here — beneficence toward the system, precaution, and
  interdisciplinary review — with autonomy, justice and transparency given a
  sentence each against the chapters that own them. 485 words.
- **A boxed history.** The mirror-neuron box in 2.2.1, 465 words on a discovery
  and its overshoot, compressed to a body paragraph that keeps all four cited
  findings and the lesson section 5.1.1's box compares itself to.
- **Inventories.** 2.3.1's four components of emotional intelligence, 2.3.2's
  dated affective-computing snapshot box, 2.3.3's four applications of the
  self-model, 2.4.1's seven-item criteria list, 2.2.3's four opportunities.
- **Residue.** 2.1.3's paragraph about three worked examples the section no longer
  contains; 2.4.4's consent-rights paragraph, which specified protections for a
  case the section's own conclusion has already closed.
- **Compression throughout**, heaviest in 2.4.5 and 2.4.7, which are legal and
  procedural material chapter 8 develops and chapter 3 does not use.

## Item 2: chapter 3 as the center

Done by what leads into it and what follows from it, not by adding to it. Chapter
3 is byte-identical after this pass.

- **Chapter 1's roadmap carried a claim chapter 3 had already reversed.** It billed
  chapters 4 and 5 as "the learned half that sits above that floor," where section
  3.6 concludes they "turn out to be the material the floor is made of." Corrected.
  This is Q-022's complaint, which D-078 closed by declaring in section 3.7 and
  which chapter 1 had gone on contradicting.
- **Chapter 1 now names chapter 2 as what the next chapter argues from**, and its
  closing line makes chapter 3 the hinge rather than one of three carriers:
  the argument turns on chapter 3, chapters 7 and 8 carry what follows from it.
- **Chapter 2's opener** is rewritten around the four results chapter 3 uses,
  and says plainly that the rest of the chapter is groundwork.
- **Four section openers in chapter 5** — 5.2, 5.3, 5.4 and 5.7 — now state what
  the floor takes from them. These are additions, about 120 words in total, and
  the only prose this pass added.

Not done, and available: moving chapter 3 earlier in the book. It sits third of
twelve and reverses D-043's promotion if moved; the author has not asked for it.

## Item 3: chapters 4 and 5, cut by 13.5 percent together

The floor requires, from these two chapters: a goal representation with the
standing to bias competing processes (4.1.2), a disposition acquired by exploring
and therefore written down nowhere (4.3.1), each alignment method judged by
whether it can produce a commitment that survives its own teacher (4.2), a system
that treats its own errors as data (5.1.1), the mentor understood as a control
channel (5.1.3), a moral sense that does not run on supervision (5.6), motivation
pointed at something (5.6.2), and the honest-witness disposition section 3.4 hands
back. All of it is intact.

What came out:

- **A duplication across sections.** Section 4.1.3's transfer run-in and section
  4.2.4 carried the same argument in nearly the same words, down to "arriving a
  third time." 4.2.4 keeps it, with the citation moved there.
- **A duplication inside a section.** Section 5.6.3 stated the genuine-override
  paragraph twice, once mid-section and once at the end, and used the
  content-moderation drift example in both of its run-ins. The end instance, which
  carries the section 3.3 conclusion, is the one kept. This is a defect repair as
  much as a cut.
- **Neuroscience with no design consequence**, which section 4.1.2 says it excludes
  and then included: the working-memory capacity number, the three plasticity
  principles, and a treatment of emotion recognition that chapter 2 and section
  9.2.1 both already give.
- **Boxes and inventories:** the XPRIZE box in 5.5.2, the GDPR box in 5.4.2 folded
  to a clause, the Moral Machine passage in 5.3.3 that section 4.2.3 already makes,
  the few-shot technique families in 4.1.3, the deployed-tools paragraph in 5.4.3,
  and 5.1.4's three applications, which are compressed to keep the auditable
  indicator that is the section's point.
- **Speculation labeled as such:** 5.7.2's game proposals, which the section
  itself calls a proposal rather than a description of research.

**Why 13.5 percent and not 20.** Chapters 4 and 5 were cut 29.9 percent at P11 and
rewritten at P26 to carry chapter 3's obligation. What is left is mostly argument
addressed to the floor, and the survey in them is what came out. Reaching 20
percent means cutting evidence — the inattentional-blindness studies, the
predictive-coding account, the Kohlberg box, the trolley literature section 2.3.4
points at — each of which is what makes a claim in those chapters believable rather
than asserted. Left with the author.

## Considered and declined: removing sections

Chapter 2 has three subsections thin enough after the cut to consider folding —
2.2.2, 2.3.2, 2.4.5 — and chapters 4 and 5 have four more. Folding any of them
renumbers everything after it in its chapter, which rewrites labels across the
book's 796 resolved references eight days after P28 rebuilt the reference regime,
and needs a renumber map on the D-031/D-043 model. Not taken, on the ground that
the instruction asked for a cut and not a restructure. Available on request.
