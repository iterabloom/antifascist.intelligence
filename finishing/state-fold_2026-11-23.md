# The fold of STATE.md's leads, 2026-11-23: what was found closed, and on what evidence (D-1253)

This file is the record behind D-1253. Thirty-three lead paragraphs of `finishing/STATE.md` (the thirty then live, dated 2026-10-29 to 2026-11-23, and the three superseded leads under them that the oldest pointed at, back to the fold of 2026-10-25) were read for every item they stated as open. Language-model agents (Opus subagents) did the reading, the merging and the checking; the session ran them and assembled the result. It is not the author's text, and where it and the repository differ the repository is right.

The steps: three agents extracted 492 open-item statements and the leads' own statements of closure; one merged them into 316 distinct candidates, 64 items a later lead had already closed, and 9 that were moot; six checked the 316 against the tree, each by the phrase an item quotes and not by its section number; one read the 52 verdicts of *closed* a second time; one wrote the open list now at the head of `STATE.md` from the 264 items still open; one checked that list bullet by bullet against its sources. Item numbers (C001 to C316) are those of the merge and appear nowhere else in the repository. Section numbers inside an item are those of the lead that stated it, and many have changed since.

What the check is worth: one agent's reading of the repository against an item another agent extracted, with a second agent's reading for each item taken off the list. It is not a human check.
