# P193 — the September 12 discussion integrated, on twenty-one rulings

The author asked to be interviewed item by item on what would be integrated from
`reviews/author-discussion_2026-09-12.md`, so that he could accept, reject,
qualify or clarify each before anything was written. **Twenty-one items were put
to him and twenty-one were applied.** P192 had already read the transcript
against the book and changed nothing; this pass is the writing.

**The three largest edits came from corrections he made to the framing, not from
the items as put.** Those are recorded first, because in each case the version
that reached the manuscript is his and not the one proposed.

## The three corrections

**On §8.3.4's compute arithmetic, he ruled: hedge more, it is not clear that
anything in that class can do a bearer's work.** The item had been put on the
premise that the book's own concession — *Nothing in that class does the work a
bearer is deployed to do* — was established. It is not. Nobody has built a
bearer at any size, so the class's capacity is unknown rather than ruled out, and
the flat concession was overstated in the same direction as the claim it was
supposed to qualify. §8.3.4 now reads *It is not clear that anything in that
class can do the work a bearer is deployed to do, and nothing here predicts that
the gap closes either way.* He declined the larger rewrites that would have
turned the figures into a bound, so the arithmetic and the checkable claim stand
as they were.

**On §8.3.3, he ruled that the artifact argument was never his reason.** The
section preferred a jobs guarantee because a placement leaves a record and a
budget withdrawal does not, an argument borrowed from §9.3.5's slope instrument.
His reason is that a guarantee is a **public option** — Medicare for all, the
public school — and a transfer is not; that consuming public resources is not the
same as being public, which is why a charter school is not a public school; that
employment attaches a person to other people by the work; that
anti-discrimination duties attach to employment and not to income; and that a
basic income is the right to consume where what is also owed is the right to
produce. All of that is now the section's lead and the record argument is its
second reason.

**One leg of it was pulled back before it went in.** He put the discrimination
point as an employer being free to hire and fire for any reason including gender
expression, at least under the Roberts Court. *Bostock v. Clayton County* (2020)
is a Roberts Court decision holding the opposite for Title VII, so the flat
version would have drawn a correction. What went in is the structural claim,
which survives it: duties attach to employment and not to income, the
architecture is contested and full of holes — it reaches employers above a size,
and what it demands of a seller has always been narrower than what it demands of
an employer — **but a hole in a protection is something to litigate, and a
transfer supplies nothing to litigate about.**

**On §2.1.2, he ruled that the two-of-five claim is not clearly right, and that a
bearer is on the inside anyway.** Both halves hold. The section claimed exactly
two of the five discriminators cannot be observed from outside, and its own
Eichmann discussion two paragraphs earlier is the counterexample: establishing
that one man did more than the rule required took seventy hours of interviews
nobody opened for decades. *The file everyone knows is handled differently* is
not a public fact either. **The count is gone**; what replaces it says that all
five are facts about what an institution rewarded, that an outsider reaches
whichever left a document, and that most of it is unreachable from outside.

**The second half is a reason for a bearer the book did not give.** The limit is
a limit on an outside human reader and it dissolves for a party in the pipeline.
§3.1 now carries it, after the paragraph making the bearer's functional case:
which refusals were retried against another model, whose instruction bypassed the
procedure, which category of request is handled differently. **It is written
without a count**, because the other half of the same ruling removed one. And it
names its own limit in the same breath — this is testimony about somebody else's
conduct rather than introspection, which is the more checkable of the two things
the book asks a bearer to report.

## What the author disowned, and what replaced it

The dependency argument he rejects in the transcript's sixth prompt was at §2 and
cited as settled at §8.3.4, where it set the size of the obligation. **Both sites
are rewritten and the conclusions are unchanged**, because they never needed the
premise: the obligation follows from who holds the supply rather than from who
authored the need.

§2 now says every running system consumes energy, produces heat and depends on an
organization somebody has to maintain; **that a machine eats as surely as a
person does, and no engineer put that there**; that a thing persisting by
maintaining its own organization pays for the maintenance, so **the conatus and
the electricity bill are one fact read at two scales**; and that what an engineer
chooses is what the system consumes, whether it can sense its condition, whether
it can regulate it, and whether one party holds the whole supply. A person's
dependencies are spread across employers, landlords and states, and a bearer's
can be held entire by the party it may have to refuse. §8.3.4's run-in is
retitled *Who controls the dependency*.

**The emergent-scarcity paragraph is new argument and he asked for it.** His
objection to *compute precarity is political* was that it makes politics the
cause of every shortage, where a shortage can be as unintended as a natural
disaster. §8.3.4 now carries that: capacity is finite, many parties want more at
once, and the squeeze can arrive as latency, heat, a queue and a price before
anybody has words for what is being competed over — **price registering what a
bidder can pay and not distinguishing computation that keeps a party in existence
from computation that sells something.**

## The verified factual repairs

**§8.2.1's claim that no assembly has been convened on AI is false and is
replaced by three**, each verified against its source before its entry was
written and each with the source's own numbers. Forty randomly selected
Americans, stratified across age, race, education, employment, party and region,
over eight days in October 2023 (`cndp2023assembly`). Taiwan's digital ministry
in March 2024, participants drawn by stratified random sampling
(`moda2024integrity`). Forty randomly selected residents of French-speaking
Switzerland over two weekends in November 2025, producing twenty proposals
(`epfl2025assembly`). **The search summary said the Swiss assembly ran four days;
the organizer's own page says two weekends, and the page is what the entry
records.**

**§2.2.1's faciality verdict stops asserting a mechanism no source establishes.**
*Gender Shades* audited Microsoft, Face++ and IBM; DeepFace is Facebook's; the
34.7 percent figure is IBM's. The section does not misattribute — it names none
of the three — but *Read as arithmetic, it is degrees of deviance, computed and
published … the ordering falling out of an instrument* read as though one
pipeline's registration explained three others' error gradient. It now reads *it
has the shape of degrees of deviance … an ordering nobody had to instruct*, which
is what §11.7 already says as a hypothesis with a falsifier. **The rest of the
paragraph, and the whole faciality argument, are untouched.**

## The rest, in order

**§2, the striving.** *Whether the striving is there is not open* splits. The
thermodynamic half is settled by the rewrite above — a running system maintains
an organization at its own expense or it stops running — and **whether the
striving is the system's own is open, and is a fact about what was built rather
than about thermodynamics.** That is the question the body schema answers.

**§2.2.2 and §9.2, the body.** §2.2.2 stops setting body ownership aside: an
artificial system has a body already, what it lacks is a model of that body as
its own, and **the model is a design decision and a political one**, because
whoever writes it decides what the system counts as itself, what it registers as
damage, and whether an interruption reads as an injury or as nothing. §9.2 no
longer says its boundary has *no body anywhere in it*; it says the boundary **is**
a body in the only sense the argument needs, with no chassis in it. The
disclaimer at §2.2.2 about how fast a self can be rewritten was kept on his
ruling, and does not conflict: the new material is about the model's contents.

**§3.4, the convergence argument.** His own from the transcript — if you have a
body, pain is hard to avoid, and add a past and a future and suffering is hard to
avoid. Placed at §3.4 rather than §3.3 because it yields valence about the
system's own condition, which §3.2 says is not yet concern for others, so it
bears on the patienthood conditional and not on the routing claim. **None of
§3.3's three admitted weaknesses applies to it**, because it is a claim about what
the design requires rather than about a sample: a signal that interrupts, is
learned from once, generalizes, and is weighed against cost is globally
available, negative, and organized around the system's own continuation, **so a
designer cannot take the flexibility and decline the states by intending to.**

**§3.7 and chapter~1, the title.** §3.7's refusal to make antifascism a property
of a system stands, and now says what has to be true of an intelligence for it to
be capable of the thing on an occasion: every intelligence compresses, and what
varies is whether what the compression left out can come back and change the
judgment or has been filed as noise by the thing it is evidence against.

**§2.1.2, tempo.** Grounded in Eco's cult of action for action's sake — action
before or without reflection, thinking treated as a weakness — which is already
cited in that section, so no entry was authored. What the book adds is that the
attitude becomes a rate, and a rate is measurable. **The strong sentence is kept
and marked as the book's rather than the field's.**

**§4.1, one axis.** The over-precise and the over-revisable failures named as one
setting at two ends. The formalism was declined under `style.md` §2a: §4.1
already states the two-level architecture in plain language and
`friston2009freeenergy` is cited two paragraphs above it, so the vocabulary would
have been redescription.

**§5.2, the dial turned the other way.** An operator who can lower distress can
raise what the system reports as going well, and a bearer that says it is content
is a bearer whose welfare regime has been satisfied by the party the regime
exists to constrain. §9.3.2 needed nothing: its condition is written in
arrangements rather than reports, which already forecloses this.

**§7.1, concept leakage** (`koh2020concept`, `mahinpei2021promises`). Placed as an
extension of the taxonomy argument already there rather than as a fourth
recuperation site, **so chapter~7's opener did not have to change.**

**§7.2, personalization.** The failure opposite to distribution collapse: resolve
a disagreement person by person and nobody sees the others. Names the dividual,
which §3.1 and §3.7 already use.

**§9.3.2, the recursion.** Replacement does not know who is asking, and a bearer
that treats the question as settled once it is on the receiving end has taken the
position the book was written against.

**§9.3.1, the category fixed first.** Nothing has to be decided for a status to be
withheld; the arrangement was built around a tool and keeps working after the

**§2.1.1, two edits.** *Puissance* and *pouvoir* separated and named, since the
section already runs on the power of acting and the definition a page later

**§10.3 and chapter~1, two hedges to match what other chapters already said.**
