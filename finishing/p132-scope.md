## Four defects in my own drafting, found on the second read

None caught by the suite, the build, or the run check. All four are in text written during this pass.

- **Two duplications the run check caught before the second read.** The first draft of edit B
  restated feature 2's definition — *the measure has substituted for the thing measured* — which is
  `09_03_05.tex:12`'s sentence, in the same construction. The first draft of edit C wrote *a
  commitment nobody can enforce is a different kind of object from one somebody can*, which collapses
  `12_01_02.tex:5`'s two sentences into one and reuses both phrases. §12.1.2 and §9.3.5 are the
  surviving instances under D-013; both were rewritten.
- **Edit D contradicted the sentence it was appended to.** It followed P131's *both enumerations are
  written by the deployment about itself* with *One part of the list did not come from the
  deployment*, one clause later. Split into its own paragraph and opened on the distinction that
  makes both true: **the acts are the deployment's and the interests behind them are not.**
- **Edit D made a 305-word paragraph 405 words.** The next longest in §3.6 is 157. The split fixed
  this as well.
- **Edit C never named the Universal Declaration.** The first paragraph said *the most widely adopted
  statement of them is thirty articles old enough to have outlived most of the governments that voted
  for it*, and the second paragraph then said *The Declaration* with nothing having introduced it;
  *voted for it* also had no clean antecedent. Named now, and the rhetorical clause dropped.
  A fourth clause in the same paragraph — *which this book names elsewhere as a recurring error* —
  was a name-dropped argument under `style.md` §7 and was cut; the claim beside it carries itself.
