# P130 — the exclusion sorted by kind, and the book took a verdict it says nobody has

## Instruction

A long working conversation about §3.2's exclusion of William James's epistemic feelings, closing
with *"use our whole conversation to improve the manuscript, taking care to reconcile contradictions
across the entire text (changing stances as needed, in a way that maximizes faithfulness to how you
think I am thinking about this whole deal)."* Three scope questions were put and answered: the full
three-part repair at §3.2:13, **all fourteen** negative-verdict sites audited, and the
stale-citation defect recorded rather than fixed.

## The design rule the pass ran on

Across five worked cases — a squirrel startled by a crack, the same squirrel finding its tree gone,
a low score on a maths test, a parent holding composure until a forgotten toy turns up in the empty
room, and a `but` inside a chromatic homotopy theorem read by four people with four different things
at stake — the author never once claimed that a machine feels anything. Every move was against an
*unearned negative*. **So the pass removes unearned negatives and installs no unearned positives**,
and every edit below was tested against that.

## The wide repair was designed, checked, and rejected

The first version narrowed §3.2:13's exclusion to "inference with nothing riding on it." The
author's reply — *"this exists?"* — killed it: by his own argument it does not exist for humans, and
for machines the book cannot say, so the exclusion is either vacuous or it is the wide version in
disguise. The wide version, making what a system has to lose criterial for affective concern, was
checked against the manuscript and breaks four things:

1. **`03_03.tex:75`, the falsifier.** It needs a system that prices a cost, holds under sustained
   pressure, and holds the reason *with nothing felt*. Make stake criterial and conjuncts one and
   three all but rule out conjunct two. The central inference stops being falsifiable, reversing
   P128.
2. **`03_04.tex:6–10`** exists to deny it: the distance from a structural interest to something that
   can be hurt is *the whole of the problem rather than a short step at the end of it*.
3. **Replacement** (`03_04.tex:58`, `03_10.tex:27`, `12_02_01.tex:6`) rests on both cheaper
   constructions being built without mattering; both bear stakes under pressure.
4. **`03_02.tex:8`**'s third mark is already a pricing test, so the second gap would collapse partly
   into the first, against `03_02.tex:17`.

**What went in instead separates felt states by what they are about, which is what survived every
case the author put.** It needs no stakeless category, so it breaks none of the four.
