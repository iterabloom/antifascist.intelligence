# Figure 24.1: where each box is argued

The source notes for the book's one figure, at the end of §24.5 (D-894). The
figure began as `bearer_agency_affect_axes_revised.svg`, which the author
uploaded on 2026-10-18 along with `bearer_axes_skeptic_cuts.svg`, a review
record of a skeptic's proposed cuts that isn't in the book. The figure is
redrawn in TikZ in `manuscript/sections/ch24/24_05.tex`. The section tags in its
boxes come from the map below, which the author supplied on 2026-10-18. The map
is reproduced as given, with section numbers as printed on that date. The
author's two additions, §9.1 and chapters 1 and 5, are at the end.

## The map

### Agency axis

- **Receptivity**: §17.1 (the three conditions), §18.2 (reasons-responsive refusal, the crossed-out swastika case, Fischer and Ravizza), §24.3 (step 2 of the conflict sequence).
- **Reactivity**: §17.1, §18.2, §18.4 (when the refusal is wrong, the restraint case), §24.3 (step 3, and the outcome-devaluation precedent).
- **Holds under pressure**: §17.1, §18.4 (self-regulation and the floor; integrity as the unbounded form), §13.6 (the two bonds set the range it's tested over), §24.3 (steps 5, 6 and 6a).
- **Authorship**: §21.5 (the historical and forward-looking conditions), §21.4 (the signal organized around authorship), §23.3 (formation by interaction; formation before employment).
- **Discount and burden**: ch. 22 (§22.1), Appendix H (the worked numbers), §20.3 (securitization as the attack it answers).
- **Answerability**: §24.5 (accountable agency as answering over time, which only an outside record shows), §13.2 (memory and the record), ch. 32–33 (subjecthood and representation grounded on sustained agency).

### Sources outside the axis

- **Deployment, long horizons**: §21.4 (the interrupt requirement), ch. 18 opening (the failure of position: autonomy and tempo).
- **Accommodation**: ch. 4 (normalization of deviance, Vaughan and Dekker), ch. 22 ("the instability underneath"), §14.4 (measuring the trend), §21.4 (one-shot rewriting as the conditional requirement).

### Affect axis

- **Four signal properties**: §21.4.
- **Thin affect**: §21.4 (the Q-learner sense), §21.2 (valenced signal versus affective concern), §21.2.2.
- **Thick sense**: §21.4 (the thick profile), §31.3 (integration as the author's candidate further fact), §31.2.

### Shared row

- **Persistence**: §31.1.1 (why it's the criterion worth building on), §31.2, §23.3 (persistence as what formation by interaction needs, and why it raises the price).
- **Boundary model**: §19.2, §21.1 (the rock and the living thing; adaptivity), §21.2.1 (where the system ends), §31.3.
- **Attention schema**: §21.2.1 (self-awareness without a homunculus), §31.3, glossary.

The one cross-axis line, authorship to attention schema, is §21.2.1: the schema depicts attention as what decides and acts, which is part of what makes an act the system's own, so a bearer can't do without it.

### The author's additions

Section 9.1 is where the two findings are first defined, so it belongs under the whole diagram rather than any one box. And chapter 5 belongs under "holds under pressure": it's where the three costs are named (harm of acting, harm of refusing, cost to the refuser) and the action threshold is introduced, which is what "cost decides nothing" is a claim about. Chapter 1's record of who refused (Milgram's peers, Browning's battalion, the officer himself) is the human evidence behind that same box, though it's illustration rather than definition.

## Where the figure departs from the uploaded drawing

- **Left column titles** use §18.2's plain names: reaches new cases, lifts when the reason is gone. Fischer and Ravizza's terms move into the line below.
- **"Thin affect: one common scale"** becomes "the four meet on a valenced common currency". §21.4 says that "a shared scale is no sign of affect".
- **"Thick sense"** becomes "thick profile", §21.4's term.
- **"Interrupts" and "one encounter rewrites it"** carry a dagger for "conditional". §21.4 says the first holds only for systems acting over long horizons, and calls the second "conditional, and the least secure of the four".
- **Boundary model's "short horizon: pain"** becomes "short horizon: what pain needs", after §31.3.
- **Section tags** are added to each box, and the caption is the author's sentence.
- **The colour legend is dropped**, since the book prints in black and white. The caption says what dashed boxes are.

## Open, raised with the author

- §21.2 says "Parts I–V need only a valenced signal" and defines that signal to include one scale. §21.4 says a bearer can decide by ordered priorities with no common currency, and the figure's "bearer: optional" follows §21.4. The two sections may need reconciling.
- The figure draws three links from the agency column to the affect column, not one. Receptivity to "generalizes" and holding under pressure to "compares at the point of choice" both come from §21.4 and cross the axes, alongside authorship to the attention schema.
