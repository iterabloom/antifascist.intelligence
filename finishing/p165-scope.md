# P165 — molar and molecular restated as segmentarity, and resonance

Author instruction: they are not sizes. They are rigid and supple segmentarity,
both running at every scale. Rewrite §2.1.2's "Two scales" and delete the whole
"scale problem" argument — the three paragraphs defending kind-versus-severity
are answering a question the mis-statement created. Replace with resonance.

**The correction is right about the source.** In *A Thousand Plateaus* the pair
is two kinds of segmentarity, not two magnitudes, and the book had been teaching
the common misreading.

## What changed

**"Two scales" is now "Two segmentarities."** Rigid divides in advance, the
category preceding the case; supple is what moves underneath, a threshold
crossed one case at a time. *A state has both. So does an office, so does a
review queue, and so does the person filling out the form.* The paragraph names
the error it corrects.

**Five paragraphs deleted**, including the three defending kind-versus-severity,
the Arendt authority and the Stangneth correction.

**"Resonance" replaces them.** Micro-fascisms exist first and everywhere, each a
small hardening standing on its own; what a fascist state does is not invent
them but induce accord among them. It also answers Griffin, which the deleted
argument had been doing badly: the escalation ladder *is not a small movement. It
is the thing itself, in the place where it is lived.*

**And it answers the everywhere-objection the author named.** §2.1.2 now reads:
that the four features are found nearly everywhere *is not a defect in them. It
is the finding, and an instrument reporting otherwise would be the broken one.*
What the reading is for is not presence but accord.
