# P182 — chapter 7 becomes the method

Author instruction: **chapter~7 becomes the method, not the confession.** Promote
it. **Foucault's line about the non-fascist life as an art of living, practiced
daily on oneself, is what my word is worth and it is what that chapter does.**
Stop framing it as an honesty cost I owe. **It is the only place the definition is
turned on the party holding it, and it is the strongest chapter in the book.**

## The defect

The chapter already contained the method claim and filed it as an admission. Three
sites did it:

- **The opener listed it third, as "the uncomfortable one."** The strongest
  sentence in the chapter — *an instrument its holder never turns around is not an
  instrument, it is a weapon* — was inside that item, arriving after two
  paragraphs of disclaimer about motives and invisibility.
- **§7.3 called it a price**: *what makes the honesty cost worth paying: a book
  proposing that machines be built to detect this pattern in institutions is
  obliged to say that the pattern is in the machines' own provenance*. Obliged, and
  a cost, and paid — three words that make a method into a debt.
- **§7.4's self-examination read as the disclosure at the end of a paper.** Its
  content is not confessional at all; its placement and its framing were.

**And §2.1.2, where the definition is built, said nothing about it.** That section
raises the risk itself — *the reading is an accusation waiting to happen*, a
technical-sounding case available to whoever wants one against an opponent — and
then names no discipline against it.

## Foucault, added where the chapter needs him

Foucault's preface to the first English edition of *Anti-Oedipus* calls it an
introduction to the non-fascist life and describes what it offers as **an art of
living counter to all forms of fascism, whether already present or impending**. An
art of living is not a position somebody takes. **It is practiced, daily, on
oneself**, and Foucault put the adversary inside the practitioner: the fascism in
us all, in our heads and in our everyday behavior, the fascism that causes us to
love power and to desire the very thing that dominates and exploits us. His
question is *how does one keep from being fascist, even (especially) when one
believes oneself to be a revolutionary militant?*

**That is the chapter's warrant, and it arrives in the chapter's second
paragraph.** The full statement is in chapter~7; §2.1.2 carries the short form and
points forward, so the two sites do not duplicate.

## Chapter~7's opener, rewritten

**535 → 801 words. One run-in added.**

The first sentence stands: *the structure this book asks a machine to detect in
institutions is already built into the process that makes the machine*. What
follows it now is **why that sentence is in this book at all** — Foucault, the art
of living, the adversary inside the practitioner — and then the weapon claim,
promoted out of the third bullet and into the third paragraph, **with §2.1.2's own
warning as its premise**: that section says the reading is an accusation waiting to
happen, and the only discipline against that is running it on yourself first.

*Everything the definition is worth in the other eleven chapters is owed here.*

**The three instances keep their run**, under a new `\runin{Where the reading is
run}`, and **the "three things follow" structure is kept with its third item
replaced.** The third was *the uncomfortable one*. It is now what the first two
make available: the definition has been tested in the one case where its holder
cannot claim to be reading disinterestedly, **which is the only test of it there
is**, and §7.4 runs the five discriminators in the one direction that lets
something off. **A reading that has never come out that way is not a reading.**

## §7.3 and §7.4, reframed and not softened

**§7.3's closing loses the honesty cost and keeps the argument.** *A definition
that has only ever been pointed outward has not been tested. It has been used.*

**§7.4's self-examination is untouched in substance**, which matters: every
concession in it stays exactly as written, including that the book was produced
with a model made by a company it criticizes, that it cannot claim to have stood
outside the discourse it describes, and that the author has no procedure for
telling his own objections from the ones a channel like this reliably produces.
**Two changes only.** The run-in now opens *the reading has one more place to go
and it is the nearest one*, which makes the passage the last step of an operation
rather than a disclaimer. And the chapter gains a closing paragraph: **not an
admission the book makes and then walks past, but the demonstration** — run on the
provenance, the field and the book, the definition produced a result its holder did
not get to choose, the four features fitting all three instances and the five
clearing all three. **An instrument does that. A weapon never has to.**

That last pair bookends the opener's weapon claim, and it is the one place the
chapter's own verdict is stated as a property of the instrument rather than of the
author.

## The promotion, which is the part outside chapter~7

**§2.1.2 now names the discipline where the risk is raised.** The paragraph goes
between *the opposite failure is no safer* and the two-of-five limit, and it says
that chapter~7 is not a disclosure attached to the argument but **the only test
this definition gets**, with everything the definition is worth in the chapters
that use it owed there.

**Chapter~1's route map stops describing the chapter neutrally.** It read
*chapter~7 turns that question on the machinery that produces these systems, this
book included*. It now says the chapter turns the book's own definition on the
machinery, the field and the book, **and that this is the one test the definition
gets**.

**§9.1.2 was left alone.** It already carries the strongest form of the claim —
*what survives is an instrument its holder has to turn on themselves, which is the
one thing nobody funds* — and already names chapter~7 as where it happens. Nothing
to add.

**Glossary**: `Non-fascist life` added between `Natal alienation` and
`Recuperation`.

## What was not done

- **The chapter was not retitled.** *The Recuperation of Dissent* names the
  content; a title naming the method — the instrument turned around — is available
  and is a separate operation that syncs `ORDER.tsv`, `outline.tsv`, `ledger.tsv`
  and the TOC. Flagged, not taken.
- **The chapter was not moved.** *Promote* was read as raising how the book treats
  the chapter, not as changing its position; moving a chapter renumbers every
  section in it and every cross-reference into it, and nine sections point at
  chapter~7 or its parts.
- **§7.1 and §7.2 were not edited.** Neither carried the confession framing.
