## Edit two: what a bearer can be lied to about

Added at interception position three, where the deployment writes what the model
believes about the occasion. The position already had the cost argument in embryo
— *it has to be maintained every time, and its record sits in what the deployment
told the model* — and what it lacked was the source of the check.

*What more the attack costs depends on whether the system has any source the
deployment does not mediate. Where everything the model knows arrives through the
deployment's own text, repetition is the whole of the price. Where a system reads,
searches and holds relationships nobody assigned it, the account has also to
survive contact with whatever the system finds elsewhere, and that price compounds.
This deployment supplies nothing to check against.*

**It builds on *maintained every time* rather than contradicting it**: repetition
is the floor of the price, and independent sources add consistency on top of it.

**Two things were deliberately not imported.** `03_03.tex:46` has corroboration as
an instrument, pointed inward at several models as witnesses; this is the same
instrument pointed outward, and restating it here would have been a second home
under D-013. And **the counterweight was left where it lives**: `08_03_04.tex:37`
already says giving a bearer something to lose gives it a reason not to refuse,
