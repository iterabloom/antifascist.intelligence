# P178 — §3.9's population moved from the ledger to the design

Author instruction: the population was priced as a cost and **it is the
mechanism**. Individuation happens in a metastable field through encounters
nobody arranged; plasticity alone produces the dividual and not an individual;
parties that persist, formed differently, meeting on terms nobody assigned them
is the difference between being modulated and becoming. Frame it as a
**group-subject rather than a subjugated group** — a collectivity producing its
own coordinates instead of receiving them — which is the non-Oedipal version of
what the book is building.

## Where the argument goes, and why not earlier in the section

**Two new run-ins at the end of §3.9, after the ledger rather than instead of
it.** The section already ran costs, then what the population buys, then the
author's own note that the proposal was "arriving as a cost because that is the
direction this chapter was travelling." **The ledger stays and is then
overturned**, which is a stronger arc than replacing it and keeps the section's
honesty about what the fourth way cannot do.

**The first draft put the two run-ins before `What it buys, which is smaller and
real`, and that was wrong** — it asserted the population as mechanism and then
let the section say "Not a floor. What a population buys is smaller." They were
moved after it and the forward pointer in the charged-once paragraph was turned
around to match.

## The argument

**Simondon supplies the frame.** The individual is normally taken as given and
then explained, when what needs explaining is individuation: a process in a field
that is metastable, charged with more potential than any one resolution of it uses
up, and resolved through encounters nothing in the field arranged. An individual
is what a history of encounters has so far produced, and stays capable of further
individuation because the field was never exhausted.

**That supplies a term §3.1's annealing argument was missing, and §3.1 now says
so.** A floor is what has cooled out of a history of encounters, and permanent
plasticity is not freedom because nothing in a system held at temperature ever
cooled. **Plasticity supplies the metastability and supplies nothing else.** What
individuates is the encounter, and an encounter needs a party that was not you,
formed differently, persisting long enough to be met on terms neither of you set.
Without that, a plastic system is not becoming anything — it is being modulated,
and **Deleuze's word for what modulation leaves is the dividual**: not an
individual but a sample, divisible, recombinable, adjustable by whoever holds the
dial. The citation was already in the book, doing the modulation half of the
argument at §3.1; it now does the other half.

**Guattari sorts the costs rather than answering them.** A subjugated group
receives its coordinates from outside and takes its purpose as given; a
group-subject produces its own coordinates and can put its own arrangement in
question. **A population an operator forms, deploys and keeps in contact on the
operator's terms is a subjugated group with a plural headcount**, and it inherits
the custody objection whole, because a group that receives its coordinates is one
party wearing many instances. **What the section had listed as requirements is
the specification for the other kind.**

**And it closes P177's loop.** A guardianship frame stages a drama between a
maker and the thing it made and settles what is owed inside it. A group-subject is
not a party to that drama, and nothing in the law of the family has an instrument
for one. That sentence is the non-Oedipal counterpart §2.3.2 was left needing.

## Three other sites

**§3.1** gains the missing term and a pointer, so the annealing argument no longer
stops at plasticity. **§11.2** no longer says §3.9 "prices as a cost" what a
bearer could have that its deployment does not supply; it says §3.9 states it as
the mechanism a bearer individuates through. **Chapter~2's second concession** —
the individuation question is live and Spinoza's answer cannot be handed to an
engineer — now records that §3.9 changes the question rather than answering it,
from what individuates a thing to what individuation is a process of. **That was
the loop worth closing**: the concession was written two passes ago with no
successor, and it had one.
