# P98 — the prior in §3.3 and §3.4 reordered: mechanism routes first, the affective route once they have failed

**The instruction.** The author, shown §3.3's *What survives still settles an engineering
question … the route with a working instance in nature is the one to build first … That is a
prior about where to spend effort rather than a finding about what is possible*: *a form of the
argument holds perfectly fine, but it is distinct from "the observation licenses a prior about
where to spend engineering effort". it is more like "a prior about where to CONSIDER spending
engineering effort AFTER YOU HAVE TRIED AND FAILED THE NON-AFFECT ROUTE".* And on §3.4's close,
*enough to proceed on and not enough to close the question*: *incredibly weaksauce. just
stupendously vague.*

Then: *of course, how is anyone supposed to know the difference between trying and failing and
not trying hard enough and long enough.*

**The paragraph stating the prior was the outlier**, and it was corrected inside its own section:

called vague and in *Suppose the burden is met the ordinary way, by building what the evidence
recommends*.

**Whether the book has an answer to the author's second question.** It has an admission, in
three places, and no criterion: §3.3's *Nothing here fixes the rate of exchange between them.
What the Three Rs fix is the order of the attempts*; §3.10's *That fixes an order and not a
schedule, because the party who pays for a delay is the party the floor exists for*; and
§12.2.1's milestone, a public record of which construction was attempted, at what scale, against
which test, and where it failed, with *nothing was tried* counting as a finding.

## What was done

Four rewrites in two files.

**§3.3, the prior.** Now: the two mechanism constructions are tried first, against the test
stated below, and the affective route is where effort goes once they have failed it. Two limits
stated: it says nothing about what is possible, since neither construction has been tried; and
it does not say what counts as having failed, because nothing distinguishes a route that cannot
work from one that has not been worked hard enough or long enough. What the book asks for is
the attempt recorded in a form others can judge, so the question is argued in public and the
party that wants the bearer does not settle it alone; `section~\ref{sec:12.2.1}` makes that
record the milestone. **The record moves who judges and does not supply the judgment.** No
threshold was invented, and none should be added later.

**§3.3, the Three Rs paragraph.** *on the prior* and *So the order is the other way round* are
gone; the paragraph now says the Replacement reason is why the prior puts the constructions ahead
of the bearer, and keeps the sentence about a deployment that goes straight to the bearer. The
restatement of the order itself, which the prior paragraph now carries, is cut here so it is said
once.

**§3.4, the close.** *What it licenses is a prior about where effort goes once those two
constructions have been tried against the test section 3.3 states and have failed it. Whether
reasons-responsive refusal can be reached without affect is settled only by the attempt, and
nobody has made it.*

**§3.4, the Suppose paragraph.** *Suppose the two constructions have been tried and have failed,
and the burden is met the way the evidence then recommends.*
