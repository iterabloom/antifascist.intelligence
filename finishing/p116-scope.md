# P116 — chapter 7 and the structural definition: four author findings, one of them already closed

**The instruction.** Four issues raised against chapter 7 and the structural definition, with §7.1,
§7.2 and §7.4 passed as sharp. The four: the five molecular discriminators are underused and §11.3
is built on the four; two sentences in §8.3.3 use the definition to dismiss an objection to the
author's own proposal; Paxton is called *worth having in full* and then truncated; and the
definition's best example — *Trump v. Hawaii* — is not in the book.

## Issue 2 needed no work, and the reason matters

**The two sentences do not exist.** *"Reading it as an arithmetic question at all is the
aestheticization of the metric"* and *"This is the aestheticized metric applied to labor
economics"* were both removed at `d1914f1`, P112's compression of the jobs guarantee from 2,586
words to 914 — **cut for length, by an instruction about compression, and not because anybody saw
the self-application problem.** `git log -S` on each phrase returns P37, which wrote them, and
P112, which removed them.

The diagnosis was right and the section is already clean. What stands in §8.3.3 now names the
objection it has to answer as capture rather than cost, answers it with three auditable design
conditions, and declines the economics on the page — *what that proposal is doing in a book about
machine ethics is not the economics, it is the structure*. **A sweep for the same shape elsewhere
found none**: every other use of the four features is diagnostic or self-applied, and the only
place the definition is turned on an objection to the book's own proposals was the two cut
sentences.

## Issue 1 — §11.3 rebuilt on the five

**The finding is exact.** The five appeared in §2.1.2 and §7.4 and nowhere else, and §11.3 —
the section that owes the detector its specification — was built end to end on the four. Worse,
§11.3 posed the discrimination problem, declared it possibly unanswerable, and never mentioned
that §2.1.2 answers it: *I do not think the difference is always there to be found.*

What changed:

- **The discrimination section is rewritten.** It now says the problem is unanswerable only when
  stated on the four, names the five as the detection targets, and makes the four the trigger.
  **That ordering disposes of the accusation objection**: a tool reporting the four reports a
  condition nearly everything meets, and a tool that reports the four and then asks the five has a
  second gate most of what it flags does not pass.
- **A new run-in, *What the five cost to observe*.** Three of the five are within reach of records
  an organization keeps — participation against the rule, who asked for each exception, and the
  category question, which carries the hardest inferential step because a group treated worse is
  common to both diagnoses and what separates them is cost minimized against purpose served. Two
  are not reachable from outside at all. **That split is measured, not guessed.** §7.4 ran
  the five and settled three from the public record, leaving two undetermined, and the two left
  undetermined were procedure waived upward and reward in the promotion.
- **The claim section** said *the specification has been run once*. It now says what was run, and
  adds that a hand read of three cases by the person who wrote the definition is the weakest
  evidence that would count for anything.
- **The near-term work** now codes for the three reachable marks as a second column.

## Issue 3 — Paxton quoted in full

The full definition was verified and the omitted half is what the author said it was: a mass-based
party, collaboration with traditional elites, abandonment of democratic liberties, and redemptive
violence. **Quoted in full rather than dropping *in full*,** because the remainder restates the
list of discriminators in the paragraph it sits in, which is what makes the sentence do work
instead of merely supplying a mood. One clause added saying so. The bibliography note carried the
truncated quotation as though it were the whole and now carries all of it.

## Issue 4 — *Trump v. Hawaii*, and the sentence the book never wrote

Placed in **§2.1.2**, immediately after the sentence stating the four artifact questions, so that
the first test is stated and then run. Everything was verified: the reporter citation, the vote,
the authorship, the majority's two sentences on *Korematsu*, Sotomayor's dissent, and Jackson's
1944 line, each against at least two independent sources.

**The passage is written so it cannot be read as a legal claim.** It gives the majority's own
position that the older case had nothing to do with this one, gives the dissent's position that
the majority redeployed its logic, says which of them is right about the law is not what the
section can settle, and rests only on the structure: an objection made against the institution was
accepted, canonized and quoted back with approval in the same opinion that let the thing objected
to proceed.

**The joining paragraph is the second half of the finding and it is the more important half.** It
says the vocabulary answers how a wrong survived its own condemnation and does not answer whether
the thing was wrong; that the second judgment is made elsewhere in the book by parties for whom an
outcome registers as mattering rather than merely ranking — the book's own words from §2.2 — and
that the mechanism is described in mechanical terms because it has no capacity for that judgment
to appeal to. It ends by naming the misreading directly: that is a reason to specify against the
structure, not a reason to think the moral question has been answered by describing the machinery
that defeats it.

## Two repairs the changes made necessary

- **§2.1.2 said the molecular case *stays genuinely unsolved*** — three paragraphs after supplying
  the five that settle it. With §11.3 now leaning on the five, the overstatement had to go. It now
  says what is unsolved is not which marks would settle the case but how anyone outside would
  observe two of them.
- **The glossary** defined *four structural features of fascism* as "the book's structural
  definition" with no mention of the five. It now says the four report sickness and the five are
  the detection targets.
