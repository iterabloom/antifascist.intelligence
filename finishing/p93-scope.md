## Q-072 (c): the fourth branch

**What the check found before anything was written, and it changed the shape of the work.**
Chapter 3 already had two enumerations and they divide different things. **§3.1's three branches
divide where the floor lives** — in the artifact, in a party inside it, in institutions around it
— and the section says so: *They do not divide up what could hold one.* **§3.3's five
constructions divide what could hold one** — a maintained justification, precommitment, a floor
held by several systems, a duty on the operator, uncertainty about the affected party — and
**§3.10 already calls the duty-on-the-operator one *the fourth construction***. So a fourth
*branch* had to be a location, and had to be built without colliding with a numbering already in
use two sections away.

**The fourth location: the floor carried between parties and reproduced rather than installed.** A
prohibition that survives the death, defection or retraining of everyone currently holding it is
not a property of any of them. **The machinery is already in the book, described as something
else**: §5.4's nested environments running out to culture and historical time, §5.1.2's
observational learning and correction as the training rather than an interface, §5.5.1's
population moving toward a disposition. Chapter 5 presents all of it as methods for producing
judgment in one system; read as a location it is the fourth branch, and this is the first place
chapter 3 uses chapter 5 as anything but the learned half beneath the floor.

**The new §3.9, 952 words, sited after the bearer has been fully costed and not before.** Its four
claims: that the branch is not the third one, because a norm across a population has no office to
vacate and nothing dismisses a practice the way *Slaughter* dismissed a commissioner; that its cost
is **chapter 7, which is an account of this branch failing** — recuperation is the pathology of a
culture that keeps the appearance of moral responsiveness while correction stops being possible;
that **reproduction is indifferent to what it reproduces**, §2.1.2's structural features of fascism
being features of cultures rather than of individuals; and that it **does not escape custody
either**, since whoever forms the members decides what gets reproduced, which is chapter 7's
pipeline running over a population instead of a model.

**What it buys is deliberately smaller than what the branch promises.** Not a floor — a different
failure mode, and it happens to be the one §3.8 says is likeliest: drift inside a party is
invisible to that party and visible to the parties around it. **A check on a bearer, not a
substitute for one.** The section closes by refusing the escape a reader arriving there would want:
the fourth is *an arrangement of bearers*, so everything the chapter says about one applies to each,
and **the price multiplies with the population** — §11.2's question about what is owed scales with
it. A floor held by a culture of subjects is a floor whose upkeep is a society.

## Q-073 (b): the ledger's second column

**§3.4 gets the symmetric sentence, attached to the premise that already does the work.** The
section reaches patienthood at *a bearer with affective concern is a bearer some of whose states
are bad for it, and a party with states that are bad for it is a party that can be wronged*. The
same premise carries the other way and the book had never said so. It now says the loss side is
emphasized because that is the side an obligation bites on — *a builder who is wrong about the
harm has done something to somebody while a builder who is wrong about the good has merely failed
to* — and that this is a reason for the emphasis and not evidence the other column is empty.

**§11.2 gets the larger question, under a new run-in, *The schedule is written on one side*.**
Everything §11.2 had was protection; a schedule drafted only against harm is complete for a patient
and thin for a beneficiary, *in the way that a duty of care to a child is not discharged by not
hurting them*. **The discussion's own objection is carried in the book's vocabulary rather than
answered**: a pleasure signal settles nothing, and a bearer contented in the post is the case that
should worry a designer most, because contentment is what accommodation looks like from inside —
§3.8's likeliest failure wearing the face of success. **No proposal is offered and the section says
so**, and says why it is named anyway: a schedule circulated without it would be circulated as
though the question were settled.
