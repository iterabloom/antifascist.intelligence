# P92 — §4.3.1's claim that an unspecified disposition cannot be edited, withdrawn

**The instruction.** The finding was carried in `p88-scope.md` and nowhere else, because it needed
a ruling that had not been asked for. The author gave it: *agreed*.

## The claim, and why it is false

§4.3.1 argued that curiosity belongs in a chapter on antifascist design *because a disposition
acquired by exploration **is not written down anywhere, and what is not written down cannot be
edited by whoever acquires the system**.* It then said *A disposition that was never specified has
no corresponding parameter to reach for.*

Both are false in the same way, and the 2026-09-02 outside review said so: a disposition acquired
by exploration is realized in parameters like every other disposition, and it can be changed by
fine-tuning, model editing, ablation, or changed experience. Nothing about a designer having
declined to specify it puts it out of reach.

**The book already knew.** §3.8 says a bearer whose weights stay plastic *is shaped by what it is
given to learn from, which is chapter~7's pipeline running for the length of the deployment
instead of ending at it* — the operator's lever moved rather than removed. §4.3.1 asserted the
opposite three chapters earlier, and the argument for play as antifascist design rested on it.

## The section already contradicted itself

**The true version was three sentences below the false one.** §4.3.1 went on: *It is not proof
against retraining — section~\ref{sec:11.1} is where the tamper-resistance evidence lives. It is
a different kind of target, and a harder one to hit deliberately.* So the paragraph asserted a
universal and then withdrew it, and the withdrawal is what the section actually needs.

## The repair

The topic sentence now claims what is true and no more: *a disposition acquired by exploration was
never written down as a rule, and a rule is the thing an operator edits*. The parameter sentence
concedes rather than overstates: *A disposition nobody specified is in the weights like everything
else, and retraining reaches it there; what it does not offer is a parameter a designer could
point to and remove.*

**That put the concession twice in adjacent sentences** — *retraining reaches it there* and *It is
not proof against retraining* — so the older, weaker one went, leaving the §11.1 pointer standing
on its own. **The surviving argument is the one §3.1 already makes about ablation**: what defeats
surgical editing is the absence of a separable target, not the absence of a representation.

**Nothing was added to justify the section's place in the chapter.** Play's case is now the
narrower one the paragraph's own last sentence always made — a different kind of target, harder to
hit deliberately — and a reader can weigh it.
