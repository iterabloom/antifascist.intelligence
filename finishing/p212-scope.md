# P212 — the D-009 gap closed, and one claim corrected by closing it

Three named instruments in §2.2 carried no citation: NKVD Order 00447, the 1938
turn against excesses committed on instruction, and the destruction ordered in
March 1945. D-009 says endnotes are for named studies, statutes, systems and
quotations, and all three qualify. The facts had been verified by search when
item 5 put them in at P210; the page had no source on it.

## Three entries, and what each is cited for

**`werth2010nkvd00447`** — Nicolas Werth's entry in the Sciences Po Mass
Violence and Resistance encyclopedia, 24 May 2010. It carries the mechanism the
text reports: round-number quotas per region and republic in two categories,
269,100 persons of whom 76,000 were to be shot, against a four-month deadline;
the operation instead ran fifteen months and condemned some 767,000, of whom
about 387,000 were shot.

**`getty2002excesses`** — J. Arch Getty, *The Russian Review* 61:1, 113–138.
The 1938 turn against excesses is the subject its title names.

**`ghdi1945nerodecree`** — the 19 March 1945 order and Speer's 29 March
memorandum together, in German History in Documents and Images, with both
Bundesarchiv shelfmarks.

## The sourcing corrected a claim, which is the point of doing it

**The text had the Nero Decree's reasoning in the wrong document.** It said
*what was ordered in March 1945 was destruction on the reasoning that the
nation had shown itself the weaker and had forfeited what it would have needed
to survive.* The order says no such thing. It opens on military necessity — the
nation's struggle for existence requiring every means, within Reich territory
included, to weaken the enemy's fighting power — which is the description the
book uses for the **other** category, an operation with a war still inside it.
The reasoning is in Speer's memorandum ten days later, quoting back the
philosophy he had been given: *the nation has showed itself to be the weaker one
and the future belongs exclusively to the stronger eastern nation.*

**So the book's own test fails on the document and succeeds on the speech, and
the passage now says so.** It had read *the distinction is in the stated reason
and in the direction of travel*; the stated reason sorts this instance the wrong
way. It now reads as a caution about the instrument, which is the same finding
§2.2 already reaches about the five discriminators — all five are facts about
what an institution rewarded rather than about what it wrote.

**One characterization was tightened to what a source will carry.** *Gave
three-man boards minutes a case, with no defense and no appeal* was an
order-of-magnitude estimate. Werth's sentences are that the troikas sat behind
closed doors with the accused absent and undefended, and went through several
hundred cases in sittings of about half a day. The text now says that, and the
no-appeal half is dropped rather than credited to a source that does not state
it.

## What this does not reach

**Two further flags from P210 are still open.** §12.2.1's third milestone is
drafted prose where item 4 specified content only. And the citation added here
is an encyclopedia entry and a document collection, not a monograph; both are
scholarly and both are secondary to the archival sources they name, which is
the ordinary standing of a reference work.
