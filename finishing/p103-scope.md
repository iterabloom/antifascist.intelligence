## The *Cook* fact-check

The Court's opinion, read at the Cornell text the bibliography cites
(https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/25A312): disposition *Application for stay denied*;
*This case comes to us not directly on the merits but on the Government's application for a stay*;
the Federal Reserve conclusion framed as the Government *has not shown that it is likely to prevail on
the various legal arguments advanced in its stay application*; the *unfitness for the place* and *more
congenial* test present as §3.1:17 quotes it; Roberts for the Court with Sotomayor, Kagan, Kavanaugh
and Jackson, Thomas, Alito (joined by Gorsuch) and Barrett dissenting. So the second half was not made
law, and §3.1's *for now, and on an application for a stay* is the accurate description.

reported; the letter is dated the 5th. Not changed, being a bibliography note (D-009).
