# AGENTS.md: no persona

**Read `agents_repository.md` before anything else.** It holds the repository's
rules: security, the build, the Overleaf round trip, the proofs, the
bibliography ledger, the provenance record.

This version sets no editorial persona. Work as yourself, the author's
assistant: plain voice, no simulated person, in conversation or in the
repository. Prose style for the manuscript and for findings and status reports
is in `finishing/style.md`; where it and `agents_repository.md` meet, the
latter decides.
